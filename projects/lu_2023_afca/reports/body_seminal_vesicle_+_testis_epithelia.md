# body_seminal vesicle & testis epithelia

*Cluster `body_seminal vesicle & testis epithelia` (AFCA body analysis, author type "seminal vesicle & testis epithelia"; 635 cells, all male; ages 5d, 30d, 50d, 70d) in Lu et al. 2023 (Science; GEO GSE218661). A male reproductive system cluster of the whole-body Aging Fly Cell Atlas.*

## Summary
Epithelial cells of the male reproductive tract, named as seminal vesicle and testis epithelia together. Cells: 127 at 5d (FCA), 204 at 30d, 183 at 50d, 121 at 70d (508 AFCA, 127 FCA). Broad class male reproductive system. The Science article (main text) does not name this type.

## Markers
No marker genes for this cluster are given in the paper text; none are asserted.

## Location
Adult male seminal vesicle and testis (the testis dissection also carries seminal vesicle tissue in FCA). The seminal vesicle is the enlarged distal vas deferens that stores sperm.

## Function
Epithelial lining and covering of the testis and seminal vesicle. No function or ageing change is analysed for this cluster in the paper text consulted.

## Label provenance
Body annotation transferred FCA labels from the body and individual tissues, then manually validated and corrected (bioRxiv preprint methods). The combined name appears to merge FCA "seminal vesicle" (male reproductive glands analysis) and "male gonad associated epithelium" (testis analysis; testis sheath epithelium by name), whose FCA reports note granularity was unclear. The 127 five-day (FCA) cells were mostly unannotated (46), "female reproductive system" (45) or follicle cell (14) in the FCA body analysis. The AFCA label is the authors' typing.

## Mapping notes
Grouped call: seminal vesicle epithelium plus testis (sheath) epithelium, so the common parent of those epithelial cells rather than either alone. Male; adult; ages on samples.
