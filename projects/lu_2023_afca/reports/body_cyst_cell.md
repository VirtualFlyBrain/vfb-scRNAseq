# body_cyst cell

*Cluster `body_cyst cell` (AFCA body analysis, author type "cyst cell"; 201 cells, all male; ages 5d, 30d, 50d, 70d) in Lu et al. 2023 (Science; GEO GSE218661). A male reproductive system cluster of the whole-body Aging Fly Cell Atlas.*

## Summary
Testis somatic cyst cells, as a single grouped type. Cells: 69 at 5d (FCA), 60 at 30d, 26 at 50d, 46 at 70d (132 AFCA, 69 FCA). The authors' broad class is male reproductive system. The Science article (main text) does not name this type.

## Markers
No marker genes for this cluster are given in the paper text. The preprint methods state that annotations were manually validated with cell type-specific markers, but none are listed for this type in the evidence available here; none are asserted.

## Location
Adult male testis. Cyst cells are the somatic cells that enclose germline cysts during spermatogenesis (established Drosophila anatomy; derived from cyst stem cells at the testis apical tip).

## Function
Support of germline cyst development. No function or ageing change is analysed for this cluster in the paper text consulted.

## Label provenance
Body annotation transferred FCA labels "from the body and individual tissues (e.g., gut, wing, leg, testis, ovary, fat body etc)", then manually validated and "manually added and corrected" (bioRxiv preprint methods). The label therefore groups the FCA testis cyst-cell types (cyst stem cell, early cyst cell 1/2, cyst cell intermediate, cyst cell branch a/b, late and spermatocyte cyst cell branch a/b, head cyst cell). The 69 five-day (FCA) cells were mostly unannotated or broad in the FCA body analysis (unannotated 29, follicle cell 20, germline cell 9). The AFCA label is the authors' typing.

## Mapping notes
Grouped call: the testis somatic cyst cells as a whole (all cyst-cell stages), not a single stage or branch. Male; broad class male reproductive system. Adult; ages 5-70d are on samples.
