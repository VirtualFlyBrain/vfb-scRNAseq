# female reproductive system

*Author type `female reproductive system` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x body 709 cells, 10x fat body 374 cells; Smart-seq2 fat body 21 cells; author term: FBbt:00004864 female reproductive system). A group of female reproductive-tract cells appearing in non-ovary analyses.*

## Summary
A coarse, organ-level group in the body and fat body analyses. It is not from the ovary dissection, so it is a contaminating or ectopic population of female reproductive-system nuclei (ovary or reproductive-tract cells carried into body and fat body preparations), as named by the authors.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Body (709 cells; 447 female, 232 male, 30 mixed-sex-origin) and fat body (374 cells; 244 female, 130 male) analyses. The male-labelled cells in these analyses indicate that the group is a transcriptional cluster not strictly restricted to female origin.

## Function
No cell-specific function is stated; the label identifies tissue origin.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt female reproductive system (an anatomical system term); broad class: the same term. The label is used both as type and class. Smart-seq2 fat body cells were classifier-assigned.

## Mapping notes
Organ-level call, a remainder/contaminant group rather than a cell type; no cell-level identity can be derived. Adult data.
