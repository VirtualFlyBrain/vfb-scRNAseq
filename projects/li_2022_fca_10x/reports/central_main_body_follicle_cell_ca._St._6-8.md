# central main body follicle cell ca. St. 6-8

*Author type `central main body follicle cell ca. St. 6-8` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:ovary 2324 cells, ss2:ovary 40 cells; author term: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors)). Adult ovary follicle-cell population.*

## Summary
Main body (oocyte-overlying) follicle cells of roughly stage 6-8 egg chambers, i.e. after the mitotic-to-endocycle switch but before the columnar epithelium of stage 10 forms. Distinguished from the earlier 'until St. 5' and later choriogenic groups.

## Markers
The paper and supplementary text give no marker list for this type; none is invented here. Annotation of ovary types follows the Nystul/Rust lab ovary atlases cited in the supplement (Jevitt et al. 2020; Rust et al. 2020), with the ovary integrated against published scRNA-seq datasets (supplementary fig. S37).

## Location
Follicular epithelium covering the oocyte/nurse-cell complex in mid-stage egg chambers of the vitellarium. Source: dissected ovaries of 5-day-old w1118 adult females (Nystul lab sample), single-nucleus RNA-seq.

## Function
Pattern the egg chamber and oocyte, and begin yolk uptake and vitelline membrane production in the mid-oogenesis follicular epithelium (general knowledge, not from the paper).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors); broad class: female reproductive system. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation, not annotated independently.

## Mapping notes
A stage-bin of the main body follicle cell lineage (an uncertain 'ca.' stage range), more specific than main body follicle cell; the label is a combination of cell lineage and approximate egg-chamber stage. The data are from adult flies (the cells are adult ovarian follicle cells at the stated egg-chamber stage, not a developmental time series of the animal).
