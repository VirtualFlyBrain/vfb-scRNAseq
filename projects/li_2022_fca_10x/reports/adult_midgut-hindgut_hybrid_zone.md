# adult midgut-hindgut hybrid zone

*Author type `adult midgut-hindgut hybrid zone` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 276, Smart-seq2 gut 18; author term: FBbt:00047207 adult midgut-hindgut hybrid zone). Region-named group from the gut analysis.*

## Summary
Epithelial cells of the boundary between midgut and hindgut, the hybrid zone.

## Markers
No cluster markers in the sources. The FBbt definition (citing Sawyer et al. 2017) says the zone's small cells express markers of both midgut and hindgut together with wingless.

## Location
Gut analysis: the FCA gut sample included the midgut/hindgut junction (where the Malpighian tubules join) and the crop, which the earlier Hung et al. gut dataset had removed (supplement, fig. S36 legend). Expected at the posterior midgut/anterior hindgut boundary.

## Function
Per the cited definition, the zone lacks stem cells but regulates proliferation of nearby posterior midgut stem cells and blocks cells crossing between midgut and hindgut.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00047207 adult midgut-hindgut hybrid zone; authors' broad class: epithelial cell. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Region-named group consisting of the cells of one gut region (a ring of small epithelial cells); effectively a single, region-defined cell population. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
