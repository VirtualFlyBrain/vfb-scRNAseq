# follicle cell St. 9+

*Author type `follicle cell St. 9+` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:body 39 cells; author term: FBbt:00004904 follicle cell). Small group of late-stage follicle cells in the whole-body sample.*

## Summary
Follicle cells of egg chambers from about stage 9 onward, found in the whole-body (not ovary) 10x analysis. Only 39 nuclei, so a minor population there.

## Markers
No marker genes are given in the paper or supplementary text for this type; none is invented here.

## Location
Egg chambers in the ovaries of adult females carried in the whole-body sample (5-day adults). Because the ovary-specific analysis resolves follicle cells into finer stage types, this group is the body analysis's coarse late-stage counterpart.

## Function
Late-stage follicular epithelium: eggshell (vitelline membrane and chorion) deposition and oocyte patterning (general knowledge, not from the paper).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00004904 follicle cell (the generic follicle cell term); broad class: female reproductive system. Another body-analysis group is labelled simply 'follicle cell' (7813 cells, broad class epithelial cell).

## Mapping notes
Generic follicle cell is the authors' call; the label adds an approximate stage restriction (stage 9 onward), so this is a stage-bin of the follicle lineage, not a distinct type. Adult data.
