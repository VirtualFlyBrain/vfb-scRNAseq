# visceral muscle of the midgut

*Author type `visceral muscle of the midgut` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 261, Smart-seq2 gut 15; author term: Manual Annotation). Region-qualified muscle type from the gut analysis.*

## Summary
Visceral muscle cells of the midgut, a muscle population the authors highlight as newly characterised in the gut.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Gut analysis: the FCA gut sample included the midgut/hindgut junction (where the Malpighian tubules join) and the crop, which the earlier Hung et al. gut dataset had removed (supplement, fig. S36 legend).

## Function
Visceral muscle surrounds the gut tube; the authors note the integration with earlier published gut data identified visceral muscle cells, absent from those data.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (the authors had no FBbt term); authors' broad class: muscle cell. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Single region-specific muscle type (visceral muscle of the midgut); a sibling type covers the other region. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
