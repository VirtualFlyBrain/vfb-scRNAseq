# post-mitotic endocycling nurse cell

*Author type `post-mitotic endocycling nurse cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x ovary 396 cells; Smart-seq2 ovary 8 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Nurse cells that have exited mitosis and are endoreplicating.*

## Summary
Germline-derived nurse cells of the adult ovary after the mitotic cyst divisions, in the endocycling (polyploidising) phase in which they synthesise material for the oocyte.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Adult ovary, egg chambers. Found only in the ovary analysis.

## Function
Nurse cells are polyploid, support the oocyte by producing RNA, proteins and organelles that are transferred via ring canals.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term). The authors' broad class is recorded as "female reproductive system" (not "female germline cell" as for the other germline types), which is a broad organ-level class and not a cell class; this may be an inconsistency in the authors' assignment and is stated here without correction. Smart-seq2 cells were classifier-assigned from the 10x labels.

## Mapping notes
A single nurse-cell population defined by a developmental state (post-mitotic, endocycling), so it is a stage-qualified cell type. Its broad class is organ-level, so curation should not rely on that class. Adult data.
