# choriogenic main body follicle cell St. 12

*Author type `choriogenic main body follicle cell St. 12` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:ovary 476 cells, ss2:ovary 11 cells; author term: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors)). Adult ovary follicle-cell population.*

## Summary
Main body follicle cells of stage 12 egg chambers that are actively producing chorion (eggshell) components.

## Markers
The paper and supplementary text give no marker list for this type; none is invented here. Annotation of ovary types follows the Nystul/Rust lab ovary atlases cited in the supplement (Jevitt et al. 2020; Rust et al. 2020), with the ovary integrated against published scRNA-seq datasets (supplementary fig. S37).

## Location
Columnar follicular epithelium over the oocyte in late-stage egg chambers. Source: dissected ovaries of 5-day-old w1118 adult females (Nystul lab sample), single-nucleus RNA-seq.

## Function
Secrete chorion (eggshell) proteins in late oogenesis (general knowledge).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors); broad class: female reproductive system. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation, not annotated independently.

## Mapping notes
A stage-bin of the main body follicle cell lineage restricted to the choriogenic phase; single stage-specific population, no direct term in the authors' ontology. The data are from adult flies (the cells are adult ovarian follicle cells at the stated egg-chamber stage, not a developmental time series of the animal).
