# germ cell stage 4 and later

*Author type `germ cell stage 4 and later` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x ovary 420 cells; Smart-seq2 ovary 14 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Female germline cells of egg chambers from stage 4 onward.*

## Summary
Germline cells (nurse cells and oocyte of developing egg chambers) from egg-chamber stage 4 onwards, grouped by the authors as one late-oogenesis bin.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Adult ovary, egg chambers in the vitellarium (posterior to the germarium). Found only in the ovary analysis.

## Function
Late egg chambers grow by nurse-cell transport into the oocyte and undergo vitellogenesis and eggshell/yolk deposition at later stages. The authors do not separate nurse cells from the oocyte in this type.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class: female germline cell. The ovary annotation (fig. S18) was done with ovary experts (Nystul/Rust) and integrated with published ovary datasets (fig. S37). Smart-seq2 cells were classifier-assigned from the 10x labels.

## Mapping notes
A grouped, stage-bounded call ("stage 4 and later") that spans several egg-chamber stages and probably more than one cell type (nurse cells and oocyte), so it does not correspond to a single cell type. Closest broad level is the female germline cell. A separate author type, post-mitotic endocycling nurse cell, overlaps this stage range conceptually. Adult data.
