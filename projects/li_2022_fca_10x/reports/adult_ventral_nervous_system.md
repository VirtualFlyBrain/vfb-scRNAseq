# adult ventral nervous system

*Author type `adult ventral nervous system` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x body 9768 and body_wall 543 cells, ss2 body_wall 11 cells; author term: FBbt adult ventral nerve cord). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. The large neuron class of the ventral nerve cord in the body.*

## Summary
The largest neuron group in the body dataset: 10,322 nuclei labelled by region, i.e. neurons of the ventral nerve cord (thoracic and abdominal neuromeres). It is a coarse annotation: the body data are not resolved into VNC neuron types, and the paper describes body annotation as including 'ventral nerve cord and peripheral neurons'.

## Markers
No specific markers; neuron identity from pan-neuronal expression (the authors' broad class neuron).

## Location
Body (9768; 5100 female, 4197 male, 471 mixed) and body wall (543); ss2 body wall (11), classifier-typed. Cells come from the VNC, within the thorax.

## Function
Neurons of the ventral nerve cord, which control leg and wing movement; unresolved here.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: adult ventral nerve cord (a region); broad class: neuron. Body annotation of Fly Cell Atlas.

## Mapping notes
Structure-named group: all VNC neurons pooled (a region-based bulk category, not a type). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
