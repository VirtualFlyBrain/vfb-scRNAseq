# mechanosensory neuron of haltere

*Author type `mechanosensory neuron of haltere` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x haltere 716, Smart-seq2 haltere 14 (label transfer); author term: Manual Annotation. Haltere sensory neurons.*

## Summary
The larger of two neuronal populations in the haltere dataset (Liqun Luo lab), 730 cells, broad class sensory neuron.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Adult haltere, the dipteran balancing organ, whose campaniform sensilla and chordotonal organs detect strain and motion (established background).

## Function
Mechanosensation for flight stability (established background).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term), broad class sensory neuron. Sibling label: neuron of haltere (broad class neuron).

## Mapping notes
The call implies a grouped mechanosensory neuron class of the haltere, not resolved into campaniform versus chordotonal types. Adult data.
