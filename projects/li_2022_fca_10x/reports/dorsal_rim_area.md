# dorsal rim area

*Author type `dorsal rim area` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 2 cells; author term: FBbt dorsal rim area). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Two photoreceptor nuclei assigned to the dorsal rim.*

## Summary
Two head nuclei, broad class sensory neuron, labelled dorsal rim area. In the paper, subclustering of the integrated head/brain data separated inner and outer photoreceptors from dorsal rim area and ocellar photoreceptors, so these cells would be photoreceptors of the dorsal rim ommatidia of the retina.

## Markers
No marker genes are listed for this group in the paper.

## Location
Head, dorsal margin of the compound eye (single row of ommatidia); 2 cells (1 female, 1 male).

## Function
Dorsal rim photoreceptors are the specialised polarised-light-detecting photoreceptors of the eye (general knowledge; not stated in the paper).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: dorsal rim area (a retinal region, not a cell); broad class: sensory neuron. Origin: photoreceptor subclustering (Fig. 3F).

## Mapping notes
Structure-named group: the label names a retinal region, so the cells are its photoreceptor neurons; tiny cluster (2 cells). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
