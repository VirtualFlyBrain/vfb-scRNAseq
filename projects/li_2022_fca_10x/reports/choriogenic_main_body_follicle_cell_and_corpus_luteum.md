# choriogenic main body follicle cell and corpus luteum

*Author type `choriogenic main body follicle cell and corpus luteum` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:ovary 1444 cells, ss2:ovary 22 cells; author term: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors)). Adult ovary follicle-cell population.*

## Summary
A combined call covering choriogenic main body follicle cells (late stage) together with corpus luteum cells, the follicle-cell remnants left in the ovary after ovulation.

## Markers
The paper and supplementary text give no marker list for this type; none is invented here. Annotation of ovary types follows the Nystul/Rust lab ovary atlases cited in the supplement (Jevitt et al. 2020; Rust et al. 2020), with the ovary integrated against published scRNA-seq datasets (supplementary fig. S37).

## Location
Late-stage egg-chamber follicular epithelium and post-ovulatory follicle remnants in the ovary. Source: dissected ovaries of 5-day-old w1118 adult females (Nystul lab sample), single-nucleus RNA-seq.

## Function
Eggshell production by the choriogenic cells; the corpus luteum is the degenerating follicular sheath after the egg is released (general knowledge).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors); broad class: female reproductive system. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation, not annotated independently.

## Mapping notes
A grouped call of two related populations (choriogenic follicle cells plus corpus luteum), not a single type; the authors did not split the two. It overlaps the stage-specific St. 12 and St. 14 choriogenic types, so it is a separate author category covering the remaining late-stage cells. The data are from adult flies (the cells are adult ovarian follicle cells at the stated egg-chamber stage, not a developmental time series of the animal).
