# adult pylorus

*Author type `adult pylorus` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 181, Smart-seq2 gut 10; author term: FBbt:00100784 adult pylorus). Region-named group from the gut analysis.*

## Summary
Epithelial cells of the pylorus, the anterior hindgut constriction from which the Malpighian tubules arise.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Gut analysis: the FCA gut sample included the midgut/hindgut junction (where the Malpighian tubules join) and the crop, which the earlier Hung et al. gut dataset had removed (supplement, fig. S36 legend). The pylorus is the anterior part of the hindgut.

## Function
Hindgut epithelium lined by cuticle (intima) rather than peritrophic matrix; the sources give no functional detail for this cluster.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00100784 adult pylorus; authors' broad class: epithelial cell. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Region-named group: the epithelial cells of an anterior hindgut region, not a defined cell type. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
