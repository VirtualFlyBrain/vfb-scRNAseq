# adult olfactory receptor neuron acid-sensing, Ir75a/b/c, Ir64a

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron acid-sensing, Ir75a/b/c, Ir64a` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 411, Smart-seq2 antenna 2; author term: Manual Annotation). Acid-sensing IR ORN cluster with Ir75a, Ir75b, Ir75c and Ir64a.*

## Summary
Acid-sensing ionotropic-receptor ORNs expressing Ir75a, Ir75b, Ir75c and Ir64a. Ir75a/b/c neurons are known coeloconic-sensillum ORNs responsive to acids.

## Markers
Ir75a, Ir75b, Ir75c, Ir64a (from the label). Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna (coeloconic sensilla; Ir64a also sacculus). Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Acid detection via IRs.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call of several named acid-sensing IR ORN classes; adult.
