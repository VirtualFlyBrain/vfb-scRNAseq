# photoreceptor

*Author type `photoreceptor` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x proboscis_and_maxillary_palps 489 cells; ss2 proboscis_and_maxillary_palps 5; author term: FBbt photoreceptor). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Light-sensing cells annotated in the proboscis and maxillary palp dataset.*

## Summary
494 sensory neuron nuclei labelled photoreceptor in the proboscis/maxillary palp tissue (a dataset that is mostly male: 413 male, 76 female). The label is a general photoreceptor term; the authors do not say which photoreceptor subtype or from which structure these cells derive. The paper treats photoreceptors in the head as eye photoreceptors with R7/R8 subtypes, but a retina is not part of the proboscis/palp dissection, so possible carry-over (e.g. adjacent eye or ocellar tissue) is not explained.

## Markers
No markers listed in the paper for this group.

## Location
Proboscis and maxillary palps (489 cells; 5 ss2). Source structure unstated.

## Function
Light detection; unspecified.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: photoreceptor (the light-sensitive organ term, not a cell type); broad class: sensory neuron. Annotated in the proboscis/maxillary palp analysis.

## Mapping notes
Generic group named for a structure; the call implies photoreceptor cells without subtype. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
