# follicle stem cell and prefollicle cell

*Author type `follicle stem cell and prefollicle cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:ovary 339 cells, ss2:ovary 10 cells; author term: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors)). Adult ovary follicle-cell population.*

## Summary
Germarial somatic precursors: follicle stem cells and their immediate progeny, the prefollicle cells, which give rise to follicle cells.

## Markers
The paper and supplementary text give no marker list for this type; none is invented here. Annotation of ovary types follows the Nystul/Rust lab ovary atlases cited in the supplement (Jevitt et al. 2020; Rust et al. 2020), with the ovary integrated against published scRNA-seq datasets (supplementary fig. S37).

## Location
Germarium (anterior tip of each ovariole), follicle stem cells at the region 2a/2b border and prefollicle cells in region 2b/3. Source: dissected ovaries of 5-day-old w1118 adult females (Nystul lab sample), single-nucleus RNA-seq.

## Function
Self-renew and generate follicle, stalk and polar cell lineages (general knowledge; see Rust et al. 2020, Jevitt et al. 2020 cited by the supplement).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term used by the authors); broad class: female reproductive system. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation, not annotated independently.

## Mapping notes
A grouped call of two adjacent lineage states (stem cell and its progeny); broad class given as somatic precursor cell. Not split by the authors. The data are from adult flies (the cells are adult ovarian follicle cells at the stated egg-chamber stage, not a developmental time series of the animal).
