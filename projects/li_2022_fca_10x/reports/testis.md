# testis

*Author type `testis` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x fat body 69, body wall 31, Smart-seq2 fat body 2; author term: FBbt:00004928 testis). Cells of testis origin found in the fat body and body wall analyses, labelled with the organ name.*

## Summary
The label names an organ, not a cell type. It collects nuclei in the fat body (69 male cells) and body wall (31 cells, 19 male and 12 female) analyses that the authors assigned to the testis, probably carry-over tissue in the dissections (inference). It is distinct from the testis tissue analysis, where the germline and somatic cells are typed individually.

## Markers
None given in the paper or supplement text.

## Location
Fat body and body wall (carcass) analyses of adult flies. Note the body wall analysis contains cells from female flies under this label.

## Function
Not applicable; an organ-level label.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00004928 (testis); broad class male reproductive system.

## Mapping notes
An organ-level, whole-tissue call with unspecified constituent cell types, not a single cell type. The mix of male and female cells in the body wall cluster is notable. Data are adult.
