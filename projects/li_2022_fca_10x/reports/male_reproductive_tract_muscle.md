# male reproductive tract muscle

*Author type `male reproductive tract muscle` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x male reproductive glands 210 cells; Smart-seq2 male reproductive glands 8 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Muscle cluster of the male reproductive tract.*

## Summary
Muscle cells of the male reproductive tract; broad class muscle cell. The paper highlights multinucleated muscle cells found in the atlas (Fig. 2B), though it does not state that this cluster is the one illustrated.

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the supplementary text available here (figure S17 legend shows only the tSNE annotation); none are asserted.

## Location
Male reproductive glands analysis (accessory glands, ejaculatory ducts and bulbs, which are surrounded by visceral-type muscle); adult males.

## Function
Contractile sheath of the reproductive tract, presumably moving glandular secretions and sperm; not analysed in the paper.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id was Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class muscle cell.

## Mapping notes
A single organ-system-level muscle type, not split by organ region. No author FBbt term. Adult.
