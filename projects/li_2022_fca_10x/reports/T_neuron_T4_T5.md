# T neuron T4/T5

*Author type `T neuron T4/T5` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 376 cells; author term: FBbt T4 neuron and T5 neuron). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. The T4/T5 cells not assigned to either subcluster.*

## Summary
The residual T4/T5 population: 376 head nuclei assigned as T4/T5 neurons but not to the a-b or c-d subclusters. T4/T5 motion-detecting neurons of the optic lobe (medulla/lobula to lobula plate).

## Markers
acj6 and SoxN (used by the authors to identify T4/T5 as a group; acj6 and SoxN identified T4/T5 neurons, which were then split by subclustering into T4/T5a-b and T4/T5c-d subtypes.). No subtype markers are given.

## Location
Cell bodies lie near the lobula plate; T4 dendrites arborise in medulla layers M9-M10 and T5 in lobula layer 1, with axons terminating in lobula plate layers 1-4 (a-d, by direction preference). Head only (376 cells; 178 female, 195 male, 3 mixed).

## Function
Local motion detectors: T4 (ON) and T5 (OFF) neurons, cholinergic, with a-d subtypes tuned to four cardinal directions (front-to-back, back-to-front, up, down) that map to lobula plate layers 1-4.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology ids: T4 neuron and T5 neuron (two terms); broad class: neuron. Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration). The sibling labels T4/T5a-b and T4/T5c-d are subclusters of the same T4/T5 group; the plain `T neuron T4/T5` label is the remainder after that split.

## Mapping notes
Grouped call covering both T4 and T5 (common parent: T neuron), without subtype resolution; effectively the remainder of the T4/T5 group after the a-b and c-d subclusters. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
