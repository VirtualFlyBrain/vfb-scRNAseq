# adult olfactory receptor neuron Or67a and likely other unknown ORN types

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron Or67a and likely other unknown ORN types` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 1322, Smart-seq2 antenna 14; author term: Manual Annotation). Largest antennal ORN cluster; one named class plus unresolved others.*

## Summary
A large cluster of odorant-receptor ORNs with Or67a detected and, per the authors, likely other unidentified ORN types. Or67a is a known antennal ORN class (ab10).

## Markers
Or67a (from the label); remaining types unknown. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Olfactory.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: remainder: one named ORN class plus unresolved other ORN types, so effectively a general ORN call; adult.
