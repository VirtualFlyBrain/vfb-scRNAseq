# insulin-producing cell

*Author type `insulin-producing cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); Smart-seq2 dilp2IPC 658 cells (not in 10x); no author ontology term. Sorted dilp2-expressing neurons of the adult brain.*

## Summary
The whole Smart-seq2 dilp2IPC analysis: GFP+ nuclei FACS-sorted from heads of dilp2-GAL4 > UAS-unc84GFP flies (Seung Kim lab), typed from the sample description "insulin-producing cell (IPC)". Supplementary table lists 345 / 313 cells (per run).

## Markers
dilp2-GAL4 driver defines the sort; IPCs are known to express Ilp2, Ilp3 and Ilp5 (established background). No cluster-specific markers in the supplement.

## Location
Adult brain, pars intercerebralis neurosecretory cells projecting to the corpora cardiaca, aorta and hypocerebral ganglion (established background).

## Function
Secretion of insulin-like peptides regulating growth and metabolism.

## Label provenance
Not from h5ad clustering; the label comes from the sample description. No author ontology id or broad class. 5-day adults.

## Mapping notes
Authors' call is a sorted-population label, implying a single IPC type; any non-IPC contaminants in the sort are not separated. Adult data.
