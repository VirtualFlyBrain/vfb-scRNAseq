# adult antenna glial cell

*Author type `adult antenna glial cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 2303 nuclei, Smart-seq2 antenna 37 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Glia of the adult antenna.*

## Summary
Glial cells of the adult antenna, annotated by the Fly Cell Atlas antenna experts (Luo lab jamboree).

## Markers
The supplementary text gives no specific marker genes for this type; per-type markers are in the authors' Table S2, which was not available here. Unannotated antennal clusters were described as largely shaven+ non-neuronal, non-glial supporting cells, so this type is distinct from those.

## Location
Antenna (all three antennal segments dissected together); the glia ensheath the antennal sensory axons and sensilla-associated cells. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Not discussed in the paper beyond annotation; presumed support of antennal sensory neurons.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is glial cell. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: single glial type for the antenna, with no subtype resolution; adult.
