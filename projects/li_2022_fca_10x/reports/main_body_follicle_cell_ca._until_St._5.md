# main body follicle cell ca. until St. 5

*Author type `main body follicle cell ca. until St. 5` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:ovary 2799 cells, ss2:ovary 157 cells; author term: FBbt:00005402 main body follicle cell). Adult ovary follicle-cell population.*

## Summary
Early main body follicle cells, from the germarium through about stage 5 egg chambers, while follicle cells still divide mitotically.

## Markers
The paper and supplementary text give no marker list for this type; none is invented here. Annotation of ovary types follows the Nystul/Rust lab ovary atlases cited in the supplement (Jevitt et al. 2020; Rust et al. 2020), with the ovary integrated against published scRNA-seq datasets (supplementary fig. S37).

## Location
Follicular epithelium covering the germline cyst in germarial and early-stage egg chambers. Source: dissected ovaries of 5-day-old w1118 adult females (Nystul lab sample), single-nucleus RNA-seq.

## Function
Envelope the germline cyst, and contribute to polarity and egg-chamber growth in early oogenesis (general knowledge).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00005402 main body follicle cell; broad class: female reproductive system. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation, not annotated independently.

## Mapping notes
Maps to the generic main body follicle cell concept but restricted to an early stage range ('ca.', approximate). A stage-bin of the lineage, the authors' term is the parent type rather than a stage-specific one. The data are from adult flies (the cells are adult ovarian follicle cells at the stated egg-chamber stage, not a developmental time series of the animal).
