# post-mitotic germ cell early 16-cell cyst

*Author type `post-mitotic germ cell early 16-cell cyst` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x ovary 330 cells; Smart-seq2 ovary 5 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Early post-mitotic germ cells of the 16-cell cyst.*

## Summary
Female germline cells of the newly completed 16-cell cyst that have exited the mitotic cyst divisions.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Adult ovary, germarium (early 16-cell cyst stage). Found only in the ovary analysis.

## Function
Cells at this point commit to the oocyte versus nurse-cell fates and enter the endocycle/meiotic programmes.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class: female germline cell. Assigned in the ovary annotation by tissue experts. Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x labels.

## Mapping notes
A stage bin in the germline lineage immediately preceding (or overlapping with) the author's "16-cell germline cyst in germarium region 2a and 2b" type; the boundary between the two is the authors' own. Not a distinct cell type in the anatomical sense. Closest level is the female germline cell, in a cyst stage. Adult data.
