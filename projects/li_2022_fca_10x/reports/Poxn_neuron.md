# Poxn neuron

*Author type `Poxn neuron` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 102 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. A small head neuron group named for Poxn expression.*

## Summary
A group of 102 head neurons the authors named after the transcription factor Poxn (Pox neuro), with no FBbt term. Poxn is a gene associated with gustatory/other chemosensory and some central neurons in the fly; the paper itself does not describe the group further.

## Markers
Poxn is the naming marker. No marker genes for this type are given in the paper text or supplement; its identity rests on the authors' integrated annotation of the head with the Davie et al. 2018 brain data (annotation transfer to clusters, the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier, and an SVM classifier, followed by manual curation).

## Location
Head only (10x head, 102 cells; 46 female, 54 male, 2 mixed). Anatomical position of these neurons is not stated by the authors.

## Function
Not described in the paper.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term). Broad class: neuron. Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Marker-named (Poxn-expressing) neuron group; anatomically unspecific, so the call implies a marker co-expression group rather than a morphologically defined single type. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
