# bitter-sensitive labellar taste bristle

*Author type `bitter-sensitive labellar taste bristle` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x proboscis and maxillary palps 151, Smart-seq2 3 (label transfer); author term: FBbt bitter-sensitive labellar taste bristle. Bitter gustatory neurons of the labellum.*

## Summary
A gustatory cluster (154 cells) in the proboscis and maxillary palp dataset (Kristin Scott lab), broad class sensory neuron. The nuclei are of neurons, though the label names the bristle.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Labellum of the adult proboscis. Bitter-sensitive bristles comprise all I-type and most S-type sensilla (per the FBbt definition the authors cite).

## Function
Taste detection of bitter compounds.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00048160, a sensillum (structure) term, broad class sensory neuron. The paper notes gustatory and olfactory receptor neurons were annotated in this tissue.

## Mapping notes
The call implies the bitter gustatory neurons innervating these bristles, grouped across bristle types (a grouped call, not a receptor-defined type). Adult data.
