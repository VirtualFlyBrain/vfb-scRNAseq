# late cyst cell branch b

*Author type `late cyst cell branch b` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 316 cells, Smart-seq2 testis 6 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. Late-stage cyst cells, branch b of a pair.*

## Summary
Cyst cells at a late stage of the cyst lineage (by name), the `branch b` member of a late a/b pair. Not among the clusters used in the trajectory analysis and not described in the paper text.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given in the paper or supplement text for this type.

## Location
Adult testis (plus seminal vesicle) sample from 0-1 day old males. Cyst cells are the somatic support cells that enclose and nurture germline cells during spermatogenesis, and are derived from cyst stem cells (CySCs) at the testis apical tip.

## Function
Cyst cells ensheath germ cell cysts and support their differentiation (general Drosophila knowledge, not from the paper). The authors state no specific function.

## Mapping notes
Stage-bin of the cyst cell lineage (late) with an a/b split; not a distinct classical type. The cells are adult (0-1 day old male testis); the label names a lineage state, not a developmental stage.
