# spermatogonium-spermatocyte transition

*Author type `spermatogonium-spermatocyte transition` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x testis 202, Smart-seq2 testis 2; author term: FBbt:00004936 spermatocyte). Germ cells at the boundary between spermatogonial divisions and the spermatocyte stage.*

## Summary
A transitional state between late proliferating spermatogonia and early primary spermatocytes in the adult testis. The authors assign it the general spermatocyte ontology term despite the transitional name.

## Markers
No cluster-specific markers are given in the paper or supplement text. The trajectory analysis of spermatogonia-spermatocyte used clusters annotated as spermatogonia or spermatocytes (slingshot, start cell spermatogonium).

## Location
Testis of 0-1 day old adult males.

## Function
Exit from mitotic amplification and onset of the spermatocyte program.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00004936 (spermatocyte); broad class male germline cell. Smart-seq2 cells were classifier-assigned.

## Mapping notes
A stage-bin at a lineage boundary, mapped by the authors to the spermatocyte type (a boundary state, not a distinct anatomical type). Data are adult.
