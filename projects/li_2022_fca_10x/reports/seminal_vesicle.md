# seminal vesicle

*Author type `seminal vesicle` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x male reproductive glands 179 cells; Smart-seq2 male reproductive glands 4 cells; author term: FBbt:00004958 seminal vesicle). Organ-named cluster in the male reproductive glands analysis.*

## Summary
Cells annotated as seminal vesicle, with broad class gland. The seminal vesicle is the enlarged distal vas deferens that stores sperm; its cells would be those of its wall (epithelial, possibly secretory).

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the supplementary text available here (figure S17 legend shows only the tSNE annotation); none are asserted.

## Location
Male reproductive glands analysis; adult males. Seminal vesicle tissue is also described as collected with the testis (supplement, testis methods).

## Function
Sperm storage region (FBbt definition: it fills with mature sperm in 5-day flies, per the supplement); no function is analysed for the cluster.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00004958 (seminal vesicle); broad class gland.

## Mapping notes
Organ-named label applied to a cell cluster, implying the cells of the seminal vesicle wall as one type. Adult.
