# adult olfactory receptor neuron Ir84a, Ir31a, Ir76a, Ir76b, Ir8a, Or35a

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron Ir84a, Ir31a, Ir76a, Ir76b, Ir8a, Or35a` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 300, Smart-seq2 antenna 5; author term: Manual Annotation). Antennal ORN cluster named by a mixed set of IR and Or receptors.*

## Summary
A cluster of olfactory receptor neurons named by co-detected receptors Ir84a, Ir31a, Ir76a, Ir76b, Ir8a and Or35a. Ir8a and Ir76b are broadly used IR co-receptors; Ir84a, Ir31a and Ir76a are tuning IRs; Or35a is an odorant receptor.

## Markers
Ir84a, Ir31a, Ir76a, Ir76b, Ir8a, Or35a (from the label). Further markers in Table S2 (not available here).

## Location
Antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Chemosensory; likely ORNs of coeloconic-type (IR-expressing) sensilla plus an Or35a class, but the authors do not state this.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call of several named ORN classes (receptor-defined), not a single class; adult.
