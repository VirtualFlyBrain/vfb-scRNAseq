# antimicrobial peptide-producing cell

*Author type `antimicrobial peptide-producing cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 292, Smart-seq2 gut 12; author term: Manual Annotation). Marker-defined group; broad class 'artefact'.*

## Summary
Gut cluster defined by antimicrobial peptide (AMP) expression; the authors class it as an artefact.

## Markers
The label implies high expression of antimicrobial peptide genes; no specific genes are listed in the sources I searched.

## Location
Dissected gut sample. The sources do not say which gut region contributes.

## Function
AMPs are produced in the gut epithelium on immune stimulation; the authors, however, assign the broad class 'artefact', so this may reflect a stress/contamination-driven or ambient-RNA-driven state rather than a bona fide cell type (the reason is not stated in the sources).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (the authors had no FBbt term); authors' broad class: artefact. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Marker (expression-state) group, not an anatomical type, and flagged by the authors as an artefact. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
