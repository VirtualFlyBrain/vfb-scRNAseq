# adult olfactory receptor neuron Ir56a+, Orco-

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron Ir56a+, Orco-` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 239, Smart-seq2 antenna 3; author term: Manual Annotation). Antennal ORN cluster defined by Ir56a expression and absence of the co-receptor Orco.*

## Summary
Ir56a-expressing, Orco-negative olfactory receptor neurons, i.e. a non-Orco (ionotropic-receptor class) ORN population.

## Markers
Ir56a is the defining receptor; Orco is absent (Orco marks the Or-class ORNs). Further markers are in the authors' Table S2 (not available here).

## Location
Antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Chemosensory; Ir56a is a member of the ionotropic receptor family. Specific ligand or glomerulus not stated by the authors here.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: a single receptor-defined ORN class (or the cluster carrying it); adult.
