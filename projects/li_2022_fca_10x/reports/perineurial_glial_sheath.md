# perineurial glial sheath

*Author type `perineurial glial sheath` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 6 and body 684 cells; author term: FBbt perineurial glial sheath). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Surface glia of the nervous system.*

## Summary
690 nuclei of the perineurial glia, the outermost glial layer of the nervous system, forming the first diffusion barrier. Mostly in the body analysis (684 cells; 246 female, 402 male, 36 mixed), where it covers the ventral nerve cord and nerves; the head has only 6 cells (2 female, 4 male).

## Markers
Not stated in the paper; the glial identity is by the authors' broad class glial cell.

## Location
Surface of the CNS and peripheral nerves.

## Function
Nutrient uptake and a barrier to diffusion (FBbt definition, citing Limmer et al. 2014).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: perineurial glial sheath (a cell layer, not a cell); broad class: glial cell. Body annotation from the whole-body dataset; head annotation from the head dataset.

## Mapping notes
Structure-named group: the label names the sheath layer, so the cells are its perineurial glia. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
