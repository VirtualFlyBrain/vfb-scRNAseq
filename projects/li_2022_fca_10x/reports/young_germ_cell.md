# young germ cell

*Author type `young germ cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x ovary 2433 cells; Smart-seq2 ovary 109 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). The largest ovarian germline group, early-stage female germ cells.*

## Summary
Early female germline cells of the adult ovary, preceding the 16-cell cyst stages: the author label implies germline stem cells and early cysts (cystoblast and mitotically dividing cysts) rather than a single cell type; the paper text does not itemise its composition.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Adult ovary, anterior germarium (inferred from the stage label). Found only in the ovary analysis.

## Function
Includes the self-renewing and differentiating early germline that feeds oogenesis; no further function is stated by the authors.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class: female germline cell. Labels come from the ovary annotation by Nystul/Rust and colleagues, with integration to published ovary scRNA-seq datasets (fig. S37). Smart-seq2 cells were classifier-assigned from the 10x labels.

## Mapping notes
A grouped, stage-bin call (young germline) spanning more than one cell state; not a single cell type. Closest level is the female germline cell, with stage-qualified wording at most. Adult data (5-day adult ovary).
