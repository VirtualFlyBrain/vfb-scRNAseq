# posterior terminal follicle cell ca. St. 5-8

*Author type `posterior terminal follicle cell ca. St. 5-8` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:ovary 377 cells, ss2:ovary 7 cells; author term: FBbt:00048554 posterior terminal follicle cell). Adult ovary follicle-cell population.*

## Summary
Posterior terminal follicle cells of roughly stage 5-8 egg chambers, the follicle cells next to the posterior polar cells.

## Markers
The paper and supplementary text give no marker list for this type; none is invented here. Annotation of ovary types follows the Nystul/Rust lab ovary atlases cited in the supplement (Jevitt et al. 2020; Rust et al. 2020), with the ovary integrated against published scRNA-seq datasets (supplementary fig. S37).

## Location
Posterior pole of the egg chamber, adjacent to the posterior polar cells. Source: dissected ovaries of 5-day-old w1118 adult females (Nystul lab sample), single-nucleus RNA-seq.

## Function
Signal back to the oocyte to polarize its cytoskeleton (per the FBbt definition quoted by the authors' term).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00048554 posterior terminal follicle cell; broad class: female reproductive system. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation, not annotated independently.

## Mapping notes
Single cell type with an approximate stage range; the stage range is part of the author label, the term is the stage-independent type. The data are from adult flies (the cells are adult ovarian follicle cells at the stated egg-chamber stage, not a developmental time series of the animal).
