Epithelial cells of the esophagus, found in the proboscis and maxillary palp dissection.

## Location
Proboscis and maxillary palp analysis. Per the definition the esophagus is the foregut portion from the cibarium through the brain to the proventriculus, so head-dissected tissue would include its anterior part.

## Function
Foregut conduit for ingested food; no cluster-specific detail in the sources.
