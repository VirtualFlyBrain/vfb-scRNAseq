# adult olfactory receptor neuron acid-sensing, Ir64a

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron acid-sensing, Ir64a` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 106; author term: Manual Annotation). Acid-sensing Ir64a ORNs of the antenna.*

## Summary
Ionotropic-receptor ORNs described as acid-sensing and expressing Ir64a. Ir64a neurons are known to respond to acids and to project to the DC4 glomerulus; they reside in the sacculus (and antennal coeloconic sensilla).

## Markers
Ir64a (from the label), with IR co-receptors (Ir8a, Ir76b) expected. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna (including the sacculus/coeloconic compartment). Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Detection of acids, in the ionotropic-receptor olfactory pathway.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: single receptor-defined ORN class (acid-sensing, Ir64a); sister cluster to the Ir75a/b/c, Ir64a one; adult.
