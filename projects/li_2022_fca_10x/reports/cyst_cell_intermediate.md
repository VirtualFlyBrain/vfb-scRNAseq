# cyst cell intermediate

*Author type `cyst cell intermediate` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 1797 cells, Smart-seq2 testis 17 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. Large intermediate-state cyst cell cluster in the testis.*

## Summary
Cyst cells in a state the name places between early and later cyst cell clusters; at 1797 nuclei it is the largest cyst cell cluster in the 10x testis analysis. The paper does not describe it specifically, and it was not among the clusters used in the cyst cell trajectory.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given in the paper or supplement text for this type.

## Location
Adult testis (plus seminal vesicle) sample from 0-1 day old males. Cyst cells are the somatic support cells that enclose and nurture germline cells during spermatogenesis, and are derived from cyst stem cells (CySCs) at the testis apical tip.

## Function
Cyst cells ensheath germ cell cysts and support their differentiation (general Drosophila knowledge, not from the paper). The authors state no specific function.

## Label provenance
The authors had no FBbt term for this label (author ontology id: Manual Annotation); broad class: male reproductive system. Not discussed in the paper text; the name is the only evidence.

## Mapping notes
Stage-bin of the cyst cell lineage (intermediate), not a distinct classical type. The cells are adult (0-1 day old male testis); the label names a lineage state, not a developmental stage.
