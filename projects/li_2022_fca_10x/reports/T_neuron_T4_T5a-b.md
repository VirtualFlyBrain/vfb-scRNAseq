# T neuron T4/T5a-b

*Author type `T neuron T4/T5a-b` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 1525 cells; author term: FBbt T4a, T4b, T5a, T5b neurons). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Subcluster of T4/T5 neurons.*

## Summary
1525 head nuclei forming the a-b subcluster of T4/T5 neurons: T4a, T4b, T5a and T5b, i.e. the subtypes whose lobula plate arbors lie in layers 1 and 2. T4/T5 motion-detecting neurons of the optic lobe (medulla/lobula to lobula plate).

## Markers
acj6 and SoxN mark T4/T5 neurons overall (paper text); the paper gives no markers that separate a-b from c-d.

## Location
Head only (1525 cells; 778 female, 617 male, 130 mixed). T4a/T5a arborise in lobula plate layer 1 and T4b/T5b in layer 2.

## Function
Layer 1 subtypes respond to front-to-back and layer 2 subtypes to back-to-front motion (FBbt definitions, citing Henning et al. 2022); cholinergic.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology ids: T4a, T4b, T5a, T5b neurons (four terms); broad class: neuron. Subclustering of the T4/T5 group in the head analysis; Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Grouped call: four subtypes (T4 and T5, layers 1-2) pooled in one cluster, so the call is a group with common parent T neuron (or a combination of the four). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
