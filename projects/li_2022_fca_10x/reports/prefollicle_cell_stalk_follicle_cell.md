# prefollicle cell/stalk follicle cell

*Author type `prefollicle cell/stalk follicle cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:body 107 cells; author terms: FBbt:00004910 stalk follicle cell and FBbt:00047835 prefollicle cell). Grouped call in the whole-body sample.*

## Summary
A combined group of prefollicle cells (germarial progeny of follicle stem cells) and stalk follicle cells (which connect successive egg chambers), not resolved from each other in the whole-body analysis.

## Markers
No marker genes are given in the paper or supplementary text for this type; none is invented here.

## Location
Germarium (prefollicle cells) and the stalks between egg chambers of the vitellarium, from the ovaries of adult females in the whole-body sample (5-day adults). In the ovary-specific analyses stalk follicle cells are a separate type, and prefollicle cells are grouped with follicle stem cells.

## Function
Prefollicle cells differentiate into follicle cells and stalk cells; stalk cells link and space adjacent egg chambers (general knowledge; the term definitions are quoted by the authors' ontology ids).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology ids: stalk follicle cell and prefollicle cell (two ids); broad class: female reproductive system. The two-id label shows the authors treat them as one group here.

## Mapping notes
Grouped call of two related but distinct lineage cell types (a slash-joined label); not a single type. Adult data.
