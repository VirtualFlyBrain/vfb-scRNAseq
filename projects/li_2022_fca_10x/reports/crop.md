# crop

*Author type `crop` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 4565, Smart-seq2 gut 150; author term: FBbt:00003133 crop). Organ-named group from the gut analysis.*

## Summary
The largest gut group (4715 cells): cells of the crop, the foregut food-storage sac.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Gut analysis: the FCA gut sample included the midgut/hindgut junction (where the Malpighian tubules join) and the crop, which the earlier Hung et al. gut dataset had removed (supplement, fig. S36 legend). Crop is a bilobed sac off the foregut before the midgut.

## Function
Per the FBbt definition the crop stores ingested liquid food. The supplement notes the integration with published gut data revealed 5 subtypes of crop cells, so this group is expected to pool several crop cell populations.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00003133 crop; authors' broad class: epithelial cell. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Organ-named group: a grouped call over the crop's (epithelial) cells, likely including several subtypes; not a single cell type. The crop's visceral muscle is a separate author type. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
