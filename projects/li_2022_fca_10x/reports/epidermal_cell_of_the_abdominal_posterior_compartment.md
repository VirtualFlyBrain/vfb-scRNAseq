# epidermal cell of the abdominal posterior compartment

*Author type `epidermal cell of the abdominal posterior compartment` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x body wall 68 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. Posterior-compartment epidermis of the abdomen.*

## Summary
A small epidermal cluster (68 cells; 48 female, 20 male) of the body wall dataset (Yuh Nung Jan lab), broad class epithelial cell.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Adult abdominal epidermis, in the posterior compartment of each segment (the compartment defined by engrailed expression, established background).

## Function
Cuticle-secreting epidermis; the posterior compartment is a developmental lineage compartment.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term), broad class epithelial cell. Sibling cluster in the same dataset: epidermal cell specialized in antimicrobial response.
