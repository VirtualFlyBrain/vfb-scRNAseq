# adult olfactory receptor neuron unknown type, Orco-

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron unknown type, Orco-` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 204, Smart-seq2 antenna 1; author term: Manual Annotation). Orco-negative antennal ORNs of unresolved receptor identity.*

## Summary
Olfactory receptor neurons lacking Orco, with no specific receptor assigned by the authors.

## Markers
Orco-negative; no defining receptor named. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Olfactory (likely non-Or-class), not otherwise characterised.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.
