# corpora cardiaca cell

*Author type `corpora cardiaca cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); Smart-seq2 akhCC 592 cells (not in 10x); no author ontology term. Sorted Akh-expressing cells.*

## Summary
The whole Smart-seq2 akhCC analysis: GFP+ nuclei FACS-sorted from Akh-GAL4 > UAS-unc84GFP flies (Seung Kim lab), typed from the sample description "corpora cardiaca cell (CC)". Supplementary table lists 351 / 241 cells (per run).

## Markers
Akh-GAL4 driver defines the sort; adipokinetic hormone is the characteristic CC product (established background). No cluster-specific markers in the supplement.

## Location
Adult corpora cardiaca, endocrine part of the ring gland adjacent to the aorta and brain.

## Function
Production of adipokinetic hormone to mobilize energy stores (functional counterpart to insulin).

## Label provenance
Label comes from the sample description, not from the h5ad. No author ontology id or broad class. 5-day adults.

## Mapping notes
Authors' call is a single sorted-population endocrine type (Akh-producing cells). Adult data.
