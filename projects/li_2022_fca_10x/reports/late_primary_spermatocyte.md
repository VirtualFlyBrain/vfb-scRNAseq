# late primary spermatocyte

*Author type `late primary spermatocyte` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x testis 301, Smart-seq2 testis 2; author term: Manual Annotation). The most advanced primary spermatocyte state in the adult testis.*

## Summary
Late primary spermatocytes, the last stage before meiotic divisions, in the testis analysis. The paper notes that late spermatocytes express, at low level, marker genes of unrelated cell types.

## Markers
From the paper (late spermatocytes generally): Y-linked fertility factors kl-3 and kl-5 upregulated; low-level Upd1, eya (somatic), grh (epithelial), Mhc (muscle) and Hml (hemocyte). The Upd1 expression is why the hub cluster was removed by the Stringent filter, so the testis UMAP uses the Relaxed dataset. Not a per-cluster list.

## Location
Testis of 0-1 day old adult males.

## Function
Terminal growth and transcriptional activity of primary spermatocytes before meiosis.

## Label provenance
No authors' ontology term (Manual Annotation); broad class male germline cell. Smart-seq2 cells classifier-assigned.

## Mapping notes
A stage-bin (late state) of the spermatocyte lineage, with no authors' FBbt term although other spermatocyte clusters were assigned the spermatocyte term. Data are adult.
