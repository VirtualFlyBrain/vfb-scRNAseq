# btl-GAL4 positive female cell, likely to be ovary cell, sim+, H15+

*Author type `btl-GAL4 positive female cell, likely to be ovary cell, sim+, H15+` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:trachea 1009 cells, ss2:trachea 15 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Female cells of unclear origin in the btl-GAL4 trachea sample, attributed by the authors to the ovary.*

## Summary
A group of female cells recovered in the trachea sample that the authors judged likely to be ovarian cells rather than tracheal cells. Distinguished by co-expression of sim and H15 (a T-box gene), per the label; presumably a sub-population of the sim+ group.

## Location
Not a tracheal type. The trachea sample was btl-GAL4, tub-Gal80ts > UAS-lamGFP adult flies (shifted from 18 C to 29 C for 5 days), nuclei enriched by GFP FACS. The authors infer an ovarian origin for these female cells (the label says 'likely').
