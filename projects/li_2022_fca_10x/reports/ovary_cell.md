# ovary cell

*Author type `ovary cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x oenocyte 1028 cells, 10x body wall 49 cells; Smart-seq2 oenocyte 49 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). A group of ovary-like cells appearing in non-ovary analyses.*

## Summary
A coarse group in the oenocyte and body wall analyses that the authors named "ovary cell". Because those tissues are not ovary, it is a contaminating or ectopic population (ovarian cells carried into oenocyte and body-wall preparations), named by the authors; cell identity within the ovary is not resolved.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Oenocyte analysis (1028 cells: 583 female, 445 male) and body wall analysis (49 cells: 44 female, 5 male). The male-labelled cells show that the group is a transcriptional cluster and not strictly female or ovarian in origin.

## Function
None specified; the label reflects tissue origin.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class: female reproductive system. Smart-seq2 oenocyte cells were classifier-assigned from the 10x labels. It should not be confused with ovarian cells annotated in the ovary analysis.

## Mapping notes
An organ-level remainder/contaminant group rather than a cell type; the broad class is the female reproductive system as a whole. No specific cell type is implied. Adult data.
