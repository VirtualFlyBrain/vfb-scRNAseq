# adult olfactory receptor neuron Or47a, Or56a and likely other ORN types

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron Or47a, Or56a and likely other ORN types` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 594, Smart-seq2 antenna 6; author term: Manual Annotation). Antennal ORN cluster named by Or47a and Or56a plus unspecified others.*

## Summary
Odorant-receptor ORNs with Or47a and Or56a detected; the authors say the cluster likely contains other ORN types too. Or47a (ab5) and Or56a (ab4, geosmin-sensing) are known antennal classes.

## Markers
Or47a, Or56a (from the label); others not named. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna (basiconic sensilla). Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Olfactory; contains the geosmin-responsive Or56a class and the Or47a class.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call: two named ORN classes plus an explicit unresolved remainder of other ORN types; adult.
