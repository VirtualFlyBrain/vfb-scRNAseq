# adult peripheral nervous system

*Author type `adult peripheral nervous system` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x body 2588, body_wall 175, proboscis_and_maxillary_palps 2637 cells; ss2 body_wall 4, proboscis_and_maxillary_palps 62; author term: FBbt adult peripheral nervous system). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Peripheral neurons of the body and the proboscis/maxillary palps.*

## Summary
5466 neuron nuclei labelled as adult peripheral nervous system (PNS) neurons: sensory and other neurons outside the CNS, in the body (including body wall) and in the proboscis with maxillary palps. It is a coarse annotation, not resolved into sensory modalities.

## Markers
No markers are given for this class in the paper or supplement; neuron identity from the broad class.

## Location
Body (2588; 1441 F, 1069 M), body wall (175), proboscis and maxillary palps (2637; 140 F, 2497 M); ss2 adds 66 cells. The large male skew in proboscis/palps reflects sample composition.

## Function
Peripheral sensory neurons (mechano-, chemosensory) and related neurons; unresolved.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: adult peripheral nervous system (a system); broad class: neuron.

## Mapping notes
Structure-named group: peripheral neurons pooled across tissues (region-based bulk category). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
