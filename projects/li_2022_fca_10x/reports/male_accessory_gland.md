# male accessory gland

*Author type `male accessory gland` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x body 338, fat body 56, gut 56; Smart-seq2 fat body 6; author term: FBbt:00004959 male accessory gland). Gland cluster found in non-reproductive tissue analyses (body, fat body, gut).*

## Summary
Cells labelled male accessory gland, broad class gland. Notably they occur only in the body, fat body and gut analyses and not in the male reproductive glands analysis, where accessory gland cells are not labelled with this type; this suggests accessory gland tissue present in those dissections. Accessory glands are composed of binucleate secretory cells; the paper reports two nuclear types of main cell (fig. S17).

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the supplementary text available here (figure S17 legend shows only the tSNE annotation); none are asserted.

## Location
Whole-body, fat body and gut analyses; adult males.

## Function
Seminal fluid protein secretion (main and secondary cells) in general fly biology; the paper gives no analysis for these clusters.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00004959 (male accessory gland); broad class gland.

## Mapping notes
Organ-named label for a gland cell group; it implies accessory gland secretory cells (main and secondary, unresolved) as a single, coarse type. Adult.
