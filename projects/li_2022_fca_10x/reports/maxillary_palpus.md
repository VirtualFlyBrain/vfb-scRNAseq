# maxillary palpus

*Author type `maxillary palpus` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x proboscis and maxillary palps 660, Smart-seq2 21 (label transfer); author term: FBbt maxillary palp. Sensory neurons of the maxillary palp.*

## Summary
A 681-cell cluster in the proboscis and maxillary palp dataset (Kristin Scott lab), broad class sensory neuron. Fig. S7B shows orco expression in one cluster annotated as maxillary palp olfactory receptor neurons, in which all 7 known palp olfactory receptor genes are detectable, including co-expressed Or33c and Or85e; the palp types pb1, pb2 and pb3 are named.

## Markers
orco (olfactory co-receptor); palp ORs including Or33c and Or85e (fig. S7 legend). Unclear whether the cluster listed as maxillary palpus is the same one shown.

## Location
Adult maxillary palp, the paired sensory appendage on the proboscis, carrying basiconic sensilla.

## Function
Olfaction (and some other chemosensation) by palp neurons.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00004546 (maxillary palp, an appendage), broad class sensory neuron.

## Mapping notes
The call implies a single grouped class of palp sensory neurons (likely olfactory) rather than receptor-defined types. Adult data.
