# male gonad associated epithelium

*Author type `male gonad associated epithelium` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 11063 cells, Smart-seq2 testis 85 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. The largest cluster in the testis analysis, an epithelial cell type of the male gonad.*

## Summary
By name, epithelial cells associated with the male gonad. At 11063 nuclei it is by far the largest testis cluster in the 10x data. The paper and supplement text consulted give no description, markers or composition for it, and it is not part of the cyst cell trajectory; whether it covers testis sheath, seminal vesicle or other epithelia cannot be determined from the evidence.

No cluster-specific marker genes are given in the paper or supplement text for this type.
