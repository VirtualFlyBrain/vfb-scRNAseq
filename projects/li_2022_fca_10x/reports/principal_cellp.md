# principal cell*

*Author type `principal cell*` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x Malpighian tubule 633, Smart-seq2 Malpighian tubule 59; author term: Manual Annotation). Asterisked cell type; the dominant tubule cluster.*

## Summary
Principal cells, the main secretory cells of the Malpighian tubule, labelled with an asterisk.

## Markers
No markers listed for this cluster. For the neighbouring stellate cells the supplement gives tsh, SecCl and Drip. The paper reports strong female bias in principal and stellate cell gene expression.

## Location
Malpighian tubule dissection analysis (the authors drove nuclear GFP with Drip-GAL4 to label stellate cells and sequenced all nuclei).

## Function
Principal cells are the main cation-transporting, fluid-secreting cells of the tubule epithelium; the sources give no further detail.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (the authors had no FBbt term); authors' broad class: epithelial cell. The asterisk is not explained in the paper text or supplementary methods and legends I searched; it appears only in the Malpighian tubule analysis labels. The supplement says only that Drip-GAL4>UAS-nlsGFP was used to label stellate cells in this tissue and that stellate cells were readily annotated from tsh, SecCl and Drip. The asterisked labels may flag provisional or non-tubule-intrinsic calls, but that is an inference, not stated. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Cell-type call: the principal cell of the tubule; the asterisk's meaning is unknown, so any caveat on its scope (for example tubule region) cannot be derived. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
