# cyst cell branch a

*Author type `cyst cell branch a` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 299 cells, Smart-seq2 testis 3 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. One of two sibling branches of cyst cells in the testis somatic lineage.*

## Summary
Testis cyst cells forming the `branch a` cluster of a branch a/b pair. The authors give no further definition; the paper text does not describe this pair specifically.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given in the paper or supplement text for this type.

## Location
Adult testis (plus seminal vesicle) sample from 0-1 day old males. Cyst cells are the somatic support cells that enclose and nurture germline cells during spermatogenesis, and are derived from cyst stem cells (CySCs) at the testis apical tip.

## Function
Cyst cells ensheath germ cell cysts and support their differentiation (general Drosophila knowledge, not from the paper). The authors state no specific function for this branch.

## Label provenance
The authors had no FBbt term for this label (author ontology id: Manual Annotation); broad class: male reproductive system. The a/b naming parallels the late and spermatocyte cyst cell branch pairs.

## Mapping notes
