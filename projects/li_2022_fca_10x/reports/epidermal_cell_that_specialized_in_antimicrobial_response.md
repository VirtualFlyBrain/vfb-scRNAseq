# epidermal cell that specialized in antimicrobial response

*Author type `epidermal cell that specialized in antimicrobial response` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x body wall 1043, Smart-seq2 body wall 11 (label transfer); author term: Manual Annotation. Epidermis with an antimicrobial-peptide program.*

## Summary
The largest epidermal cluster in the body wall dataset (1054 cells), broad class epithelial cell. The name describes a functional specialization rather than a position.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots). The label implies expression of an immune/antimicrobial effector program (e.g. antimicrobial peptides), but the supplement we have does not list the genes.

## Location
Adult body wall epidermis.

## Function
Local epithelial immune defence against microbes (as implied by the label).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term), broad class epithelial cell.

## Mapping notes
The call implies a functional state/subtype of epidermal cell (possibly most epidermal cells recovered), not an anatomically located type; a grouped call under epidermal cell is plausible. Adult data.
