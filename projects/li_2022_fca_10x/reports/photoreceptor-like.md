# photoreceptor-like

*Author type `photoreceptor-like` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 117 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Sensory neurons resembling photoreceptors.*

## Summary
117 head sensory neurons the authors labelled photoreceptor-like, with no FBbt term. The paper describes photoreceptors of the head as head-unique clusters (separate from brain cells) but does not define this group further.

## Markers
Not given. Identity comes from the head annotation; no markers listed in the paper or supplement.

## Location
Head only (117 cells; 73 female, 44 male). Exact location unspecified; presumably retina/eye-associated.

## Function
Not described.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class: sensory neuron. Annotated in the head analysis.

## Mapping notes
Vague, resemblance-named group; the authors did not commit to a specific photoreceptor type (a remainder-like category). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
