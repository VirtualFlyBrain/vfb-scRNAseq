# antennal trichoid sensillum at4

*Author type `antennal trichoid sensillum at4` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 7 cells; author term: FBbt antennal trichoid sensillum at4). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Seven olfactory sensory neuron nuclei assigned to a sensillum class.*

## Summary
Seven head nuclei, broad class sensory neuron, labelled by the antennal trichoid sensillum class at4. In the FBbt definition this sensillum houses the dendrites of Or47b and Or88a ORNs (some with Or65a). The cells themselves would be olfactory receptor neurons of that sensillum type.

## Markers
Not given in the paper; the sensillum identity corresponds to Or47b/Or88a (and sometimes Or65a) ORNs by the FBbt definition.

## Location
Head (antenna, third segment) only; 7 cells (3 female, 4 male). Trichoid sensilla are on the antennal surface.

## Function
Pheromone-sensing ORNs (Or47b and Or88a are linked to fly social/courtship odours; FBbt definition cites Grabe et al. 2016; Gonzales et al. 2021).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: antennal trichoid sensillum at4 (a structure, not a cell); broad class: sensory neuron. Annotation made in the head analysis; no specific rationale in the text.

## Mapping notes
Structure-named group: the label names a sensillum, so the cells are the olfactory sensory neurons housed in it (Or47b/Or88a). Very small cluster (7 cells). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
