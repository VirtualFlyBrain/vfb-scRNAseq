# adult salivary gland

*Author type `adult salivary gland` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x Malpighian tubule 876, 10x heart 43, 10x body wall 24, Smart-seq2 Malpighian tubule 15; author term: FBbt:00003135 adult salivary gland). Organ-named group; the broad class is 'gland'.*

## Summary
Gland cells (958 cells) of the salivary gland, found mostly in the Malpighian tubule dissection.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Largely the Malpighian tubule dissection (891 cells), with small numbers in heart and body wall; the sources do not say why salivary gland cells dominate that sample (likely co-dissected tissue; inference).

## Function
Per the FBbt definition, paired long epithelial tubes of secretory epithelium extending from the neck region into the abdomen near the gut; secretory.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00003135 adult salivary gland; authors' broad class: gland. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Organ-named group: a grouped call over salivary gland secretory cells (and possibly duct cells), not one defined type. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
