# spermatocyte cyst cell branch a

*Author type `spermatocyte cyst cell branch a` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 491 cells, Smart-seq2 testis 2 cells; author term: FBbt:00057004 spermatocyte cyst cell. Cyst cells associated with spermatocytes, branch a of two sister clusters.*

## Summary
One of the two related cyst cell clusters that the paper says are likely associated with spermatocytes, branching from early cyst cells (Fig. 6G); both were used in the early cyst cell trajectory. The paper's testis case study describes the somatic lineage as CySCs expressing the cell-cycle marker string, transitioning into post-mitotic (string-negative) early cyst cells, then branching into two related clusters of cyst cells likely associated with spermatocytes (Fig. 6G). The supplement's trajectory methods used cyst stem cell, early cyst cell and the two spermatocyte cyst cell branches clusters, with cyst stem cell as the start.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given in the paper or supplement text for this type.

## Location
Adult testis (plus seminal vesicle) sample from 0-1 day old males. Cyst cells are the somatic support cells that enclose and nurture germline cells during spermatogenesis, and are derived from cyst stem cells (CySCs) at the testis apical tip.

## Function
Cyst cells ensheath germ cell cysts and support their differentiation (general Drosophila knowledge, not from the paper). Spermatocyte cyst cells enclose spermatocyte cysts.

## Label provenance
The authors assign the FBbt term spermatocyte cyst cell (defined there as a cyst cell that is part of a spermatocyte cyst; author ontology id FBbt:00057004), the same term for branches a and b; broad class: male reproductive system. The paper says the branches are 'likely' associated with spermatocytes.

## Mapping notes
Grouped call: two clusters (a, b) share one author concept (spermatocyte cyst cell); the branch split is a clustering distinction only. Adult testis.
