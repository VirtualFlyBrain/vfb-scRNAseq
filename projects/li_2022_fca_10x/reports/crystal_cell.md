# crystal cell

*Author type `crystal cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x heart 162, Smart-seq2 heart 5 (label transfer); author term: FBbt embryonic/larval crystal cell. Melanizing hemocytes of the adult.*

## Summary
Crystal cells (167 cells) annotated in the heart dataset; broad class hemocyte. In the cross-tissue Hml+ hemocyte analysis the authors state crystal cells segregate clearly through high PPO1, PPO2 and lozenge expression, while plasmatocytes form several other clusters.

## Markers
PPO1, PPO2, lozenge (lz) (supplementary methods, hemocyte analysis; fig. S26B shows PPO1/PPO2).

## Location
Adult hemocytes circulating or sessile in the abdomen and recovered in the heart dissection; the cross-tissue analysis found hemocytes in many tissues.

## Function
Prophenoloxidase-mediated melanization in immune and wound responses (established background).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00001690 (embryonic/larval crystal cell), broad class hemocyte. The authors used a larval/embryonic term for adult cells, apparently because no adult-specific term was chosen.

## Mapping notes
The call implies a single crystal cell type; the stage in the author term is embryonic/larval, while the data are adult.
