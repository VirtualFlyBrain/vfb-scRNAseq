# olfactory receptor neuron, coeloconics

*Author type `olfactory receptor neuron, coeloconics` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 393, Smart-seq2 antenna 8; author term: Manual Annotation). ORNs of coeloconic sensilla.*

## Summary
Olfactory receptor neurons annotated at the level of the coeloconic sensillum class, without naming specific receptors in the label.

## Markers
No receptor named in the label. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna (coeloconic sensilla, mostly ionotropic-receptor ORNs). Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Olfactory, with roles in detecting amines, acids and other volatiles.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call defined by sensillum class (coeloconic) rather than receptor; adult.
