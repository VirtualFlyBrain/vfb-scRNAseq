# transmedullary neuron Tm3a

*Author type `transmedullary neuron Tm3a` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 615 cells; author term: FBbt transmedullary neuron Tm3). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Optic lobe medulla-to-lobula neurons.*

## Summary
615 head neurons identified as the Tm3 transmedullary neuron (labelled Tm3a), a narrow-field medulla-to-lobula neuron. The paper lists lamina, medulla, lobula and lobula plate types identified using the Ozel et al. classifier.

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the paper text or supplement; its identity rests on the authors' integrated annotation of the head with the Davie et al. 2018 brain data (annotation transfer to clusters, the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier, and an SVM classifier, followed by manual curation).

## Location
Head only (615 cells; 309 female, 252 male, 54 mixed). Medulla (layers M1, M4, M5 arbors) projecting to lobula layers 1 and 4.

## Function
Receives input from L1, L5 and Mi1 and outputs to T4 neurons (FBbt definition, citing Takemura et al. 2013); part of the ON motion pathway.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: transmedullary neuron Tm3; broad class: neuron. The author label carries a subtype letter 'a' while the term is the general Tm3. Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Single type (Tm3), with author subtype suffix 'a' not mapped to a separate class. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
