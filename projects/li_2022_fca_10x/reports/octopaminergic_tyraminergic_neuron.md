# octopaminergic/tyraminergic neuron

*Author type `octopaminergic/tyraminergic neuron` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 120 cells; author term: FBbt octopaminergic neuron and tyraminergic neuron). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Neurons defined by monoamine biosynthesis.*

## Summary
120 head neurons labelled by neurotransmitter identity: octopaminergic and tyraminergic (octopamine is synthesised from tyramine, so these populations overlap in markers). The paper does not elaborate.

## Markers
No genes are given in the paper; the identity relies on the authors' integrated annotation. (Tdc2 and Tbh are the canonical enzymes, but this is not stated by the authors.)

## Location
Head only (120 cells; 54 female, 63 male, 3 mixed). Brain/central neurons; no subregion stated.

## Function
Release of octopamine/tyramine, modulating arousal, locomotion, feeding and sensory processing.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology ids: octopaminergic neuron and tyraminergic neuron (two terms); broad class: neuron. Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Neurotransmitter-defined grouped call (two transmitter classes combined); not morphological. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
