# adult ostium

*Author type `adult ostium` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x heart 56 cells; author term: FBbt adult ostial pore. Ostial cardial cells of the adult heart.*

## Summary
A small cardial cell cluster (56 cells; 16 female, 40 male) in the heart dataset, distinct from the working cardiomyocytes. The label names the ostia, the openings through which hemolymph enters the heart.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Ostia are lateral slits in the adult heart wall (five pairs: two in the first chamber and one in each other chamber); the cells would be those forming or flanking these inflow tracts.

## Function
Hemolymph inflow into the heart (established background).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00003161 (adult ostial pore, a structure rather than a cell), broad class cardial cell. Contrast with the working cardiomyocyte cluster (non-ostia) from the same dataset.

## Mapping notes
The call implies a single ostium-specific cardial cell type, defined by position within the heart; the authors' term is a structure, so the cell identity is inferred from context. Adult data.
