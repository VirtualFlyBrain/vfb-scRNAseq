# pheromone-sensing neuron

*Author type `pheromone-sensing neuron` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x wing 1020, Smart-seq2 wing 11 (label transfer); author term: Manual Annotation. Wing sensory neurons annotated by function.*

## Summary
A sizeable wing cluster (1031 cells; 703 male, 317 female in 10x), broad class sensory neuron, annotated by the wing experts (Kristin Scott lab).

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Adult wing, where chemosensory bristles and other sensilla carry sensory neurons; the label does not specify which sensilla.

## Function
Detection of pheromones (as implied by the label).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term), broad class sensory neuron.

## Mapping notes
The call implies a single functionally defined neuron class (pheromone detection) in the wing, potentially a grouped call over several receptor-defined neurons. Adult data.
