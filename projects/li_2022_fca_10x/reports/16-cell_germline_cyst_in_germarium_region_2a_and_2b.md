# 16-cell germline cyst in germarium region 2a and 2b

*Author type `16-cell germline cyst in germarium region 2a and 2b` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x ovary 628 cells; Smart-seq2 ovary 58 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Female germline cells of the adult ovary at the 16-cell cyst stage in the germarium.*

## Summary
Germline cysts of 16 interconnected cells located in germarium regions 2a and 2b, the stage after four mitotic divisions of a cystoblast and before the cyst is enveloped by follicle cells and buds off as an egg chamber (region 3, stage 1). The authors' label is a developmental stage within oogenesis.

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the paper text or supplementary materials available to us (ovary annotation is shown only as a tSNE, fig. S18).

## Location
Adult ovary, anterior germarium. Found only in the ovary analysis (10x and Smart-seq2).

## Function
Cyst cells in this stage are committed to oogenesis: one of the 16 cells becomes the oocyte (meiotic) and the other 15 become polyploid nurse cells.

## Label provenance
The authors had no FBbt term (Manual Annotation); the broad class is female germline cell. Labels came from the Nystul/Rust ovary annotation group, with the ovary data integrated with three published ovary scRNA-seq datasets (fig. S37). Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
A stage-defined single germline population (16-cell cyst, regions 2a/2b), narrower than "female germline cell" but a stage rather than a cell type. Neighbouring author types (young germ cell, post-mitotic germ cell early 16-cell cyst, germ cell stage 4 and later) partition the germline lineage by stage. Adult (5 day) tissue; the cells are a developmental stage within the adult ovary.
