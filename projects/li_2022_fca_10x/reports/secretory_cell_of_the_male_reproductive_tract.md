# secretory cell of the male reproductive tract

*Author type `secretory cell of the male reproductive tract` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x male reproductive glands 250, 10x testis 117; Smart-seq2 male reproductive glands 5, testis 1; author term: Manual Annotation). Generic secretory cell group of the male tract, annotated in two tissues.*

## Summary
A broad secretory cell group, annotated in the male reproductive glands and in the testis analysis (which includes seminal vesicle region tissue, per the supplement). Broad class: male reproductive system.

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the supplementary text available here (figure S17 legend shows only the tSNE annotation); none are asserted.

## Location
Male reproductive glands (250) and testis (117) analyses; adult males (testis 0-1 d, glands 2-3 d).

## Function
Presumably secretory function in seminal fluid production; the paper does not detail this group. The paper does describe two distinct nuclear types among accessory gland main cells (fig. S17), but does not link them to this cluster.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id was Manual Annotation; broad class male reproductive system.

## Mapping notes
A generic, coarse type not tied to one organ; the authors' call may combine secretory cells from several parts of the tract (grouped call). Adult.
