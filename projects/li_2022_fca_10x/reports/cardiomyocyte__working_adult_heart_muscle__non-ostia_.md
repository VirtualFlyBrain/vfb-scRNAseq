# cardiomyocyte, working adult heart muscle (non-ostia)

*Author type `cardiomyocyte, working adult heart muscle (non-ostia)` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x heart 298, Smart-seq2 heart 5 (label transfer); author term: Manual Annotation. Contractile heart cardial cells.*

## Summary
The main cardial cell population of the heart dataset (303 cells), broad class cardial cell. The qualifier "non-ostia" separates it from the ostial cluster.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Adult heart (dorsal vessel, abdominal segments 1-6): the cardiomyocytes of the working myocardium, i.e. contractile muscle of the heart tube excluding the ostial cells.

## Function
Rhythmic contraction that pumps hemolymph (established background).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term), broad class cardial cell.

## Mapping notes
The call implies one cardiomyocyte type defined by exclusion of the ostial cells (a working myocardium group). Adult data.
