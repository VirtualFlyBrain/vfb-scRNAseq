# cyst stem cell

*Author type `cyst stem cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 344 cells, Smart-seq2 testis 3 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. Somatic stem cells of the testis (CySCs) at the root of the cyst cell lineage.*

## Summary
The CySCs are the somatic stem cells of the testis; the authors describe them as expressing the cell-cycle marker string and use them as the start of the cyst cell trajectory. The paper's testis case study describes the somatic lineage as CySCs expressing the cell-cycle marker string, transitioning into post-mitotic (string-negative) early cyst cells, then branching into two related clusters of cyst cells likely associated with spermatocytes (Fig. 6G). The supplement's trajectory methods used cyst stem cell, early cyst cell and the two spermatocyte cyst cell branches clusters, with cyst stem cell as the start.

## Markers
string (cell-cycle marker) is the only gene the paper names for this cluster. Other well-known CySC markers (e.g. Zfh1, Tj) are general knowledge and are not stated in the supplement text consulted.

## Location
Adult testis; CySCs sit at the apical tip around the germinal proliferation center hub and divide to self-renew and produce early cyst cells.

## Function
Somatic stem cells of the testis; their daughters enclose germline daughters and support spermatogonial and spermatocyte differentiation (general knowledge).

## Label provenance
No FBbt term was assigned (author ontology id: Manual Annotation); broad class: somatic precursor cell, unlike the other cyst cell types, which carry male reproductive system.

## Mapping notes
Single, well-defined stem cell type (CySC), annotated with a stem-cell-like broad class. The cells are adult (0-1 day old male testis); the label names a lineage state, not a developmental stage.
