# pericerebral adult fat mass

*Author type `pericerebral adult fat mass` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x head 198 cells; author term: FBbt adult pericerebral fat mass. Fat cells of the head, surrounding the brain.*

## Summary
A fat-body cell population found only in the whole-head 10x dataset (198 cells; 110 female, 78 male, 10 mixed). The authors' broad class is fat cell.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Adult head; the pericerebral fat is the part of the adult fat body that surrounds the brain. Fat body proper was separately profiled by FACS of Cg-GAL4 > UAS-lamGFP nuclei, but this type was called only in the head sample.

## Function
Adipose tissue; as a fat body depot it has storage and metabolic roles (established background, not specific to this dataset).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00048409 (adult pericerebral fat mass), broad class fat cell. The label is a tissue-part name applied to the nuclei, i.e. the fat cells composing that structure.

## Mapping notes
The call implies the fat cells of a specific head depot (single, location-defined type), not generic fat body cells. Adult data (5-day adults).
