# peripheral glial cell

*Author type `peripheral glial cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x leg 450, proboscis_and_maxillary_palps 635, wing 649 cells; ss2 leg 19, proboscis_and_maxillary_palps 18, wing 17; author term: FBbt peripheral glial cell (now obsolete, replaced by PNS glial cell)). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Glia of peripheral nerves.*

## Summary
Glia of the peripheral nervous system in the leg, wing and proboscis/maxillary palps: 1788 nuclei (10x) plus 54 classifier-typed Smart-seq2 cells. They wrap and support peripheral nerves and sensory neuron axons outside the CNS.

## Markers
No markers given for this type in the paper; glial class from the broad annotation.

## Location
Leg (450), wing (649), proboscis and palps (635), with small Smart-seq2 sets.

## Function
Ensheath peripheral neurites and some sit at nerve branch points (FBbt definition).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: the FBbt peripheral glial cell term, now obsolete and replaced by PNS glial cell; broad class: glial cell.

## Mapping notes
Single broad type (peripheral glial cell) pooled across several appendages, not subdivided into wrapping, subperineurial or perineurial glia. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
