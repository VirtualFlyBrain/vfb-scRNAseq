# arista and sacculus thermosensory and hygrosensory neuron Ir21a, Ir40a, Gr28b

*Author type `arista and sacculus thermosensory and hygrosensory neuron Ir21a, Ir40a, Gr28b` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 2; author term: Manual Annotation). Tiny antenna cluster of arista/sacculus thermo- and hygrosensory neurons.*

## Summary
Thermosensory and hygrosensory neurons of the arista and sacculus, named by Ir21a, Ir40a and Gr28b. Ir21a and Gr28b are associated with cool sensing and Ir40a with humidity sensing in the sacculus/arista.

## Markers
Ir21a, Ir40a, Gr28b (from the label). Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Arista and sacculus of the antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Temperature and humidity detection rather than olfaction.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call of thermo- and hygrosensory neuron classes; only 2 nuclei, so very low support; adult.
