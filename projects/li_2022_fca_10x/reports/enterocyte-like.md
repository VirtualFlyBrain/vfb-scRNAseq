# enterocyte-like

*Author type `enterocyte-like` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 985, Smart-seq2 gut 42; author term: Manual Annotation). Enterocyte-like epithelial group, no ontology term.*

## Summary
Gut epithelial cells resembling enterocytes, the absorptive midgut cells.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Dissected gut sample; the specific region is not given in the sources.

## Function
Enterocytes are the main absorptive and digestive cells of the midgut; 'like' means the authors consider the cells similar to enterocytes but not assignable to a defined enterocyte subtype.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (the authors had no FBbt term); authors' broad class: epithelial cell. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Hedged/approximate cell-type call ('enterocyte-like'); likely a remainder-style group of enterocyte-resembling cells. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
