# adult hindgut*

*Author type `adult hindgut*` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x Malpighian tubule 28, Smart-seq2 Malpighian tubule 2; author term: Manual Annotation). Asterisked label; region-named group.*

## Summary
Epithelial cluster in the Malpighian tubule dissection labelled hindgut, probably hindgut tissue attached to the dissected tubules.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Malpighian tubule dissection analysis. Tubules join the gut at the midgut/hindgut junction, so adjacent gut cells are plausibly co-dissected (inference).

## Function
Hindgut epithelium; no cluster-specific function given.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (the authors had no FBbt term); authors' broad class: epithelial cell. The asterisk is not explained in the paper text or supplementary methods and legends I searched; it appears only in the Malpighian tubule analysis labels. The supplement says only that Drip-GAL4>UAS-nlsGFP was used to label stellate cells in this tissue and that stellate cells were readily annotated from tsh, SecCl and Drip. The asterisked labels may flag provisional or non-tubule-intrinsic calls, but that is an inference, not stated. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Region-named group. Its unasterisked counterpart (adult hindgut) comes from body and tubule analyses with an ontology term, whereas this one has none. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
