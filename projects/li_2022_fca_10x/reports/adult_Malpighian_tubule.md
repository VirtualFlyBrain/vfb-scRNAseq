The label names the excretory organ. The cells are most plausibly tubule cells carried over into the gut and heart dissections.*

## Summary
Small group (73 cells) labelled with the whole organ, the Malpighian tubule, rather than a cell type; it occurs in the gut and heart tissue analyses, not the dedicated Malpighian tubule analysis.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Dissected gut (37 cells, plus 1 Smart-seq2) and heart (35 cells) samples. The gut sample retained the midgut/hindgut junction where the tubules join, so tubule fragments are expected there; the heart origin is not explained by the sources.

## Function
Malpighian tubules are the fly's excretory/osmoregulatory epithelium (principal, stellate and other cells). The authors group the cells under the organ name; no finer type is given.
