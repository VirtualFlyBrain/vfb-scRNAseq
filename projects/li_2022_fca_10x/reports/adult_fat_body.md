# adult fat body

*Author type `adult fat body` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x fat_body 16950, body 3169, oenocyte 2621, body_wall 1267, heart 830, haltere 346, proboscis/maxillary palps 293, head 103, and smaller counts in 6 other tissues (26862 cells in total); Smart-seq2 (label transfer) fat_body 880 plus small numbers elsewhere; author term: FBbt adult fat body. Adipocytes of the adult fat body.*

## Summary
The main fat cell population of the atlas, the dominant cell type of the FACS-enriched fat body sample (Cg-GAL4 > UAS-lamGFP nuclei; Heinrich Jasper lab) and recovered as a contaminating or co-dissected population in many other tissue datasets (body, oenocyte, body wall, heart, haltere, etc.). Broad class: fat cell.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
Adipose tissue distributed in unconnected depots throughout the adult body; present in nearly all dissections because of fat attached to other organs.

## Function
Metabolic storage and endocrine tissue, and a source of immune effectors (established background).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00003209 (adult fat body), broad class fat cell. The term is an organ/tissue name used for its cells.

## Mapping notes
The call implies the generic adult fat cell (adipocyte) of the fat body as a whole; distinct from the head-specific pericerebral fat label, which the authors keep separate. Adult data. Some Smart-seq2 entries are classifier-transferred.
