# anterior ejaculatory duct

*Author type `anterior ejaculatory duct` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x male reproductive glands 550 cells; Smart-seq2 male reproductive glands 17 cells; author term: FBbt:00004967 anterior ejaculatory duct). Epithelial cluster of the male reproductive tract.*

## Summary
Cells annotated as the anterior ejaculatory duct, an organ-level label. Given the broad class epithelial cell, the cluster consists of the duct's epithelial cells.

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the supplementary text available here (figure S17 legend shows only the tSNE annotation); none are asserted.

## Location
Dissected male accessory glands, ejaculatory ducts and ejaculatory bulbs (fig. S17); adult males, 5 days.

## Function
The anterior ejaculatory duct links the vasa deferentia and accessory glands to the ejaculatory bulb; its wall is a single layer of epithelial cells with a thin chitinous intima (FBbt definition of the structure). Its cells transport and condition seminal fluid; the paper gives no specific functional analysis.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00004967 (anterior ejaculatory duct); broad class epithelial cell. The Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
The authors' call is a single anatomical-organ label applied to a cell cluster, implying the epithelial cells of that duct (one type, organ-named). Adult stage.
