# adult heart

*Author type `adult heart` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x oenocyte 20 cells; author term: FBbt adult heart. Cardial cells recovered in the oenocyte dataset.*

## Summary
A very small group (20 cells; 8 female, 12 male) annotated only in the oenocyte analysis (PromE800-GAL4 > UAS-unc84GFP sorted nuclei), with broad class cardial cell. It is presumably heart tissue adhering to the dorsal abdominal cuticle with oenocytes; the authors do not discuss it.

## Markers
The supplementary text available to us gives no marker genes for this type; its identity rests on the authors' tissue-level expert annotation (fig. S6-S18 legends show only annotated tSNE plots).

## Location
The adult heart is the caudal, pulsatile region of the dorsal vessel (abdominal segments 1-6), lying under the dorsal cuticle close to oenocytes. The dedicated heart dataset (Rolf Bodmer lab) uses finer labels (ostium, working cardiomyocyte) that are separate types here.

## Function
Pumps hemolymph (established background).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id FBbt:00003154 (adult heart), broad class cardial cell. An organ name applied to cardial cells.

## Mapping notes
The call implies heart cardial cells of unspecified sub-type, labelled with the organ term; coarser than the heart-dataset types. Adult data.
