# adult olfactory receptor neuron Or22a, Or42b, Or59b

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron Or22a, Or42b, Or59b` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 669, Smart-seq2 antenna 9; author term: Manual Annotation). Antennal ORN cluster grouping Or22a, Or42b and Or59b neurons.*

## Summary
Odorant-receptor ORNs expressing Or22a, Or42b or Or59b. These are established antennal basiconic-sensillum ORN classes (Or22a in ab3, Or42b in ab1, Or59b in ab2).

## Markers
Or22a, Or42b, Or59b (from the label); Orco is expected as the co-receptor. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna (basiconic sensilla). Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Olfactory detection of volatile odorants; each receptor defines a distinct ORN class projecting to its own glomerulus.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call of three named ORN classes sharing a cluster; adult.
