# lamina intrinsic amacrine neuron Lai

*Author type `lamina intrinsic amacrine neuron Lai` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 368 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Optic lobe lamina neurons identified from the brain/head integration.*

## Summary
368 head neurons labelled as Lai, the lamina intrinsic amacrine neuron, an interneuron class of the lamina; the authors had no FBbt term for it. The paper identifies lamina cell types (e.g. L1-L5) among many optic lobe types via the Ozel et al. classifier.

## Markers
No marker genes for this type are given in the paper text or supplement; its identity rests on the authors' integrated annotation of the head with the Davie et al. 2018 brain data (annotation transfer to clusters, the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier, and an SVM classifier, followed by manual curation).

## Location
Head only (368 cells; 174 female, 188 male, 6 mixed). Lamina.

## Function
Lai neurons are lamina intrinsic neurons with processes confined to the lamina (general knowledge; not described in the paper).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (no FBbt term); broad class: neuron. Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Single named cell type (Lai) with no author-linked FBbt term. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
