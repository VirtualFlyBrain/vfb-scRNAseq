# adult lamina epithelial/marginal glial cell

*Author type `adult lamina epithelial/marginal glial cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 984 cells; author term: FBbt adult lamina marginal glial cell and adult lamina epithelial glial cell). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Lamina glia of the optic lobe.*

## Summary
984 head nuclei of the glia of the lamina (first optic neuropil): epithelial glia (astrocyte-like, wrapping photoreceptor terminals and lamina cartridges) and marginal glia (a layer at the proximal lamina surface). The two are pooled in one cluster.

## Markers
The paper names repo (pan-glial) and Optix as markers of lamina glia, which it notes were detected only in the head sample.

## Location
Head only (984 cells; 513 female, 434 male, 37 mixed). Lamina, beneath the retina and basement membrane, at the lamina plexus (epithelial) and proximal lamina surface (marginal).

## Function
Epithelial glia wrap the R1-R6 photoreceptor axon terminals in lamina cartridges and receive photoreceptor input; marginal glia ensheath proximal cartridges and form a boundary at the outer chiasm (FBbt definitions, citing Edwards et al. 2012; Kremer et al. 2017).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology ids: adult lamina marginal glial cell and adult lamina epithelial glial cell (two terms); broad class: glial cell. Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Grouped call of two lamina glial types (common parent: lamina glial cell / adult glial cell), not resolved to either. The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
