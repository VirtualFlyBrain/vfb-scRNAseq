# btl-GAL4 positive female cell, cluster 1, likely to be ovary cell

*Author type `btl-GAL4 positive female cell, cluster 1, likely to be ovary cell` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x:trachea 4598 cells, ss2:trachea 155 cells; author term: Manual Annotation). Female cells of unclear origin in the btl-GAL4 trachea sample, attributed by the authors to the ovary.*

## Summary
A group of female cells recovered in the trachea sample that the authors judged likely to be ovarian cells rather than tracheal cells. The largest of the groups; the Smart-seq2 members include 71 male cells (84 female), so the classifier-transferred label is not strictly female-only there.

## Markers
No marker genes are given in the supplementary text for this group beyond the label itself. The Smart-seq2 transfer assigned some male cells to this label (71 of 155).

## Location
Not a tracheal type. The trachea sample was btl-GAL4, tub-Gal80ts > UAS-lamGFP adult flies (shifted from 18 C to 29 C for 5 days), nuclei enriched by GFP FACS. The authors infer an ovarian origin for these female cells (the label says 'likely').

## Function
Unknown; no function is described. It is an uncertain provenance call, not a defined cell type.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation; broad class: female reproductive system. Smart-seq2 cells (where present) were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation. The paper's supplement notes only the btl-GAL4 sample design and gives no further justification here.

## Mapping notes
Uncertain call ('likely to be ovary cell') with no specific cell type; at most a female reproductive system / ovary origin, not a follicle cell type. Anonymous cluster label. The data are adult.
