# cardia (2)

*Author type `cardia (2)` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x gut 298, Smart-seq2 gut 20; author term: Manual Annotation). Region-named group; two numbered clusters share the organ name.*

## Summary
One of two gut clusters the authors label 'cardia' (the numbered duplicates are separate author clusters); epithelial cells.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Gut analysis: the FCA gut sample included the midgut/hindgut junction (where the Malpighian tubules join) and the crop, which the earlier Hung et al. gut dataset had removed (supplement, fig. S36 legend). The cardia (proventriculus) lies at the foregut-midgut junction.

## Function
The cardia produces the peritrophic matrix; the sources give no cluster-specific functional detail.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: Manual Annotation (the authors had no FBbt term); authors' broad class: epithelial cell. Numbering (1)/(2) separates two clusters with the same name; the authors do not describe how they differ. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Region-named group: epithelial cells of the cardia region, split into two author clusters (maybe different cardia cell populations; unstated). Must stay distinct rows. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
