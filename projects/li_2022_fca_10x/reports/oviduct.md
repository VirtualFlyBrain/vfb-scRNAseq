# oviduct

*Author type `oviduct` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x ovary 580 cells; Smart-seq2 ovary 7 cells; author term: FBbt:00004911 oviduct). Cells of the female oviduct captured in the ovary dissection.*

## Summary
Cells of the oviduct, the duct that connects the ovaries to the uterus (lateral oviducts joining a common oviduct), co-dissected with the ovary. The authors' broad class is epithelial cell, so these nuclei are presumably the oviduct epithelium, although the authors do not subdivide it.

## Markers
No marker genes are reported for this type in the paper text or supplementary materials we could access.

## Location
Female reproductive tract, attached to the ovary and carried over in the ovary dissection. Found only in the ovary analysis; all cells are female.

## Function
Passage of eggs from the ovary toward the uterus; the oviduct epithelium and associated muscle contribute to egg transport (general knowledge, not from the paper).

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt oviduct (an anatomical structure term, not a cell type); broad class: epithelial cell. Smart-seq2 cells were classifier-assigned from the 10x labels.

## Mapping notes
The authors' call is a tissue-level (anatomical part) term applied to a cell cluster, qualified by an epithelial broad class; it is a single grouped call (oviduct cells) rather than a specific cell type. Adult data.
