# early cyst cell 2

*Author type `early cyst cell 2` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas); 10x testis 353 cells; author term: Manual Annotation. Early post-mitotic cyst cells, second of two early clusters.*

## Summary
Second early cyst cell cluster; post-mitotic (string-negative) per the paper's lineage description. The paper's testis case study describes the somatic lineage as CySCs expressing the cell-cycle marker string, transitioning into post-mitotic (string-negative) early cyst cells, then branching into two related clusters of cyst cells likely associated with spermatocytes (Fig. 6G). The supplement's trajectory methods used cyst stem cell, early cyst cell and the two spermatocyte cyst cell branches clusters, with cyst stem cell as the start.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given in the paper or supplement text for this type.

## Location
Adult testis (plus seminal vesicle) sample from 0-1 day old males. Cyst cells are the somatic support cells that enclose and nurture germline cells during spermatogenesis, and are derived from cyst stem cells (CySCs) at the testis apical tip.

## Function
Cyst cells ensheath germ cell cysts and support their differentiation (general Drosophila knowledge, not from the paper). Early cyst cells enclose newly formed germ cell cysts (spermatogonia).

## Label provenance
The authors had no FBbt term for this label (author ontology id: Manual Annotation); broad class: male reproductive system. The 1/2 split is the authors' clustering; the paper does not say how the two differ.

## Mapping notes
Stage-bin of the cyst cell lineage; sub-cluster 2 of the early cyst cell state. The cells are adult (0-1 day old male testis); the label names a lineage state, not a developmental stage.
