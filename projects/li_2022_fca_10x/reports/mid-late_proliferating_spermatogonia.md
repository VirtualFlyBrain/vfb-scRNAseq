# mid-late proliferating spermatogonia

*Author type `mid-late proliferating spermatogonia` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x testis 401, Smart-seq2 testis 5; author term: Manual Annotation). Mitotically amplifying male germ cells of the adult testis.*

## Summary
Spermatogonia are the transit-amplifying germ cells derived from germline stem cells, dividing synchronously before entering the spermatocyte stage. This cluster is the mid-to-late part of that proliferating stage, annotated by the testis experts (Fuller lab and colleagues) in the testis case study.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker list is given in the paper or supplement text. The authors' trajectory analysis starts from "spermatogonium" and lists the stage order GSC, proliferating spermatogonia, spermatocytes, spermatids.

## Location
Testis of 0-1 day old adult males; apical-to-mid testis region in anatomical terms (general knowledge, not stated in the paper).

## Function
Transit-amplifying mitotic divisions of the male germline lineage.

## Label provenance
No authors' ontology term (Manual Annotation); broad class male germline cell. Smart-seq2 cells were classifier-assigned from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
An author-defined stage-bin of the germline lineage (the "mid-late" qualifier splits the proliferating spermatogonia state). Data are adult. No authors' FBbt term to anchor to.
