# sacculus/arista neuron

*Author type `sacculus/arista neuron` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 455, Smart-seq2 antenna 3; author term: Manual Annotation). Sensory neurons of the sacculus or arista.*

## Summary
Neurons of the sacculus and arista, annotated with a general neuron class (not sensory neuron) by the authors, and without receptor detail.

## Markers
No markers named in the label. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Sacculus and arista of the antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Likely thermo/hygro/mechanosensory or related, not stated by the authors.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call across two antennal compartments, coarse; adult.
