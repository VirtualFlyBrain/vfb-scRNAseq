# adult olfactory receptor neuron Or85a, Or43b

*Author type `adult olfactory receptor neuron Or85a, Or43b` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x antenna 161, Smart-seq2 antenna 1; author term: Manual Annotation). Antennal ORN cluster grouping Or85a and Or43b neurons.*

## Summary
Odorant-receptor ORNs expressing Or85a or Or43b, two known antennal ORN classes; the authors group them in one cluster.

## Markers
Or85a, Or43b (from the label); Orco expected. Table S2 not available here.

## Location
Antenna. Cells were dissected from adult (5-day) fly antennae and profiled by single-nucleus RNA-seq; Smart-seq2 cells, where present, were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Function
Olfactory.

## Label provenance
The label is a Fly Cell Atlas jamboree annotation by tissue experts; the authors had no FBbt term for it (Manual Annotation), and the broad class is sensory neuron. ORN types in the atlas are named by the receptors they express, since one receptor can specify one ORN type. Smart-seq2 labels come from label transfer from the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Granularity implied: grouped call of two named ORN classes; adult.
