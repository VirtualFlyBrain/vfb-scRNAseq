# adult hindgut

*Author type `adult hindgut` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x body 348, 10x Malpighian tubule 38; author term: FBbt:00003143 adult hindgut). Region/organ-named group.*

## Summary
Hindgut epithelial cells, mostly from the whole-body analysis.

## Markers
The sources available (type evidence, per-cluster annotations, paper text and supplementary methods/legends) list no marker genes for this type; the authors' annotation rests on tissue-expert consensus voting plus a transfer of labels in the tissue analysis.

## Location
Whole-body sample (348 cells) and the Malpighian tubule dissection (38 cells); the hindgut is the posterior alimentary canal reaching from the tubule junction to the rectum.

## Function
Hindgut epithelium (reabsorption and waste handling in general terms); no cluster-specific detail in the sources.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology id: FBbt:00003143 adult hindgut; authors' broad class: epithelial cell. Distinct from the asterisked 'adult hindgut*' cluster and from the gut-analysis pylorus type. Cell types were annotated by tissue experts with consensus voting; Smart-seq2 cells were typed by a classifier trained on the 10x annotation.

## Mapping notes
Organ-named group: a grouped call over the hindgut's epithelial cells, not a single cell type. The data are from 5-day-old adult flies.
