# T neuron T4/T5c-d

*Author type `T neuron T4/T5c-d` in Li et al. 2022 (Fly Cell Atlas; 10x head 734 cells; author term: FBbt T4c, T4d, T5c, T5d neurons). Single-nucleus RNA-seq of dissected adult Drosophila tissues. Subcluster of T4/T5 neurons.*

## Summary
734 head nuclei forming the c-d subcluster of T4/T5 neurons: T4c, T4d, T5c and T5d, the subtypes with lobula plate arbors in layers 3 and 4. T4/T5 motion-detecting neurons of the optic lobe (medulla/lobula to lobula plate).

## Markers
acj6 and SoxN mark T4/T5 neurons overall (paper text); the paper gives no markers that separate c-d from a-b.

## Location
Head only (734 cells; 336 female, 309 male, 89 mixed). T4c/T5c arborise in lobula plate layer 3 and T4d/T5d in layer 4.

## Function
Layer 3 subtypes respond to upward and layer 4 subtypes to downward motion (FBbt definitions, citing Henning et al. 2022); cholinergic.

## Label provenance
Authors' ontology ids: T4c, T4d, T5c, T5d neurons (four terms); broad class: neuron. Subclustering of the T4/T5 group; Annotation is in the head 10x (Stringent) analysis, where the authors integrated head nuclei with Davie et al. (2018) brain scRNA-seq, transferred labels, applied the Ozel et al. optic lobe classifier and manually curated (supplement, Brain-Head integration).

## Mapping notes
Grouped call: four subtypes (T4 and T5, layers 3-4) pooled, common parent T neuron (or a combination of the four). The data are adult (5-day-old adult flies; mixed sexes).
