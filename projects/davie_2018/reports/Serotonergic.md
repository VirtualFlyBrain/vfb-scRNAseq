# Serotonergic

*Cluster `Serotonergic` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 38; 391 cells; ages 0-50 days)
in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A monoamine-defined
neurotransmitter class, not a single morphological cell type.*

## Summary

Cells identified as serotonergic (5-HT-producing) neurons of the adult central
brain, annotated by literature markers directly at Seurat resolution 2.0
(Couch et al., 2004). Serotonergic neurons in the fly brain comprise many
morphologically and functionally distinct types (e.g. contralaterally-
projecting serotonin-immunoreactive neurons, median bundle neurons, and
neurons innervating the mushroom body, antennal lobe and optic lobe), which
this atlas does not further resolve.

## Markers

Top res-2.0 cluster 38 markers: Trh (tryptophan hydroxylase, serotonin
synthesis), SerT (serotonin transporter), Vmat (vesicular monoamine
transporter), Ddc (dopa decarboxylase, also required for 5-HT synthesis),
JhI-21, Pu, 7B2, IA-2, Pcd - a coherent serotonergic/aminergic secretory
signature.

## Location

Serotonergic neurons are distributed across many central-brain regions
(protocerebrum, subesophageal zone, antennal lobe, mushroom body, optic
lobe); no single neuropil location applies to the whole cluster.

## Function

Serotonin (5-HT) neurotransmission; modulates diverse circuits including
sleep/arousal, feeding, aggression, and sensory processing.

## Label provenance

Table S2 res-2.0 annotation for cluster 38 is "Serotonergic" (method:
literature markers; ref Couch et al., 2004) - a direct match to the GEO
label, no REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

This is a merged/class-level cluster: it groups together the many
transcriptomically-similar but anatomically distinct serotonergic neuron
types of the adult brain under one neurotransmitter identity. The honest
mapping level is a broad "serotonergic neuron" class term, not any single
named morphological type; no evidence here resolves individual serotonergic
subtypes.
