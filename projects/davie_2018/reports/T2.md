# T2

*Cluster `T2` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 31; 507 cells; ages 0-50 days) in Davie
et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). Optic-lobe columnar/tangential
neuron; label provenance is genuinely mixed with T3, see below.*

## Summary

T2 is one of two GEO labels (T2, T3) split out of clusters that the authors'
own Table S2 handles inconsistently: res-2.0 cluster 31 (this cluster) is
annotated in Table S2 itself as the *merged* identity "T2/T3", identified by
mapping to the Konstantinides et al. (2018) optic-lobe transcriptomic atlas,
not as T2 alone.

## Markers

Top Table S3 markers for res-2.0 cluster 31: sosie, nAChRalpha6, CG9896,
beat-Ic, acj6, beat-Ib, CG14142, beat-Ia, Tk, Pde1c, dpr1, Cbp53E, scro,
Atox1, beat-IIIc, CG13685, trv. Several beat-family adhesion genes and Tk
(Tachykinin) are shared with cluster 33 (GEO's "T3"), consistent with the two
being transcriptionally close optic-lobe neuron types distinguished mainly by
mapping, not by markers unique to this dataset.

## Location

T2 and T3 are established central/proximal medulla columnar or tangential
neuron types of the optic lobe (classical Golgi nomenclature; Fischbach and
Dittrich, 1989), less thoroughly characterized than T4/T5.

## Function

Not characterized in this atlas beyond identity assignment; general
optic-lobe visual-processing role inferred from classical anatomy only.

## Label provenance

REVIEW: Table S2's own res-2.0 annotation for cluster 31 is the merged label
"T2/T3" (method: mapping; ref Konstantinides et al., 2018), not "T2". GEO
splits this into two separate labels — T2 for cluster 31 and T3 for cluster
33 — but cluster 33 is separately marked "Unannotated" in Table S2's res-2.0
table. So the GEO T2/T3 split does not line up cleanly with Table S2's own
cluster-level annotations: cluster 31 carries S2's merged T2/T3 evidence, and
cluster 33's T3 label has no S2 res-2.0 support at all (label_validation.tsv
flags both rows REVIEW).

## Mapping notes

Given the S2 table itself only supports a merged "T2/T3" identity for this
cluster, the honest evidence-based granularity here is a merged pair of
related types rather than T2 specifically resolved. A common parent term
(covering T2 and T3) may be the safer level unless independent evidence
supports splitting on cluster identity (31 vs 33) alone.
