# TmY14

*Cluster `TmY14` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 11; 1,399 cells; ages 0-50 days) in
Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A large "new" optic-lobe
cluster identified by mapping to a prior scRNA-seq atlas.*

## Summary

TmY14 is one of the clusters the authors list as newly annotated in this
study via regression-mapping against the Konstantinides et al. (2018)
optic-lobe scRNA-seq/connectome atlas, rather than by literature marker
genes. It is a large, cleanly matched cluster with no ambiguity flagged, but
its anatomical/functional characterization rests on that external atlas
rather than classical Golgi/EM studies.

## Markers

Table S2 annotation "TmY14" (method: mapping; ref Konstantinides et al.,
2018). Top Table S3 markers for res-2.0 cluster 11: D, VGlut, Sox21b,
CG45781, Nplp1, CG34355, CG32017, CG11294, kn, beat-Ic, cpx, DIP-eta, Snap25.
VGlut expression indicates a glutamatergic identity.

## Location

TmY neurons are transmedullary-Y type columnar neurons of the optic lobe,
with processes spanning both the medulla and lobula (the "Y" branch
morphology distinguishing them from simple Tm neurons). Beyond this general
class-level anatomy, no cluster-specific layer/lamina detail is available
from the sources used here.

## Function

Not characterized functionally in this atlas beyond glutamatergic
neurotransmitter identity; specific circuit role is not established from the
evidence available here.

## Label provenance

Table S2 and GEO agree: both annotate res-2.0 cluster 11 as "TmY14"
(label_validation.tsv: "match"). No REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

Presented as a single resolved cell type, but identified purely by
cross-dataset transcriptomic mapping (no independent literature markers),
so confidence in the specific "TmY14" identity depends on the reliability of
that external atlas correspondence rather than this paper's own evidence. An
adult-specific, single-type FBbt term for TmY14 would be the expected
target, with a note that the identification method is mapping-only.
