# adPN

*Cluster `adPN` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 27; 107 cells; ages 0-50 days) in
Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). Anterodorsal-lineage olfactory
projection neurons, resolved to lineage but not to a Li et al. 2017 marker
subgroup.*

## Summary

adPNs (anterodorsal projection neurons) are one of the two/three developmental
lineages of antennal-lobe olfactory projection neurons in Drosophila, derived
from the anterodorsal neuroblast and mostly uniglomerular, projecting via the
inner antennocerebral tract (iACT) to the mushroom body calyx and lateral
horn (Jefferis et al., 2001; Komiyama and Luo, 2007). Within res-2.0 cluster
27 (543 OPN cells, subclustered per Figure 2H against Li et al. 2017's
SMART-seq2 adPN/lPN reference), this label captures cells assigned to the
adPN lineage but not further resolved into any of the C15+/kn+ marker
combinations reported in Table S2.

## Markers

- Table S3 top markers are for the parent res-2.0 cluster 27 (not specific to
 the adPN subgroup): otp, ST6Gal, hth, Oaz, CG43729, caup, acj6, mirr, C15,
 Drep3, CR32111, ara, mbl, cpo, tup, tnc, kn, CG7708, ChAT, VAChT.
- tup, otp, hth and caup are established adPN/lPN lineage markers (Komiyama
 and Luo, 2007); C15 and kn are the finer markers used by Li et al. (2017)
 to split adPNs into subgroups, but are not used to define this label.
- Cholinergic (ChAT/VAChT), consistent with most PN classes.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** antennal lobe glomeruli (dendrites, uniglomerular in most
 adPNs); axons via iACT to mushroom body calyx and lateral horn.
- **Cell body:** anterodorsal cell cluster of the antennal lobe.
- **Cell class:** olfactory projection neuron, anterodorsal lineage, class N.

## Function

- Uniglomerular relay of odor information from a single antennal-lobe
 glomerulus to the mushroom body (associative learning) and lateral horn
 (innate odor valence).

## Label provenance

Table S2 res-2.0 cluster 27 is annotated "OPN: adPN and lPN"
(literature markers/mapping; Komiyama and Luo, 2007; Komiyama et al., 2003;
Li et al., 2017). label_validation.tsv: match (curated synonym of S2 'OPN:
adPN and lPN'). No REVIEW flag. This label is a curator-assigned refinement
identifying the adPN lineage within cluster 27, one level more resolved than
the bare cluster-27 annotation but coarser than the marker-defined subgroups
(C15+, kn+, C15+kn+) also listed under Table S2.

## Mapping notes

adPNs comprise ~40 individual, mostly uniglomerular PN types (one per
glomerulus they innervate), each a genuine distinct anatomical/functional
type. This 107-cell label is a merged group at lineage resolution only - no
marker information narrows it to a specific glomerulus/type. The honest
mapping level is "anterodorsal olfactory projection neuron (lineage)," not
any individual glomerular PN type.
