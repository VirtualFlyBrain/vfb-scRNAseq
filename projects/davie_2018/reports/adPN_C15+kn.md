# adPN/C15&kn

*Cluster `adPN/C15&kn` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 27; 131 cells; ages 0-50 days)
in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). "C15(+) kn(+) adPNs" - a
marker-defined subgroup of anterodorsal olfactory projection neurons,
replicating a subcluster from Li et al. 2017 (Table S2).*

## Summary

Within res-2.0 cluster 27 (543 OPN cells), the authors subclustered adPNs and
lPNs against Li et al. 2017's SMART-seq2 reference and reproduced all of that
study's reported subclusters (Figure 2H). This label is the "C15(+) kn(+)
adPNs" subgroup: anterodorsal-lineage PNs co-expressing both the homeodomain
factor C15 and the COE/Collier-family transcription factor knot (kn), used
by Li et al. (2017) in combination to further subdivide adPNs beyond the
single-marker C15(+) group. At 131 cells, this is the largest of the five
Table S2 marker subgroups within cluster 27.

## Markers

- Defining markers: C15-positive AND kn-positive, within the adPN lineage.
- Table S3 top markers are for the parent res-2.0 cluster 27, not specific to
 this subcluster: otp, ST6Gal, hth, Oaz, CG43729, caup, acj6, mirr, C15,
 Drep3, CR32111, ara, mbl, cpo, tup, tnc, kn, CG7708, ChAT, VAChT. Both C15
 and kn appear in this parent list, consistent with their role in defining
 this two-marker subgroup.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** antennal lobe glomeruli (dendrites, uniglomerular); axons via
 iACT to mushroom body calyx and lateral horn.
- **Cell body:** anterodorsal cell cluster of the antennal lobe.
- **Cell class:** olfactory projection neuron, anterodorsal lineage,
 C15(+)/kn(+) subgroup, class N.

## Function

- Uniglomerular relay of odor information to mushroom body and lateral horn;
 no glomerulus-specific function is assigned by this atlas.

## Label provenance

Table S2 lists "C15(+) kn(+) adPNs" as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 27
("OPN: adPN and lPN"), by literature markers, referencing Li et al. (2017).
label_validation.tsv: match (curated synonym of S2 'C15(+) kn(+) adPNs'). No
REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

C15 and kn together form a combinatorial transcription-factor code used by Li
et al. (2017) to distinguish this adPN subgroup from the single-marker
C15(+) and kn(+) groups; it is a finer transcriptomic split, not
independently established here as corresponding to one single named
glomerular PN type. Li et al. (2017) note that only six of the many PN
classes they profiled were "unequivocally mapped" to known glomerulus/
function; whether this two-marker group is one of those six is not stated in
the Davie et al. text. Given its comparatively large size (131 cells, the
largest subgroup here), it may represent more than one closely related PN
type rather than a single glomerular class. Treat as a transcriptomic
subgroup of uncertain anatomical granularity; flag for the mapping step to
check Li et al. 2017 directly.
