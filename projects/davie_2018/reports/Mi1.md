# Mi1

*Cluster `Mi1` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 26; 562 cells; ages 0-50 days) in
Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). Classical medulla intrinsic
neuron of the ON motion pathway.*

## Summary

Mi1 is a well-characterized medulla columnar interneuron, identified in this
atlas cleanly by its established combinatorial markers, with no ambiguity
flagged.

## Markers

The paper identifies Mi1 neurons by co-expression of hth and bsh (Hasegawa
et al., 2011). Top Table S3 markers for res-2.0 cluster 26 include Rh7,
Pka-C3, Mmp2, Tk, Octbeta1R, bsh, hth, CG42541, Nckx30C, and kek1. The paper
also reports Mi1 has a distinct SCENIC regulatory network under the control
of the transcription factor Bsh (Hasegawa et al., 2011, 2013).

## Location

Mi1 is a medullary intrinsic ("Mi") columnar neuron with dendrites spanning
medulla layers M1, M5, and M9/M10, and cell body in the medulla cortex.

## Function

Mi1 is a core element of the ON-pathway motion-detection circuit,
postsynaptic to lamina neuron L1 and presynaptic to T4, relaying brightness
increment signals into the direction-selective motion circuit.
