# Octopaminergic

*Cluster `Octopaminergic` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 64, Tbh(+) subcluster; 76
cells; ages 0-50 days) in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A
subcluster of the parent Tdc2(+) "Octopaminergic-Tyraminergic" cluster,
resolved by Tbh.*

## Summary

The paper subclustered its res-2.0 Tdc2-positive cluster (64) into two
groups "representing the octopaminergic (Tbh positive) and tyraminergic
(Tbh negative) neurons." Tbh (tyramine beta-hydroxylase) converts tyramine
to octopamine, so Tbh(+) cells are the subset of Tdc2(+) aminergic neurons
that produce octopamine as end-product neurotransmitter. Octopaminergic
neurons in the fly brain are themselves a diverse group (e.g. ventral
unpaired median, ventral paired median and dorsal clusters) not further
resolved here.

## Markers

Top res-2.0 cluster 64 markers (parent-cluster level): Vmat, Tdc2, CG4577,
SIFa, Sox21a, Tbh, Lim1, CR44165, Imp, Nep2, Nep1, hig. Tbh is the specific
marker distinguishing this octopaminergic subcluster from the Tyraminergic
(Tbh-negative) one; markers listed are for the shared parent cluster, not
resolved to the Tbh(+) subcluster alone.

## Location

Octopaminergic neurons are broadly distributed, with clusters in the
subesophageal zone, ventral nerve cord-adjacent brain regions, and
projections into the antennal lobe, mushroom body and central complex; no
single neuropil applies to the whole group.

## Function

Octopamine neurotransmission/neuromodulation - implicated in arousal,
locomotion, aggression, appetitive reinforcement and stress responses (the
insect functional analogue of noradrenaline).

## Label provenance

Table S2 records this as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 64
("Octopaminergic-Tyraminergic"), annotated "Octopaminergic" by literature
markers; no reference given, no REVIEW flag. GEO label matches the S2
subcluster annotation directly.

## Mapping notes

This is a merged group at the neurotransmitter-class level: multiple
anatomically distinct octopaminergic neuron clusters are combined here with
no further subclustering. The honest mapping level is a general
"octopaminergic neuron" parent term, not a specific named octopaminergic
cluster/type.
