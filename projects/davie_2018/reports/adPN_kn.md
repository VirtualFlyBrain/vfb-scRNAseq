# adPN/kn

*Cluster `adPN/kn` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 27; 70 cells; ages 0-50 days) in
Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). "kn(+) adPNs" - a
marker-defined subgroup of anterodorsal olfactory projection neurons,
replicating a subcluster from Li et al. 2017 (Table S2).*

## Summary

Within res-2.0 cluster 27 (543 OPN cells), the authors subclustered adPNs and
lPNs against Li et al. 2017's SMART-seq2 reference and reproduced all of that
study's reported subclusters (Figure 2H). This label is the "kn(+) adPNs"
subgroup: anterodorsal-lineage PNs expressing the COE/Collier-family
transcription factor knot (kn) but not C15, distinguishing them from the
C15(+) and C15(+)kn(+) adPN subgroups also reported in Table S2 (Li et al.,
2017).

## Markers

- Defining marker: kn-positive, C15-negative, within the adPN lineage.
- Table S3 top markers are for the parent res-2.0 cluster 27, not specific to
 this subcluster: otp, ST6Gal, hth, Oaz, CG43729, caup, acj6, mirr, C15,
 Drep3, CR32111, ara, mbl, cpo, tup, tnc, kn, CG7708, ChAT, VAChT. kn appears
 in this parent list, consistent with its role in defining this subgroup.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** antennal lobe glomeruli (dendrites, uniglomerular); axons via
 iACT to mushroom body calyx and lateral horn.
- **Cell body:** anterodorsal cell cluster of the antennal lobe.
- **Cell class:** olfactory projection neuron, anterodorsal lineage, kn(+)
 subgroup, class N.

## Function

- Uniglomerular relay of odor information to mushroom body and lateral horn;
 no glomerulus-specific function is assigned by this atlas.

## Label provenance

Table S2 lists "kn(+) adPNs" as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 27 ("OPN:
adPN and lPN"), by literature markers, referencing Li et al. (2017).
label_validation.tsv: match (curated synonym of S2 'kn(+) adPNs'). No REVIEW
flag.

## Mapping notes

kn (knot/collier) is a combinatorial transcription-factor marker used by Li
et al. (2017) to subdivide adPNs; it is not, on the evidence available here,
independently established as marking one single named glomerular PN type.
Li et al. (2017) note only six of the many PN classes profiled were
"unequivocally mapped" to a known glomerulus/function, and it is not stated
whether kn(+) adPNs is one of these. Treat as a transcriptomic subgroup of
the adPN lineage of uncertain anatomical specificity (possibly a single
glomerular type, possibly several related types) rather than a confirmed
single named cell type; flag for the mapping step to check Li et al. 2017
directly.
