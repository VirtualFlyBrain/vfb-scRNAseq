# L3

*Cluster `L3` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 20; 101 cells; ages 0-50 days) in Davie
et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). Subcluster of the merged
L1/L2/L3/L4/L5 lamina-monopolar cluster, resolved to L3 by a regression-model
match to published bulk transcriptomes.*

## Summary

L3 is lamina monopolar neuron 3, a columnar interneuron of the lamina that
relays an achromatic, sustained (as opposed to L1/L2's transient) signal from
photoreceptors to the medulla, contributing to both motion-vision channels
and to the achromatic pathway feeding lobula circuits. The paper subclustered
the merged res-2.0 cluster 20 (the pooled "L1/L2/L3/L4/L5" population mapped
by regression to Tan et al. (2015) bulk transcriptomes) and used the same
regression model to assign this subcluster to L3 (paper main text,
Figure 2E-F).

## Markers

- Identified via regression-model similarity to the Tan et al. (2015) L3
  bulk transcriptome, not a single literature marker gene (unlike L1 and
  L4/L5, which were called from VGlut and bsh respectively).
- The Table S3 top markers listed for this cluster (CG18598, CG11000, Rbp6,
  shakB, Teh2, CG14509, CG42392, CG10947, CG9813, scro, CG31345, glob1, slp1,
  pdm3, AstA-R1, cpx, CG32647, slp2, para, CR45621) are for the **parent**
  res-2.0 cluster 20 (the pooled L1-L5 population), not specific to the L3
  subcluster.

## Location

- **Cell class:** lamina monopolar columnar interneuron (class N).
- **Soma/dendrites:** lamina cortex and cartridges; **axon:** projects to the
  medulla, terminating in layer M3.

## Function

- Receives histaminergic input from outer photoreceptors R1-R6 in the lamina
  cartridge; relays a sustained, achromatic signal to medulla layer M3,
  contributing input to both the L1/L2 motion-vision channels and downstream
  lobula circuits.

## Label provenance

Table S2 res-2.0 cluster 20 is annotated "L1/L2/L3/L4/L5" (method: mapping,
ref: Tan et al., 2015) — a merged pool, not subtype-resolved at res-2.0. The
GEO "L3" label corresponds to a subcluster of this pool identified in the
paper's Figure 2E/F subclustering by regression-model mapping to Tan et al.
(2015); label_validation.tsv records this as "subcluster/res-8.0 of S2
res-2.0 'L1/L2/L3/L4/L5'", no REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

L3 is a well-established, singular Drosophila cell type; an adult-specific
FBbt term for "lamina monopolar neuron L3" is expected and this cluster
should map to it exactly. Caveat: as with L2, the L3 call rests purely on
regression-model similarity rather than a literature marker gene, and the
only marker evidence in hand (Table S3) is for the undifferentiated parent
cluster — treat the subtype assignment itself as slightly less directly
evidenced, though the identity is anatomically uncontroversial.
