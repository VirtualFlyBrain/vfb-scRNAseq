# Tyraminergic

*Cluster `Tyraminergic` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 64, Tbh(-) subcluster; 122
cells; ages 0-50 days) in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A
subcluster of the parent Tdc2(+) "Octopaminergic-Tyraminergic" cluster,
resolved by Tbh.*

## Summary

The paper subclustered its res-2.0 Tdc2-positive cluster (64) into
octopaminergic (Tbh+) and tyraminergic (Tbh-) subsets. Tbh (tyramine
beta-hydroxylase) converts tyramine to octopamine; cells lacking Tbh
expression retain tyramine as their end-product neurotransmitter rather than
converting it further, defining the tyraminergic population. As with the
octopaminergic sister subcluster, tyraminergic neurons are a heterogeneous
anatomical group not further resolved here.

## Markers

Top res-2.0 cluster 64 markers (parent-cluster level): Vmat, Tdc2, CG4577,
SIFa, Sox21a, Tbh, Lim1, CR44165, Imp, Nep2, Nep1, hig. Tdc2 (tyrosine
decarboxylase 2, tyramine synthesis) is expressed by the whole parent
cluster; the absence of Tbh expression is what distinguishes this
subcluster from Octopaminergic. Markers listed are for the shared parent
cluster, not resolved to the Tbh(-) subcluster alone.

## Location

Tyraminergic neurons overlap substantially in distribution with
octopaminergic neurons (they share the Tdc2+ lineage), including
subesophageal zone and central-brain clusters; no single neuropil applies to
the whole group.

## Function

Tyramine neurotransmission/neuromodulation - implicated in olfactory
modulation, locomotion and stress/aggression responses, functionally
distinct from but overlapping with octopaminergic signalling.

## Label provenance

Table S2 records this as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 64
("Octopaminergic-Tyraminergic"), annotated "Tyraminergic" by literature
markers; no reference given, no REVIEW flag. GEO label matches the S2
subcluster annotation directly.

## Mapping notes

This is a merged group at the neurotransmitter-class level: multiple
anatomically distinct tyraminergic neuron clusters are combined here with no
further subclustering. The honest mapping level is a general "tyraminergic
neuron" parent term, not a specific named cluster/type.
