# PAM

*Cluster `PAM` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 42, Fer2(+) subcluster; 222 cells;
ages 0-50 days) in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A
subcluster of the parent dopaminergic cluster resolved by the transcription
factor Fer2.*

## Summary

The paper subclustered its res-2.0 "Dopaminergic" cluster (42) using Fer2 as
a marker, distinguishing "a cluster of protocerebral anterior-medial
dopaminergic cells" - the PAM (protocerebral anterior medial) dopaminergic
neuron cluster. PAM is itself a large, heterogeneous group of dopaminergic
neurons known from other work (e.g. Aso et al., 2014; split-GAL4 studies) to
comprise ~15 anatomically distinct subtypes innervating different mushroom
body lobe compartments; this atlas resolves PAM as one Fer2(+) unit only,
without further compartment-level subclustering.

## Markers

Shared res-2.0 cluster 42 markers (parent-cluster level, not PAM-specific):
DAT, ple, Vmat, Fer2, JhI-21, Ddc, DIP-delta, Dhpr, Dop2R, hth, Lim1. Fer2 is
the defining marker that separates PAM from the Fer2(-) remainder
(GEO label `Dopaminergic`); Fer2 has an established role in PAM dopaminergic
neuron development/maintenance (Bou Dib et al., 2014).

## Location

Mushroom body; PAM neurons are dopaminergic afferents to the horizontal
(beta', gamma) mushroom body lobes, with cell bodies in the protocerebral
anterior-medial region.

## Function

Dopaminergic modulation of mushroom body output, principally reward/
appetitive reinforcement signalling in associative learning (contrasting
with PPL1's aversive role).

## Label provenance

Table S2 records this as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 42 ("Dopaminergic"),
annotated "PAM (Fer2(+)) neurons" by literature markers (Bou Dib et al.,
2014). No REVIEW flag; the GEO label PAM is a curated synonym of this S2
subcluster annotation. Marker genes listed are for the parent res-2.0
cluster, not resolved to the PAM subcluster specifically.

## Mapping notes

PAM as annotated here is a merged group: it corresponds to the whole PAM
dopaminergic neuron cluster as a single unit, not to any of its ~15
individually-named PAM subtypes (which differ by mushroom body compartment
innervated). The honest mapping level is a PAM-cluster/PAM-neuron parent
term, not a specific PAM subtype.
