# DN1

*Cluster `DN1` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 86; 29 cells; ages 0-50 days) in Davie
et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). Dorsal clock neuron group 1,
directly annotated at res-2.0.*

## Summary

DN1 is one of the recognized clock-neuron groups of the Drosophila circadian
network, distinct from the LNv (reported separately as `LNv`) and from the
mixed remainder reported as `Clock`. It was annotated directly on res-2.0
cluster 86 using literature markers/mapping (Abruzzi et al., 2017; Klarsfeld
et al., 2004; Kunst et al., 2014). DN1 neurons are typically subdivided in
the literature into DN1a (~2 cells/hemisphere) and DN1p (~15 cells/
hemisphere, sometimes further split anterior/posterior); this atlas resolves
DN1 as a single cluster without an a/p split.

## Markers

Top res-2.0 cluster 86 markers: CNMa, cry, Mmp2, nrv1, Dh31, AstC, CG13054,
svr, Clk, Pdfr, VGlut, inaC, gl. Dh31 (diuretic hormone 31) and CNMa are
established markers of DN1p neurons in the clock literature (Cavanaugh et
al., 2014; Kunst et al., 2014), suggesting this cluster is DN1p-dominated;
Pdfr (Pdf receptor) is consistent with DN1's known role receiving Pdf input
from the LNv; cry and Clk confirm core clock-gene identity generically.
VGlut suggests a glutamatergic component to this cluster's signalling.

## Location

Dorsal protocerebrum, near the pars intercerebralis; DN1p somata sit
dorso-posteriorly and project into the dorsal protocerebrum and toward the
pars intercerebralis/accessory medulla region.

## Function

Circadian pacemaker neurons; DN1 (particularly DN1p) integrates
light/temperature cues and Pdf signalling from the LNv, contributing to
evening activity peak timing and temperature-dependent rhythm entrainment.

## Label provenance

Table S2 res-2.0 annotation for cluster 86 is "DN1" (exact match), method
literature markers/mapping, refs Abruzzi et al. 2017; Klarsfeld et al. 2004;
Kunst et al. 2014. No REVIEW flag; no S2-related subcluster rows (not
further subclustered).

## Mapping notes

Marker evidence (Dh31, CNMa) points toward this cluster being predominantly
DN1p rather than DN1a, but the atlas label itself does not distinguish DN1a
from DN1p, and cell numbers (29) are consistent with either a DN1p-dominant
mix or an unresolved DN1a+DN1p combination. The honest mapping level is
"DN1" as a parent grouping term, with a note that available markers favour
DN1p; a DN1p-specific term should not be assigned without independent
confirmation that DN1a cells are absent.
