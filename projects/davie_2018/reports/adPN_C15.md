# adPN/C15

*Cluster `adPN/C15` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 27; 46 cells; ages 0-50 days) in
Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). "C15(+) adPNs" - a
marker-defined subgroup of anterodorsal olfactory projection neurons,
replicating a subcluster from Li et al. 2017 (Table S2).*

## Summary

Within res-2.0 cluster 27 (543 OPN cells), the authors subclustered adPNs and
lPNs against Li et al. 2017's SMART-seq2 reference of sorted adPN/lPN
transcriptomes and reproduced all of that study's reported subclusters
(Figure 2H). This label is the "C15(+) adPNs" subgroup: anterodorsal-lineage
PNs expressing the homeodomain transcription factor C15, one of the
combinatorial markers Li et al. used, together with tup/otp/hth, to
distinguish adPN subtypes (Li et al., 2017; Komiyama and Luo, 2007).

## Markers

- Defining marker: C15 (positive), within the adPN lineage.
- Table S3 top markers listed are for the parent res-2.0 cluster 27, not
 specific to this subcluster: otp, ST6Gal, hth, Oaz, CG43729, caup, acj6,
 mirr, C15, Drep3, CR32111, ara, mbl, cpo, tup, tnc, kn, CG7708, ChAT, VAChT.
 Note C15 does appear in this parent-cluster marker list, consistent with
 its role in defining this subgroup.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** antennal lobe glomeruli (dendrites, uniglomerular); axons via
 iACT to mushroom body calyx and lateral horn.
- **Cell body:** anterodorsal cell cluster of the antennal lobe.
- **Cell class:** olfactory projection neuron, anterodorsal lineage,
 C15(+) subgroup, class N.

## Function

- Uniglomerular relay of odor information to mushroom body and lateral horn;
 no glomerulus-specific function is assigned by this atlas.

## Label provenance

Table S2 lists "C15(+) adPNs" as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 27 ("OPN:
adPN and lPN"), by literature markers, referencing Li et al. (2017).
label_validation.tsv: match (curated synonym of S2 'C15(+) adPNs'). No
REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

C15 is a combinatorial transcription-factor marker used by Li et al. (2017)
to subdivide adPNs into finer transcriptomic groups; it is not, on the
evidence available here, established as a marker of one single named
glomerular PN type. Li et al. (2017) state they profiled most PN classes but
only "unequivocally mapped transcriptomes to specific olfactory function"
(i.e., to a known glomerulus/type) for six of them; whether C15(+) adPNs is
one of those six is not stated in the Davie et al. text and is not otherwise
evidenced here. Treat this as a transcriptomic subgroup of the adPN lineage
of uncertain anatomical specificity - possibly a single glomerular PN type,
possibly a small merged group of related types - rather than a confirmed
single named cell type. Flag for the mapping step to check the original Li
et al. 2017 figures/tables before assigning a specific FBbt PN type.
