# Tm5ab

*Cluster `Tm5ab` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 41; 351 cells; ages 0-50 days) in
Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). Merged label for two of the
four Tm5 colour-vision transmedullary subtypes.*

## Summary

Tm5ab groups two of the four classically described Tm5 subtypes (Tm5a and
Tm5b), distinguished from the separately clustered Tm5c (see the `Tm5c`
report) by cholinergic identity and different photoreceptor inputs. This is
a marker/literature-based identification, not a cross-dataset mapping call.

## Markers

Table S2 annotation "Tm5ab" (method: literature markers; ref Konstantinides
et al., 2018). Top Table S3 markers for res-2.0 cluster 41: DIP-theta, vvl,
CG9896, Cbp53E, Syp, Rbp6, Ggamma30A, Bx, glec, Dop2R, Tk, scro, Rbfox1,
Ten-a, 5-HT2A, Epac, rdgA, Pka-C3.

## Location

Tm5a and Tm5b are transmedullary neurons with dendrites in the medulla and
axon terminals in the lobula, part of the R7/R8-associated colour-processing
pathway; they are cholinergic, in contrast to the glutamatergic Tm5c
(Karuppudurai et al., 2014; Gao et al., 2008).

## Function

Tm5a/Tm5b relay chromatic (UV/blue-sensitive) photoreceptor and lamina
signals from the medulla to the lobula as part of colour-vision processing,
distinct from the Tm5c/Dm8 UV-preference pathway.

## Label provenance

Table S2 and GEO agree on the merged "Tm5ab" label (label_validation.tsv:
"match"). No REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

Genuine merged group of two related but anatomically/molecularly distinct
subtypes (Tm5a and Tm5b) that this cluster resolution does not separate. The
honest FBbt level is a common parent covering Tm5a and Tm5b, not either
subtype alone; note this is explicitly not the same cluster as Tm5c, which
the paper treats as transcriptionally distinct (though itself entangled with
Dm8 — see the `Tm5c` report).
