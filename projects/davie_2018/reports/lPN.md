# lPN

*Cluster `lPN` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 27; 14 cells; ages 0-30 days, no 50-day
cells sampled) in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain).
Lateral-lineage olfactory projection neurons, resolved to lineage but not to
a Li et al. 2017 marker subgroup.*

## Summary

lPNs (lateral projection neurons) are a second developmental lineage of
antennal-lobe olfactory projection neurons, derived from the lateral
neuroblast and comprising a mix of uniglomerular and multiglomerular types
that project via the middle antennocerebral tract (mACT) (Lai et al., 2008;
Komiyama and Luo, 2007). Within res-2.0 cluster 27 (543 OPN cells,
subclustered per Figure 2H against Li et al. 2017's SMART-seq2 adPN/lPN
reference), this label captures cells assigned to the lPN lineage but not
further resolved into the unpg+ or CG31676+ marker subgroups reported in
Table S2. At only 14 cells this is a very small residual group.

## Markers

- Table S3 top markers are for the parent res-2.0 cluster 27 (not specific to
  the lPN subgroup): otp, ST6Gal, hth, Oaz, CG43729, caup, acj6, mirr, C15,
  Drep3, CR32111, ara, mbl, cpo, tup, tnc, kn, CG7708, ChAT, VAChT.
- tup, otp, hth and caup are established adPN/lPN lineage markers (Komiyama
  and Luo, 2007); unpg and CG31676 are the finer markers Li et al. (2017)
  used to split lPNs, but are not used to define this label.
- Cholinergic (ChAT/VAChT); note some lPNs (particularly multiglomerular
  types) are reported as GABAergic elsewhere in the literature, which this
  small, unresolved sample cannot address.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** antennal lobe glomeruli (dendrites, uni- or multiglomerular);
  axons via mACT to lateral horn and mushroom body calyx.
- **Cell body:** lateral cell cluster of the antennal lobe.
- **Cell class:** olfactory projection neuron, lateral lineage, class N.

## Function

- Relay of odor information from antennal-lobe glomeruli (single or multiple)
  to the lateral horn (innate odor valence) and mushroom body calyx.

## Label provenance

Table S2 res-2.0 cluster 27 is annotated "OPN: adPN and lPN" (literature
markers/mapping; Komiyama and Luo, 2007; Komiyama et al., 2003; Li et al.,
2017). label_validation.tsv: match (curated synonym of S2 'OPN: adPN and
lPN'). No REVIEW flag. This label is a curator-assigned refinement
identifying the lPN lineage within cluster 27, coarser than the two
marker-defined lPN subgroups (unpg+, CG31676+) also listed under Table S2.

## Mapping notes

lPNs comprise a smaller, more heterogeneous set of individual types (uni- and
multiglomerular) than adPNs. This 14-cell label is a merged group at lineage
resolution only, with no marker information to narrow it further; the small
cell number also makes it a weak sample. The honest mapping level is "lateral
olfactory projection neuron (lineage)," not any individual glomerular PN
type.
