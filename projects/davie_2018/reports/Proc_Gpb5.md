# Proc/Gpb5

*Cluster `Proc/Gpb5` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 46; 52 cells; ages 0, 1, 3, 6,
9, 15, 30, 50 days) in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A
proctolin/Gpb5 co-expression subcluster drawn consistently from the shared
"Peptidergic" parent cluster.*

## Summary

"Proc/Gpb5" denotes cells co-expressing proctolin (Proc, a locomotor/muscle-
modulating neuropeptide) and Gpb5 (glycoprotein hormone beta-5, part of the
insect GPA2/GPB5 glycoprotein hormone that signals through the LGR3
receptor and has been implicated in ion/water homeostasis and stress
responses; Sellami and Veenstra, Paluzzi et al.). Unlike Proc/Ms, this
subcluster is drawn overwhelmingly (50/52 cells) from a single res-2.0
parent, cluster 46 ("Peptidergic"), the same shared parent as Hug and
Gr43a — indicating this is a coherent subpopulation of the general
peptidergic cluster rather than a scattered remainder.

## Markers

- Table S3 top markers are for the shared parent cluster 46 (Hug, Proc,
  twit, rhea, Crz, ITP, Gr43a, etc.); Proc is present, but Gpb5 is not
  among the top 20 — consistent with these being parent-cluster, not
  subcluster-specific, markers.

## Location

- Not established by this atlas. Gpb5/LGR3 signalling in Drosophila has
  been linked to specific neurosecretory neurons in the literature, but no
  co-expressing "Proc/Gpb5" population is independently described there;
  this may be a co-expression grouping specific to this study.

## Function

- Proctolin: locomotor/muscle modulation. Gpb5: implicated (via LGR3) in
  osmotic/ionic homeostasis and stress signalling. No combined functional
  claim is made in this paper.

## Label provenance

No REVIEW flag. GEO label "Proc/Gpb5" comes from the S2 neuropeptide
re-clustering; the shared parent res-2.0 cluster 46 is annotated
"Peptidergic" (literature markers, no reference), and 50/52 cells derive
from that one cluster, unlike the scattered Proc/Ms group.

## Mapping notes

No established, independently published "Proc/Gpb5 neuron" cell type is
known; this is best treated as a transcriptomic co-expression subcluster
first delineated by this atlas, coherent in origin (single dominant parent
cluster) but without independent anatomical corroboration. A generic
"proctolin- and Gpb5-expressing neuron" class, or leaving unmapped pending
further evidence, is the honest option — moderate confidence in the
cluster's internal coherence, low confidence in it representing a
previously recognised cell type.
