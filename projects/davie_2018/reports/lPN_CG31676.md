# lPN/CG31676

*Cluster `lPN/CG31676` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 27; 52 cells; ages 0-50 days)
in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). "CG31676(+) lPNs" - a
marker-defined subgroup of lateral-lineage olfactory projection neurons,
replicating a subcluster from Li et al. 2017 (Table S2).*

## Summary

Within res-2.0 cluster 27 (543 OPN cells), the authors subclustered adPNs and
lPNs against Li et al. 2017's SMART-seq2 reference and reproduced all of that
study's reported subclusters (Figure 2H). This label is the "CG31676(+)
lPNs" subgroup: lateral-lineage PNs expressing CG31676, an uncharacterized
gene used by Li et al. (2017) as one of the combinatorial markers
distinguishing lPN subtypes, alongside unpg (Li et al., 2017; Komiyama and
Luo, 2007).

## Markers

- Defining marker: CG31676-positive, within the lPN lineage.
- Table S3 top markers are for the parent res-2.0 cluster 27, not specific to
 this subcluster: otp, ST6Gal, hth, Oaz, CG43729, caup, acj6, mirr, C15,
 Drep3, CR32111, ara, mbl, cpo, tup, tnc, kn, CG7708, ChAT, VAChT (CG31676
 itself is not among the top 20 parent-cluster markers, consistent with it
 being a finer subcluster-defining marker rather than a cluster-27-wide
 one).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** antennal lobe glomeruli (dendrites, uni- or multiglomerular);
 axons via mACT to lateral horn and mushroom body calyx.
- **Cell body:** lateral cell cluster of the antennal lobe.
- **Cell class:** olfactory projection neuron, lateral lineage, CG31676(+)
 subgroup, class N.

## Function

- Relay of odor information to the lateral horn and mushroom body calyx; no
 glomerulus-specific function is assigned by this atlas.

## Label provenance

Table S2 lists "CG31676(+) lPNs" as a subcluster of res-2.0 cluster 27 ("OPN:
adPN and lPN"), by literature markers, referencing Li et al. (2017).
label_validation.tsv: match (curated synonym of S2 'CG31676(+) lPNs'). No
REVIEW flag.

## Mapping notes

CG31676 is a combinatorial marker (function otherwise uncharacterized) used
by Li et al. (2017) to subdivide lPNs; it is not, on the evidence available
here, independently established as marking one single named glomerular PN
type. Li et al. (2017) note only six of the many PN classes profiled were
"unequivocally mapped" to a known glomerulus/function, and it is not stated
whether CG31676(+) lPNs is one of these. Treat as a transcriptomic subgroup
of the lPN lineage of uncertain anatomical specificity rather than a
confirmed single named cell type; flag for the mapping step to check Li et
al. 2017 directly.
