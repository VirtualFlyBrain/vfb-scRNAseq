# CCHa1

*Cluster `CCHa1` (Seurat res-2.0 cluster 68; 22 cells; ages 0, 1, 3, 6, 9, 30,
50 days) in Davie et al. 2018 (GSE107451, 57K adult brain). A small
CCHamide-1-expressing population from the neuropeptide re-clustering.*

## Summary

CCHa1 (CCHamide-1) is a Drosophila neuropeptide with recognised roles in
feeding, locomotor activity and modulation of insulin/reward signalling
(e.g. Sano et al., 2015, who characterised CCHa1 primarily in gut
enteroendocrine cells, with additional CCHa1-expressing neurons in the CNS).
Unlike CCAP, Hug, and Gr43a, this cluster has no corroborating higher-resolution
(res-8.0) annotation or Table S2 entry in this atlas: Table S2 marks the
parent res-2.0 cluster 68 "Unannotated", and there is no independent literature
cross-check recorded for the brain population specifically.

## Markers

- CCHa1 (the defining neuropeptide gene) is itself among the top markers of
  parent cluster 68, along with EbpIII, Phk-3, several odorant-binding
  proteins (Obp56d/e, Obp57b) and other genes not obviously peptidergic —
  suggesting cluster 68 is a small, otherwise poorly characterised cluster
  in which CCHa1 is enriched, rather than a well-established peptidergic
  signature cluster.

## Location

- Not resolved by this atlas; CCHa1-expressing brain neurons are described
  in the literature as a small, discrete set, but their specific brain
  location is not established from this dataset alone.

## Function

- Implicated in feeding/locomotor state and interaction with insulin-like
  signalling (per general CCHa1 literature); functional role of this
  specific brain subcluster is not addressed in this paper.

## Label provenance

No REVIEW flag. GEO label "CCHa1" derives solely from the S2 neuropeptide
re-clustering (Figure 2K); there is no Table S2 direct annotation of
res-2.0 cluster 68 and no res-8.0 cross-check, unlike CCAP/Gr43a. This is a
weaker evidentiary basis than those two.

## Mapping notes

CCHa1 is a real, named neuropeptide with known CNS expression, but this
cluster is defined here purely by transcriptomic co-expression /
enrichment of CCHa1 in a small re-clustered group, without independent
anatomical corroboration in this paper. Treat as a candidate CCHa1
neuron/neuron-class grouping at best; a cautious mapping (broad "CCHa1
neuron" class, or flagged as uncertain) is more honest than a specific
morphological cell type.
