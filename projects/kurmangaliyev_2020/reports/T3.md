# T3

*Cell-type report for cluster `T3` (type T3, class N -- neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from the atlas paper
local index and established optic-lobe anatomy (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989;
Shinomiya et al., 2019).*

## Summary

T3 is a columnar T-type neuron of the *Drosophila* optic lobe. Its soma lies
posteriorly adjacent to the gap between the medulla and lobula plate. The cell
body fiber projects along the proximal surface of the medulla, then bifurcates
in the second optic chiasm: one branch forms a bushy, fine arborization in
medulla layer M9, and the other forms a terminal arborization in lobula layers
2 and 3 (mix of bleb-type and fine terminal branches). T3 appears capable of
both glutamatergic and GABAergic neurotransmission, distinguishing it from the
purely cholinergic or glutamatergic T4/T5 types. In the Kurmangaliyev 2020
atlas T3 resolves as a single transcriptional cluster (one copy per column,
unicolumnar), matched to the canonical T3 morphological cell type.

## Markers

- Classified as a T neuron (T1--T5 series, Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).
- No single distinguishing marker gene reported in the atlas text; identity is
  assigned by correlation with known morphological cell type and expression of
  cluster-specific marker genes identified in the atlas supplementary analysis.
- Neurotransmitter profile: evidence for both glutamatergic (vGlut[CNSIII]-GAL4)
  and GABAergic (VGAT-GAL4 intersection plus anti-GABA antibody) identity
  (Raghu and Borst, 2011; Raghu et al., 2013), as cited in the FBbt definition.

## Location

- **Soma:** posteriorly adjacent to the gap between the medulla and lobula plate.
- **Medulla:** fine terminal arborization in medulla layer M9 (one branch after
  bifurcation in the second optic chiasm).
- **Lobula:** terminal arborization in lobula layers 2 and 3 (the other branch).
- Columnar, unicolumnar neuron; one copy per optic column.

## Function

- T3 integrates medulla layer M9 input with lobula output; precise functional
  role in motion or form processing is not fully resolved, but its bilayer
  lobula arborization pattern places it in the OFF-channel visual pathway.
- Dual (glutamate/GABA) neurotransmitter capacity suggests it may serve context-
  dependent signalling between the medulla and lobula.

## Mapping notes

- Strong candidate for a direct exact match to FBbt:00003730 "T3 neuron".
- FBbt defines T3 neuron by the same soma position, M9 medulla branch, and
  lobula layers 2/3 branch that characterise this cluster.
- No FBbt subtypes of T3 exist; the term represents the single recognised T3
  cell class.
