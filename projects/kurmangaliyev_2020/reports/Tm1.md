# Tm1

*Cluster `Tm1` (subtype == type `Tm1`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the 13 medulla neurons profiled with connectome data
(Figure S11). Anatomy is established; dataset context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

Tm1 is a narrow-field transmedullary columnar neuron of the adult optic lobe.
Its cell body lies in the distal cortex of the medulla; it receives postsynaptic
input primarily in medulla layers M2 and M3 (with bleb-type arborizations also in
M9 and fine arborizations in M2) from lamina monopolar neuron L2, and projects
its axon across the second optic chiasma to terminate with a bleb-type presynaptic
arbor in lobula layer 1. It is a cholinergic neuron, unicolumnar (one cell per
optic column), and outputs principally onto T5 neurons. Tm1 is a core component
of the OFF motion-detection pathway, conveying luminance-decrement signals from
L2 in the medulla to T5 in the lobula.

## Markers

- Profiled among the 13 medulla neurons with mapped connectome data in the atlas
  (Figure S11; connectivity adapted from Takemura et al., 2013).
- Identity confirmed by correlation with the reference connectome dataset and by
  known cell-type-specific marker genes (Kurmangaliyev et al., 2020, Figure S5C).
- Cholinergic identity established by co-labelling with a P{Cha-GAL4.7.4} driver
  and anti-ChAT antibody (Varija Raghu et al., 2011; Shinomiya et al., 2014).

## Location

- **Cell class:** narrow-field transmedullary (Tm) columnar neuron; class N
  (neuron) in the atlas.
- **Cell body:** distal cortex of the medulla.
- **Dendrites:** medulla layers M2, M3, M9 (primary input zone M2 and M3).
- **Axon terminal:** lobula layer 1 (bleb-type output zone).
- **Organisation:** unicolumnar; one cell per optic column.

## Function

- Cholinergic; relays luminance-decrement (OFF) information from lamina monopolar
  neuron L2 in the medulla to T5 neurons in the lobula.
- Part of the OFF pathway contributing to motion vision; a principal driver of
  T5 cells in the lobula layer 1.
