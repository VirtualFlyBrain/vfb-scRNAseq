# Lawf2

*Cluster `Lawf2` (subtype == type `Lawf2`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the lamina wide-field neuron types; anatomy established from
the atlas paper and prior optic-lobe anatomical literature.*

## Summary

Lawf2 is lamina wide-field neuron 2 — a wide-field interneuron of the adult optic
lobe whose soma lies in the lamina. It belongs to the Lawf family (Lawf1, Lawf2)
of wide-field neurons that have wide lateral arborisations extending across many
cartridges of the lamina and medulla, contrasting with the narrow columnar
geometry of the lamina monopolar (L) neurons.

## Markers

- Profiled as a named optic-lobe neuronal type in the atlas (Kurmangaliyev et al.
  2020, GSE156455); cluster identity inherited from the Lawf nomenclature
  established by Hasegawa et al. 2011.
- Lamina wide-field neurons (Lawf) are putatively cholinergic; putative
  cholinergic presynaptic sites are observed in the lamina.

## Location

- **Cell class:** adult optic lobe neuron (class N).
- **Main arborisations:** lamina (distal, wide-field, spanning approximately
  28 cartridges) and medulla (M1 layer with large overlapping branches spanning
  approximately 120 cartridges; M8-M10 layers with smaller branches spanning
  approximately 17 cartridges).
- Distinct from Lawf1 in the extent and laminar distribution of its projections.

## Function

- Wide-field lateral integration across the lamina retinotopic map; putatively
  presynaptic in the lamina, suggesting a modulatory or feedback role in early
  visual processing.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt contains a specific term "lamina wide-field 2 neuron" (FBbt:00110065)
  defined from Hasegawa et al. 2011; this is the canonical morphological
  description of Lawf2. Expect an exact match.
