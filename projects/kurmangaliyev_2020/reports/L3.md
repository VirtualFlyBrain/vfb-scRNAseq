# L3

## Summary

L3 is a lamina monopolar neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe, residing in the lamina and extending its primary projection through the first optic chiasm to terminate with a presynaptic arborization in medulla layer M3. It is one of five columnar lamina monopolar neurons (L1-L5), with one cell per optic column. L3 is GABAergic and is defined by expression of GABA together with the VGAT-GAL4 driver.

## Markers

- VGAT (vesicular GABA transporter; driver used for identification)
- GABA (immunoreactivity; neurotransmitter marker)
- Short spines on the main lamina projection (morphological hallmark)

## Location

- Cell body and dendritic arbor: lamina
- Axon terminal arborization: medulla layer M3 (outer medulla)
- One cell per optic column; bilateral in the visual system

## Function

L3 receives synaptic input from all six classes of outer photoreceptors (R1-R6) and from lamina wide-field neurons (Lawf) in the lamina, as well as additional input from inner photoreceptors partly in the first optic chiasm and partly in the medulla. It forms presynaptic terminals in medulla layer M3 and provides strong output to medulla intrinsic neuron Mi9 and transmedullary neuron Tm20, contributing to the OFF motion-detection and colour-processing pathways.
