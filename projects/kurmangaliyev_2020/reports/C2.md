# C2

*Cluster `C2` (subtype == type `C2`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the optic-lobe centrifugal neurons; anatomy established by
Fischbach and Dittrich (1989), connectome resolved by Takemura et al. (2013).*

## Summary

C2 is centrifugal neuron C2, a GABAergic columnar interneuron of the optic
lobe that carries signals centrifugally from the medulla back to the lamina.
It is one of a small set of centrifugal neuron types (C2, C3) that feed
processed visual information in the retrograde direction, looping from the
medulla to the lamina. One C2 cell exists per optic column.

## Markers

- Centrifugal identity with columnar (per-optic-column) organisation.
- GABAergic neurotransmitter identity (Fei et al., 2010).
- Profiled among the lamina-projecting neurons in the atlas (Figure S11 of
  Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020).

## Location

- **Cell class:** optic-lobe centrifugal columnar neuron (dataset class N).
- **Arborisations:** medulla layers M1, M5/M6, M8 and M10; restricted
  arborisations in the lamina.

## Function

- Carries centrifugal signals from the medulla to the lamina, reversing the
  primary photoreceptor-to-medulla flow.
- Makes reciprocal connections with lamina monopolar neuron L1 in the medulla,
  and is presynaptic to L2 and L5 as well as medullary intrinsic neuron Mi1
  (Takemura et al., 2013).
- GABAergic; involved in feedback modulation of early visual processing.
