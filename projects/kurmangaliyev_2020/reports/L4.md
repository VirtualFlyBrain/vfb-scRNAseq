# L4

*Cluster `L4` (subtype == type `L4`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the lamina monopolar neurons; anatomy established, dataset
context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

L4 is lamina monopolar neuron 4 — a columnar interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. It arborizes in the proximal lamina, crosses the first optic chiasm, and
forms a spreading arbor in medulla layer M2 together with a small terminal arbor
in M4-M5 that penetrates adjacent columns. It is distinguished from the other
lamina monopolar neurons by this inter-column connectivity in the medulla and by
dual (cholinergic and GABAergic) neurotransmitter capacity.

## Markers

- Lamina monopolar (L-neuron) identity; profiled among the medulla-projecting
  lamina neurons in the atlas connectome figure (L1-L5, Figure S11).
- scRNAseq-based neurotransmitter evidence consistent with both choline
  acetyltransferase and vesicular acetylcholine transporter expression (Davis
  et al. 2020), and GABA immunoreactivity reported via VGAT-GAL4 (Raghu et al.
  2013).

## Location

- **Cell class:** lamina monopolar columnar neuron (class N).
- **Soma/dendrites:** proximal lamina; **axon:** projects to medulla layers M2
  and M4-M5, with collaterals penetrating one to two adjacent optic columns.

## Function

- Receives presynaptic input in the proximal lamina; forms presynaptic terminals
  in medulla M2 and M4-M5. Synapses onto transmedullary neuron Tm2; three L4
  neurons from adjacent columns converge on the same Tm2 target, providing
  lateral integration. Capable of both cholinergic and GABAergic
  neurotransmission. One cell per optic column.
