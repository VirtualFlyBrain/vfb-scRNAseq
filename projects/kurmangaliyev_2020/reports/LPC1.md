# LPC1

*Cell-type report for cluster `LPC1` (type LPC1, class N -- neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from established
optic-lobe anatomy (Shinomiya et al., 2022; Davis et al., 2020; Panser et al., 2016).*

## Summary

LPC1 is a columnar visual projection neuron of the adult *Drosophila* optic lobe
whose soma lies in the cell body rind of the lobula plate. It is a member of the
lobula plate columnar (LPC) family: neurons that receive input exclusively within
the lobula plate and project posteriorly to the central brain. LPC1 receives its
main dendritic input in lobula plate layer 2, from T4b and T5b neurons (the
back-to-front and null-direction motion channels for the posterior visual field).
It projects to a small glomerulus in the posterior sector of the optic glomeruli
of the posterior ventrolateral protocerebrum (PVLP), situated posterior to LC4
and ventral to LPLC3. LPC1 is cholinergic.

## Markers

- Cluster label is the canonical anatomical name LPC1 (Shinomiya et al., 2022);
  no driver-based qualifier.
- Identity assigned in the atlas by correlation with the known LPC1 morphological
  cell type and cluster-specific marker genes identified in supplementary analysis
  (Kurmangaliyev et al., 2020).
- Columnar (unicolumnar); cluster size consistent with one or a small number of
  cells per optic column.
- Neurotransmitter: cholinergic (Davis et al., 2020).

## Location

- **Soma:** cell body rind of the lobula plate.
- **Dendrites:** lobula plate layer 2 (primary input layer).
- **Presynaptic input:** from T4b and T5b neurons.
- **Axon projection:** posterior path from the lobula plate to a small glomerulus
  in the optic glomeruli of the PVLP, posterior to LC4 and ventral to LPLC3.
- Input is confined to the lobula plate; no arborisation in the lamina, medulla,
  or lobula.

## Function

- LPC1 is a motion-processing projection neuron that relays directional motion
  signals from T4b/T5b (back-to-front horizontal motion) to the central brain.
- As a cholinergic visual projection neuron targeting the PVLP optic glomeruli,
  LPC1 is thought to participate in circuits for wide-field motion detection and
  visually guided locomotion.
- It is anatomically and functionally distinct from the lobula plate-lobula
  columnar neurons (LPLC family), which arborise in both the lobula plate and
  lobula.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt carries an exact term "lobula plate columnar neuron LPC1" (FBbt:00111767)
  matching this cluster label and anatomy one-to-one.
- The FBbt definition cites Shinomiya et al. (2022) for the lobula plate layer 2
  dendritic arborisation and T4b/T5b input, and Davis et al. (2020) for the
  cholinergic neurotransmitter identity; both features are consistent with the
  cluster as annotated in this atlas.
- The parent class "lobula plate columnar neuron" (FBbt:00111766) and the sibling
  "lobula plate columnar neuron LPC2" (FBbt:00052852, layer 3, T4c/T5c input) are
  present in FBbt and confirm that LPC1 is a correctly distinguished subtype at
  the right level of specificity.
- No FBbt subtypes of LPC1 exist; the term represents the single recognised LPC1
  cell class.
