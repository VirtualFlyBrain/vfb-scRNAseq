# LC6

## Summary

LC6 is a lobula columnar visual projection neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe. It is an extrinsic neuron whose dendrites arborise in lobula layers 4 and 5B and whose axon projects via the anterior optic tract to an optic glomerulus in the dorsolateral posterior ventrolateral protocerebrum (PVLP). The cluster was identified in the Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020 transcriptomic atlas of the fly visual system (GSE156455) and corresponds to the well-characterised LC6 cell type.

## Markers

No single canonical marker gene defines LC6 exclusively, but the cluster is named after the anatomical type LC6 established by morphological and connectivity studies (Wu et al., 2016; Morimoto et al., 2020). LC6 neurons are cholinergic.

## Location

- Lobula (dendritic input, layers 4 and 5B)
- Anterior optic tract (axon fasciculation, together with LC9)
- LC6 optic glomerulus in the dorsolateral PVLP (axon terminal output zone)
- Cell body in the dorsal lateral cell body rind

## Function

LC6 neurons are visual projection neurons that relay information about looming or wide-field visual stimuli from the lobula to the central brain. Approximately 60 cells are present per hemisphere. The LC6 optic glomerulus receives postsynaptic input from several classes of downstream interneurons (LC6 glomerulus interneurons 1-5) that mediate avoidance and escape behaviours.

## Neurotransmitter

Cholinergic (Morimoto et al., 2020).
