# Tm9b

*Cluster `Tm9b` (subtype; parent type `Tm9`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). The atlas explicitly reports newly identified subtypes for Tm9 neurons
distinguished by dorsoventral lobula targeting and mutually exclusive Wnt gene
expression.*

## Summary

Tm9b is one of two transcriptomic subtypes (Tm9a, Tm9b) of Tm9 neurons newly
distinguished in this atlas. Tm9 neurons are narrow-field transmedullary interneurons
with dendritic arborisations in medulla layers M2 and M3 and axon terminals in lobula
layer 1, where they provide cholinergic input to T5 neurons. The atlas resolves Tm9
into two subtypes by dorsoventral targeting in the lobula and mutually exclusive Wnt
ligand expression: Tm9b targets the ventral half of the lobula and expresses Wnt4,
while Tm9a targets the dorsal half and expresses Wnt10.

## Markers

- Key discriminating marker: Wnt4 (ventral-specific; mutually exclusive with Wnt10
  expressed in Tm9a).
- Base Tm9 identity markers shared with Tm9a (medulla layer M2/M3 dendrites, lobula
  layer 1 output, cholinergic, sim expression).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (dendritic input, layers M2/M3) and lobula layer 1 (axon output).
- **Dorsoventral restriction:** ventral half of the lobula (distinguishes Tm9b from Tm9a).
- **Cell class:** narrow-field transmedullary columnar interneuron (class N), one cell
  per optic column.

## Function

- Cholinergic interneuron relaying photoreceptor-driven signals from the medulla to
  the lobula; outputs principally onto T5 neurons (motion detection pathway).
- Dorsoventral targeting distinction suggests Tm9a and Tm9b may serve distinct
  regions of the visual field.
