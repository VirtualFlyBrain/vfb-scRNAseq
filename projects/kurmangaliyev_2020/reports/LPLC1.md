# Cluster Report: LPLC1

## Summary

LPLC1 is a lobula plate-lobula columnar visual projection neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe. It is a wide-field columnar neuron that samples both the lobula plate and lobula, and projects to an optic glomerulus in the posterior ventrolateral protocerebrum (PVLP). It is classified as a loom-detecting neuron responsive to expanding visual stimuli.

## Cluster identity

- **Label**: LPLC1
- **Class**: neuron (N)
- **Atlas**: Kurmangaliyev 2020, GSE156455 (optic lobe scRNAseq)

## Location

- Dendrites in the lobula plate (all four layers, sparser in layer 1) and lobula (main arbors in layers 2-5B)
- Cell body in the dorsal area of the lateral cell body rind
- Axon projects to the posterior optic glomerulus of the PVLP, at the boundary with the posterior lateral protocerebrum (PLP)

## Morphology

- Wide-field columnar arbors spanning approximately 25 lobula optic columns, overlapping with neighbouring LPLC1 cells
- Presynaptic sites at the boundary of lobula layers 3 and 4
- Approximately 62 cells per optic lobe

## Neurotransmitter

Cholinergic (acetylcholine; confirmed by scRNAseq detection of choline acetyltransferase and vesicular acetylcholine transporter expression, Davis et al. 2020)

## Function

Detects looming (expanding) visual stimuli. Projects to a dedicated optic glomerulus in the PVLP that encodes approach/collision signals relayed to descending circuits.

## Key references

- Wu et al. 2016 (morphology, connectivity, glomerulus identity)
- Davis et al. 2020 (neurotransmitter, scRNAseq)
- Tanaka and Clark 2022 (lobula layer innervation)
