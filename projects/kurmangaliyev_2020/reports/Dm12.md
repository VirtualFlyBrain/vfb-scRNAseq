# Dm12

*Cluster `Dm12` (type `Dm12`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Dm12 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

Dm12 is a distal medullary wide-field amacrine interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. Its soma lies in the cell body rind of the medulla cortex. The primary
neurite extends into layer M6, then turns back to project to M3B; the arbor is
circular in shape, peripheral intracolumnar in position, and covers approximately
15-20 columns with overlapping neighbours. Dm12 is glutamatergic (Davis et al.,
2020) and is classified as a secondary Notch OFF hemilineage neuron born from a
medulla neuroblast during the Hbn/Opa/Slp temporal window. Approximately 120 Dm12
neurons are present per hemisphere.

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with a glutamatergic medulla interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Dm12
  (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (layers M6 and M3B).
- **Cell body:** medulla cortex cell body rind.
- **Cell class:** optic lobe wide-field amacrine interneuron, multicolumnar
  (circular arbor, 15-20 columns).

## Function

- Medulla interneuron; wide-field lateral integration across 15-20 columns in
  the distal medulla. Glutamatergic neurotransmission.
