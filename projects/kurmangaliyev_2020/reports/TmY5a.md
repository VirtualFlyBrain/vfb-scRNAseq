# TmY5a

*Cluster `TmY5a` (type TmY5a, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). TmY5a is an established adult optic lobe cell type whose morphology
was characterised by Morante and Desplan (2008) and Fischbach and Dittrich (1989).*

## Summary

TmY5a is a narrow-field transmedullary Y neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe.
Its cell body resides in the distal cortex of the medulla. As a TmY neuron, it
extends a primary process through the medulla, then splits at the second optic
chiasm, sending one branch to the lobula and a second branch to the lobula plate,
giving the characteristic Y-shaped arborisation. TmY5a specifically terminates with
both fine and bleb-type arborizations in lobula layers 4, 5 and 6 and in lobula
plate layer 3, and has fine-only arborizations in lobula plate layer 1; within the
medulla it displays mixed arborizations in layers M6 and M8 and fine arborizations
in layers M2, M3, M4, M5 and M9. It is glutamatergic (Davis et al., 2020).

## Markers

- TmY5a identity defined by morphological subtype within the TmY class; no
  driver-based marker name is used in the atlas label.
- Glutamatergic neurotransmitter identity (consistent with Davis et al., 2020).

## Location

- **Neuropils:** medulla (layers M2-M9), lobula (layers 4-6), lobula plate
  (layers 1 and 3).
- **Cell class:** narrow-field transmedullary Y neuron; adult optic lobe columnar
  interneuron; Notch OFF hemilineage secondary neuron.

## Function

- Optic lobe interneuron; processes visual information across medulla, lobula and
  lobula plate. Glutamatergic neurotransmission.
