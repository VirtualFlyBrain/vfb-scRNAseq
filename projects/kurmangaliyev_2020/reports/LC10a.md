# LC10a

*Cluster `LC10a` (type `LC10a`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). LC10a is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

LC10a is a lobula columnar visual projection neuron of the adult optic lobe.
It is a subtype of the LC10 group (lobula columnar neuron LC10), which itself
belongs to the LC10A division (those LC10 neurons whose axons enter the medial
zone of the anterior optic tubercle, AOTU). The cell body lies in the dorsal
region of the lateral cell body rind. The primary dendrites arborize in lobula
layers 3, 4, and 5B, with some processes extending into layer 2 and scattered
branches in layers 5 and 6; presynaptic (output) sites are concentrated in
layer 3 (Wu et al., 2016). The axon crosses ipsilaterally along the anterior
optic tract and terminates in the AOTU. LC10a is cholinergic (Davis et al.,
2020) and is involved in the detection of fly-sized moving objects; its
response sensitivity increases during male courtship or female aggression
(Schretter et al., 2025). A fruitless-expressing subpopulation of LC10a
neurons is implicated in male tracking of females during courtship (Ribeiro
et al., 2018).

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with a cholinergic visual projection
  neuron of the lobula columnar type.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type LC10a
  (Strausfeld and Okamura, 2007; Wu et al., 2016).

## Location

- **Neuropil (input):** lobula, layers 3-5B (presynaptic in layer 3).
- **Neuropil (output):** anterior optic tubercle (AOTU), medial zone.
- **Pathway:** anterior optic tract (ipsilateral projection).
- **Cell body:** dorsal lateral cell body rind.
- **Cell class:** lobula columnar neuron, visual projection neuron.

## Function

- Detects fly-sized moving objects in the visual field; sensitivity modulated
  by social context (courtship, aggression). Cholinergic neurotransmission.
  Projects from the lobula to the AOTU, linking the optic lobe to central
  brain circuits for action selection.

  whose definition matches this cluster precisely: same lobula layer profile
  (layers 3, 4, 5B; presynaptic in layer 3), same AOTU projection zone

  The cluster label is the canonical anatomical name. No subtype ambiguity
