# Dm8

*Cluster `Dm8` (type `Dm8`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Dm8 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology; later connectome and transcriptomic work split it into sub-subtypes
(Dm8a/Dm8b and the pale/yellow variants yDm8/pDm8), but the atlas cluster is
labelled at the undivided Dm8 level.*

## Summary

Dm8 is a distal medullary wide-field amacrine interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. Its soma lies in the cell body rind of the medulla cortex. It elaborates
an extensive arbor in medulla layer M6 (principally M6A), spanning approximately
16 columns with a tiling home-column architecture and centrifugal projections
back to layer M4. Dm8 is glutamatergic and is classified as a secondary
(Notch OFF hemilineage) neuron born from a medulla neuroblast. It is
postsynaptic to R7 photoreceptor axons and presynaptic to transmedullary Tm5c
neurons in layer M6, placing it on the UV-wavelength colour-discrimination
pathway (Gao et al., 2008; Karuppudurai et al., 2014).

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with glutamatergic medulla interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Dm8
  (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).
- No marker resolves Dm8a from Dm8b at the transcript level in this atlas;
  the cluster therefore represents the pre-split Dm8 cell type.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla, distal layers (principally M6A, with projections to M4).
- **Cell body:** medulla cortex cell body rind.
- **Cell class:** optic lobe amacrine interneuron, wide-field, multicolumnar
  (~16 columns), home-column tiling.

## Function

- Medulla interneuron mediating UV photoreceptor (R7) signals to the lobula
  colour pathway via Tm5c. Role in UV preference behaviour (Gao et al., 2008).
  Glutamatergic neurotransmission.

## Mapping notes

term for the Dm8 cell type, confirmed current and not obsolete in the latest
FBbt release. It sits between the parent class "distal medullary amacrine
neuron" (FBbt:00003767) and the two subtypes Dm8a (FBbt:00053638) and Dm8b
(FBbt:00053639). The atlas cluster is labelled at the pre-subtype-split Dm8
level, with no marker resolution between Dm8a and Dm8b; it therefore maps
exactly to FBbt:00013774 (skos:exactMatch, high confidence).
