# Pm4

## Summary

Pm4 is a proximal medullary amacrine neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe. It is an intrinsic neuron of the medulla neuropil that arborises at the proximal surface of the medulla, spanning approximately 10-20 columns, with synaptic input and output predominantly in layers M8-M9. Pm4 is GABAergic and belongs to the Notch OFF hemilineage of secondary neurons born from medulla neuroblasts during the Hth/Opa temporal window.

## Location

Proximal medulla (layers M8-M9), adult optic lobe.

## Neurotransmitter

GABAergic.

## Morphology

Amacrine (no primary axon); arborisations confined to the proximal surface of the medulla. Covers approximately 10-20 columns. Occupies a central intracolumnar position. Approximately 75-85 neurons per hemisphere.

## Markers

Identified as a distinct cluster in the Kurmangaliyev 2020 adult Drosophila optic-lobe scRNAseq atlas (GSE156455). Cluster label Pm4 corresponds to the canonical optic-lobe nomenclature for proximal medulla amacrine neurons of subtype 4.
