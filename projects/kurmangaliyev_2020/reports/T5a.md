# T5a

*Cluster `T5a` (subtype; parent type `T4.T5`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al.
2020 (GSE156455). Dataset facts from the atlas paper; directional/layer identity
is established optic-lobe anatomy.*

## Summary

T5a is one of the four subtypes (a-d) of T5 neurons, the OFF-motion
direction-selective columnar neurons of the Drosophila optic lobe. T5 (OFF) and
T4 (ON) are transcriptomically closely related and form a single combined `type`
(`T4.T5`) here, resolving into eight directional subtypes (T4a-d, T5a-d). Each T5
subtype projects to one of the four lobula plate layers and is tuned to one
cardinal direction; **T5a corresponds to lobula plate layer a (layer 1)**.

## Markers

- T5 shares most transcriptomic markers with T4; the atlas distinguishes the
  directional subtypes within the combined T4/T5 population.
- Subtype identity (a-d) is defined by lobula-plate layer targeting and direction
  tuning rather than a single unique transcriptomic marker.
- The paper validates T4/T5 regional subtypes with transgenic lines including
  `CG15537` restricted to T4 neurons targeting lobula plate layers a/b, and the
  `23G12-LexA` (T4/T5) line (Kurmangaliyev et al., 2020).

## Location

- **Cell class:** columnar neuron of the optic lobe (class N).
- **Dendrites:** lobula (lobula layer Lo1) — distinguishing T5 from T4, whose
  dendrites are in medulla M10.
- **Axon terminals:** lobula plate layer a (layer 1).

## Function

- First-order OFF-pathway direction-selective motion-detecting neuron; T5a encodes
  the direction read out in lobula plate layer 1.
- T5 neurons respond to OFF-motion edges; T5a is tuned to diagonal front-to-back
  direction (Henning et al., 2022).

## Mapping notes

- Strong candidate for an exact FBbt term: FBbt has T5 subtype terms by lobula
  plate layer. Expect `T5a` to map to the FBbt "T5a neuron" term targeting lobula
  plate layer 1, with the sub-subtypes T5ai/T5aii as narrower children.
