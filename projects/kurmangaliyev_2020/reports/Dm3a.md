# Dm3a

*Cluster `Dm3a` (subtype; parent type `Dm3`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). The atlas explicitly reports "newly identified subtypes for Dm3 and
Tm9 neurons"; Dm3 base-type anatomy is established.*

## Summary

Dm3a is one of two transcriptomic subtypes (Dm3a, Dm3b) of Dm3 neurons newly
distinguished in this atlas. Dm3 neurons are distal-medulla, amacrine-like,
multicolumnar interneurons arborising in the medulla (around layer M3). The atlas
resolved Dm3 into two subtypes by mutually exclusive marker expression (analogous
to the Wnt4/Wnt10 split it reports for Tm9 subtypes).

## Markers

- Dm3 base identity (distal medulla, layer M3, multicolumnar).
- Subtype split (Dm3a vs Dm3b) is transcriptomic and dataset-defined; the
  specific discriminating markers are reported in the atlas.

## Location

- **Cell class:** distal medulla interneuron (class N), multicolumnar; medulla
  layer ~M3.

## Function

- Medulla interneuron; Dm3 types are implicated in form/orientation processing.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt has "Dm3 neuron". The a/b subtype naming here is **dataset-specific** and
  may not correspond to FBbt's connectome-derived Dm3 subtypes (e.g. Dm3p/Dm3q/Dm3v
  from FlyWire). Likely a **broad match to Dm3 neuron**; an exact subtype match
  should not be forced. If no subtype term exists, flag for review — do not submit
  a term request without sign-off.
