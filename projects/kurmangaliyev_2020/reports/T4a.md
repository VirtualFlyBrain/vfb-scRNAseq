# T4a

*Cell-type report for cluster `T4a` (subtype; parent type `T4.T5`, class N — neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from the atlas paper
local index; directional/layer identity is established optic-lobe anatomy.*

## Summary

T4a is one of the four subtypes (a–d) of T4 neurons, the ON-motion
direction-selective columnar neurons of the *Drosophila* optic lobe. T4 (ON) and
T5 (OFF) neurons are transcriptomically closely related — in this atlas they form
a single combined `type` (`T4.T5`) that resolves into eight directional subtypes
(T4a–d, T5a–d). Each of the four subtypes projects to one of the four layers of
the lobula plate and is tuned to one of the four cardinal directions of motion;
**T4a corresponds to lobula plate layer a (layer 1)**.

## Markers

- T4 and T5 share most transcriptomic markers; the atlas distinguishes the
  directional subtypes within the combined T4/T5 population.
- The paper validates **regional** T4/T5 subtypes with transgenic lines, e.g.
  `CG15537` "restricted to T4 neurons targeting the dorsal third of the medulla
  and lobula plate layers a/b", and the `23G12-LexA` (T4/T5) line.
- (Directional subtype identity a–d is defined by lobula-plate layer targeting /
  direction tuning rather than a single unique transcriptomic marker.)

## Location

- **Soma / cell class:** columnar neuron of the optic lobe (class N).
- **Dendrites:** medulla layer M10 (the paper crops "the medulla 10 layer (M10)"
  when validating T4 subtypes).
- **Axon terminals:** lobula plate — T4a terminates in **lobula plate layer a (1)**.

## Function

- T4 neurons are first-order **ON-pathway, direction-selective** motion-detecting
  neurons. The four subtypes encode the four cardinal directions of visual motion,
  one per lobula-plate layer; T4a encodes the direction read out in layer a.

## Mapping notes

- Strong candidate for an exact/narrow FBbt term: FBbt has T4 neuron subtypes by
  lobula-plate layer. Expect `T4a` → the FBbt "T4 neuron" subtype targeting lobula
  plate layer 1, or broad-match to "T4 neuron" if the layer-specific subtype is
  absent.
