# Lai

*Cluster `Lai` (subtype == type `Lai`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Lamina intrinsic (amacrine) neuron; anatomy established from
serial-section EM and light-microscopy studies.*

## Summary

Lai is the lamina intrinsic (amacrine) neuron - a wide-field tangential
interneuron of the lamina with arborizations spanning both the proximal and
distal lamina. Its cell body lies proximal to the lamina. It is the sole
amacrine cell type intrinsic to the lamina proper and is therefore also called
the lamina amacrine neuron. It is glutamatergic.

## Markers

- Cluster label "Lai" directly follows the established Drosophila optic-lobe
  abbreviation for this cell type (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).
- Profiled among the 88 neuron types in the Kurmangaliyev 2020 visual-system
  developmental atlas spanning pupal 24 h, 48 h, 72 h, and adult stages.

## Location

- **Neuropil:** lamina (optic lobe first visual neuropil).
- **Arborizations:** proximal and distal lamina; tangential (wide-field), not
  restricted to a single column.
- **Cell body:** proximal to the lamina.

## Function

- Receives strong synaptic input from all six outer photoreceptors (R1-R6) and
  from lamina wide-field (Lawf) neurons.
- Principal presynaptic target is columnar neuron T1 and epithelial glia.
- Minor reciprocal contact with L3, L4, L5, C2, and C3.
- Glutamatergic; approximately 210 cells per hemisphere (adult).
- Connectivity figures from Rivera-Alba et al. 2011 (serial EM of 21 complete
  lamina cartridges).

- Expect an exact FBbt term: "lamina intrinsic neuron Lai" / FBbt:00003746.
