# L2

*Cluster `L2` (subtype == type `L2`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the lamina monopolar neurons; anatomy established, dataset
context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

L2 is lamina monopolar neuron 2 — a columnar interneuron of the lamina and a
principal input to the OFF motion-detection pathway. It is one of five lamina
monopolar cell types (L1-L5) and the primary companion to L1 in parallel
processing of photoreceptor output.

## Markers

- Lamina monopolar (L-neuron) identity; profiled among the medulla-projecting
  lamina neurons in the atlas connectome figure (L1-L5, Figure S11).

## Location

- **Cell class:** lamina monopolar columnar neuron (class N).
- **Soma/dendrites:** lamina; **axon:** projects to medulla layer M2.

## Function

- Receives histaminergic input from outer photoreceptors R1-R6 in the lamina;
  arborizes in medulla layer M2 with both pre- and postsynaptic connections.
  Strong centrifugal input from C2 and C3; strong output to L5, T1, Tm1, Tm2
  and Tm4. Cholinergic neuron; electrically coupled to L1. Primary interneuron
  of the OFF pathway.
