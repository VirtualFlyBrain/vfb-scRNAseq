# TmY3

*Cluster `TmY3` (type TmY3, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). TmY3 is an established adult optic lobe cell type characterised
morphologically by Fischbach and Dittrich (1989).*

## Summary

TmY3 is a narrow-field transmedullary Y neuron of the adult Drosophila optic
lobe. Its cell body resides in the distal cortex of the medulla. As a TmY
neuron, it extends a primary process through the medulla, then splits at the
second optic chiasm to send one branch to the lobula and a second branch to the
lobula plate, giving the characteristic Y-shaped arborisation. TmY3
specifically terminates with bleb-type arborizations in lobula layer 4 and
lobula plate layer 4; within the medulla it displays fine arborizations in
layers M1, M2, M4, M5, M6, M8 and M10, and bleb-type arborizations in medulla
layers M5 and M10. It is cholinergic (Davis et al., 2020).

## Markers

- TmY3 identity defined by morphological subtype within the TmY class; no
  driver-based marker name is used in the atlas label.
- Cholinergic neurotransmitter identity (consistent with Davis et al., 2020).

## Location

- **Neuropils:** medulla (layers M1, M2, M4-M6, M8, M10), lobula (layer 4),
  lobula plate (layer 4).
- **Cell class:** narrow-field transmedullary Y neuron; adult optic lobe
  columnar interneuron; Notch ON hemilineage secondary neuron.

## Function

- Optic lobe interneuron; processes visual information across medulla, lobula
  and lobula plate. Cholinergic neurotransmission.
