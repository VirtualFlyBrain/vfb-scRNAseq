# Mi4

*Cluster `Mi4` (subtype == type `Mi4`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the 13 medulla neurons profiled with connectome data
(Figure S11). Anatomy is established; dataset context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

Mi4 is a medulla intrinsic narrow-field columnar neuron of the Drosophila optic
lobe. It is unicolumnar and arborises in medulla layers M1-M5, M8 and M9, with
both fine and bleb-type processes in most layers. It receives input from lamina
monopolar neuron L5 and photoreceptor R8, placing it in the OFF-pathway colour
and motion circuitry of the medulla.

## Markers

- Profiled in the atlas among 13 medulla neurons with mapped connectivity
  (Figure S11; connectome adapted from Takemura et al., 2013).
- No single canonical driver marker is cited for Mi4 in the Kurmangaliyev 2020
  supplement; the cluster is identified by its transcriptomic profile relative
  to the other Mi-type neurons.

## Location

- **Cell class:** medulla intrinsic narrow-field columnar neuron (class N).
- **Neuropil:** medulla (optic lobe).
- **Arborisations:** medulla layers M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M8 and M9.

## Function

- GABAergic (Takemura et al., 2017).
- Receives input from L5 (lamina monopolar neuron) and R8 photoreceptor; part
  of the OFF-pathway colour/motion processing circuit of the medulla.

- Expect an exact FBbt term for "Mi4" / "medulla intrinsic neuron Mi4".
