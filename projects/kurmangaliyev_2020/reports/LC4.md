# Cluster Report: LC4

## Summary

LC4 is a lobula columnar visual projection neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe. It is a wide-field neuron whose dendrites arborise in lobula layers 2 and 4, spanning approximately 40 optic columns with overlap between adjacent LC4 cells. The axon projects to a dedicated optic glomerulus in the ventral medial region of the posterior ventrolateral protocerebrum (PVLP). LC4 is a canonical loom-detecting neuron: it responds to expanding visual stimuli consistent with an approaching object, and its activation drives escape behaviour.

## Cluster identity

- **Label**: LC4
- **Class**: neuron (N)
- **Atlas**: Kurmangaliyev 2020, GSE156455 (optic lobe scRNAseq)

## Location

- Dendrites in the ipsilateral lobula, main arbors in layers 2 and 4
- Cell body in an extended region of the lateral cell body rind
- Axon projects to an optic glomerulus in the ventral medial PVLP, medial to the LC12 glomerulus and ventral to the LPLC2 and LPLC1 glomeruli

## Morphology

- Wide-field; arbors span approximately 40 optic columns, overlapping with neighbouring LC4 cells
- Approximately 66 neurons per optic lobe

## Neurotransmitter

Reported as both glutamatergic (Raghu and Borst, 2011) and cholinergic (Davis et al., 2020); the neurotransmitter identity is contested in the literature.

## Function

Detects expanding (looming) visual stimuli consistent with an approaching threat. LC4 activation drives escape behaviours via downstream descending circuits. It is one of several lobula columnar neuron types projecting to distinct optic glomeruli in the PVLP that together tile approach-detection space.
