# Dm2

*Cluster `Dm2` (type `Dm2`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Dm2 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

Dm2 is a distal medullary wide-field amacrine interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. Its soma lies in the cell body rind of the medulla cortex. It elaborates
a small bushy arbor with mixed pre- and postsynaptic morphology spanning medulla
layers M3 through M6B; arbors in M6B extend into neighbouring columns on the
dorsal side, covering approximately 2 columns in M6 and one column in the other
layers. Dm2 is cholinergic (Konstantinides et al., 2022) and is classified as a
secondary (Notch OFF hemilineage) neuron born from a medulla neuroblast.

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with cholinergic medulla interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Dm2
  (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (distal layers M3-M6B).
- **Cell body:** medulla cortex cell body rind.
- **Cell class:** optic lobe amacrine interneuron, wide-field, multicolumnar
  (small arbor, ~2 columns).

## Function

- Medulla interneuron; wide-field lateral integration across a small number of
  columns in the distal medulla. Cholinergic neurotransmission.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt has a specific term "distal medullary amacrine neuron Dm2"
  (FBbt:00003769) whose definition matches this cluster precisely: same soma
  location, same layer profile (M3-M6B), same arbor size and morphology, same
  cholinergic identity. No subtype ambiguity: Dm2 is not split into further
  connectome-defined subtypes in FBbt. This is an exact match.
