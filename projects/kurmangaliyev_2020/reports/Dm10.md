# Dm10

*Cluster `Dm10` (type `Dm10`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Dm10 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

Dm10 is a distal medullary wide-field amacrine interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. Its soma lies in the cell body rind of the medulla cortex. It has a
distinct tri-stratified branching pattern, arborising extensively in medulla
layers M1, M3B, and M5. The arbor is elliptical in outline with its long axis
aligned along the dorsal-ventral axis, covering fewer than 10 columns.
Processes in M1 and M3B-M5 occupy an intracolumnar position. There are
approximately 300 Dm10 neurons per hemisphere. Dm10 is GABAergic (Davis et al.,
2020) and is classified as a secondary (Notch OFF hemilineage) neuron born from
a medulla neuroblast.

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with GABAergic medulla interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Dm10
  (Morante and Desplan, 2008; Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (distal layers M1, M3B, M5).
- **Cell body:** medulla cortex cell body rind.
- **Cell class:** optic lobe amacrine interneuron, wide-field, multicolumnar
  (small-to-medium arbor, fewer than 10 columns).

## Function

- Medulla interneuron; wide-field lateral integration across the distal medulla
  with a characteristic tri-stratified layer profile. GABAergic
  neurotransmission.
