# R1.6

*Cluster `R1.6` (subtype == type `R1.6`, class R — photoreceptor) in
Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020 (GSE156455). `R1.6` denotes the outer photoreceptors
R1-R6. Anatomy established.*

## Summary

R1.6 denotes the **outer photoreceptors R1-R6** of the compound eye — the six
outer photoreceptors present in each ommatidium. They are the primary
photoreceptors of the motion/achromatic (outer) visual pathway.

## Markers

- Express Rhodopsin 1 (Rh1 / ninaE), the rhodopsin characteristic of R1-R6.
- Class R (photoreceptor) in the atlas.

## Location

- **Cell bodies:** retina / compound eye.
- **Axon terminals:** lamina (R1-R6 terminate in the lamina; R7/R8 project to the
  medulla).

## Function

- Broad-spectrum, high-sensitivity photoreception feeding the lamina monopolar
  neurons (L1-L5); achromatic / motion vision.

## Mapping notes

- The label aggregates R1-R6 collectively. Expect an FBbt match to the outer
  photoreceptor grouping ("R1-R6" / "outer photoreceptor cell"); if FBbt only has
  individual R1..R6 terms, a broad match to the outer-photoreceptor parent is
  appropriate (the cluster does not resolve individual R1..R6).
