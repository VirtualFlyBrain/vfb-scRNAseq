# T2a

*Cell-type report for cluster `T2a` (type T2a, class N -- neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Identity inferred by the
authors from transcriptional similarity to the T2 and T3 clusters; supported
by established optic-lobe anatomy (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989;
Shinomiya et al., 2019).*

## Summary

T2a is a columnar T-type neuron of the adult *Drosophila* optic lobe, closely
related to T2 and T3. Its soma lies posteriorly adjacent to the gap between the
medulla and lobula plate. The cell body fiber projects along the proximal surface
of the medulla, then bifurcates in the second optic chiasm: one branch arborises
within a medulla column, forming a bushy, fine arborization in medulla layer M9
and then ramifying through the rest of the medulla column (bifurcating in M5
with fine arbors throughout M1-M4); the other branch forms a terminal
arborization in lobula layer 3 that spans more than a single column width with
bleb-type terminal branches. In the Kurmangaliyev 2020 atlas, the T2a cluster
was distinguished from T2 and T3 on transcriptional grounds and assigned the
name T2a by analogy with the T2 series nomenclature established by Fischbach
and Dittrich (1989).

## Markers

- Cluster label is the canonical anatomical name T2a; no driver-based qualifier.
- Atlas identity assigned by transcriptional similarity to T2 and T3 clusters,
  not by an independent morphological characterisation within this study.
- The FBbt definition cites Fischbach and Dittrich (1989) and Shinomiya et al.
  (2019) as the morphological sources; neurotransmitter profile is not reported
  for this subtype separately from T2/T3.

## Location

- **Soma:** posteriorly adjacent to the gap between the medulla and lobula plate.
- **Medulla arborisations:** layer M9 (bushy, fine); layers M1-M4 (fine arbors
  throughout), bifurcation at M5 -- a subtly different laminar profile from T2
  (M9, M1, M2, M5) and T3 (M9 only on the medulla branch).
- **Lobula arborisations:** layer 3, terminal bleb-type branches spanning more
  than a single column width.
- **Routing:** cell body fiber along the proximal medulla surface, branching in
  the second optic chiasm.

## Function

- T2a is a translobula columnar neuron conveying information between the medulla
  (M1-M9) and lobula layer 3, consistent with an OFF-channel visual processing
  role similar to T2 and T3.
- Its laminar coverage of M1-M4 (broader than T2 or T3 on the medulla side)
  may indicate a distinct integrative function sampling multiple medulla layers.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt carries an exact term "T2a neuron" (FBbt:00003729) whose definition
  describes the same soma position, medulla-column arborization pattern, and
  lobula layer 3 terminal field that characterise this cluster.
- The atlas label T2a is the same name used in Fischbach and Dittrich (1989)
  and cited by FBbt; this is a direct anatomical name, not a driver alias.
- No FBbt subtypes of T2a are known; expect a confident exact match.
