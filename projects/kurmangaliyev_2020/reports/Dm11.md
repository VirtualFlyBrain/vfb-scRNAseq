# Dm11

*Cluster `Dm11` (type `Dm11`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Dm11 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

Dm11 is a distal medullary wide-field amacrine interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. Its soma lies in the dorsal region of the cell body rind of the medulla
cortex. The primary neurite extends beyond layer M6A before turning back to form
recurrent, columnar processes running from M6A through M1 and into the first
optic chiasm. Arbor shape varies between individual cells; collectively the
terminals in M6A tile the whole layer, while processes in the other layers are
thin and columnar, each associated with R7 and R8 photoreceptor axons. The
arbor covers approximately 9 columns. There are around 70 Dm11 neurons per
hemisphere. Dm11 is glutamatergic (Davis et al., 2020) and is classified as a
secondary Notch OFF hemilineage neuron born from a medulla neuroblast.

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with glutamatergic medulla interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Dm11
  (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (layers M1-M6A) and first optic chiasm.
- **Cell body:** dorsal region of the medulla cortex cell body rind.
- **Cell class:** optic lobe wide-field amacrine interneuron, multicolumnar
  (~9 columns), with recurrent columnar arborisation.

## Function

- Medulla interneuron; wide-field lateral integration with recurrent columnar
  projections in the distal and proximal medulla, associated with R7 and R8
  photoreceptor inputs. Glutamatergic neurotransmission.
