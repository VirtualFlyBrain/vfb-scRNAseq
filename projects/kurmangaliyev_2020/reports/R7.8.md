# R7.8

*Cluster `R7.8` (subtype == type `R7.8`, class R -- photoreceptor) in
Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020 (GSE156455). `R7.8` denotes the inner photoreceptors
R7 and R8 (aggregate). Anatomy established.*

## Summary

R7.8 denotes the **inner photoreceptors R7 and R8** of the compound eye -- the
two inner photoreceptors present in each ommatidium. They form the chromatic
(colour) visual pathway and project to the medulla, in contrast to the outer
photoreceptors R1-R6 which terminate in the lamina.

## Markers

- R7 expresses UV-sensitive rhodopsins Rh3 (pale ommatidia) or Rh4 (yellow
  ommatidia).
- R8 expresses blue/green-sensitive rhodopsins Rh5 (pale ommatidia) or Rh6
  (yellow ommatidia).
- Class R (photoreceptor) in the atlas.

## Location

- **Cell bodies:** retina / compound eye.
- **Rhabdomeres:** located in the centre of each ommatidium (inner rhabdomeres),
  in contrast to the six outer rhabdomeres of R1-R6.
- **Axon terminals:** medulla. R7 axons project to medulla layers M1, M5, and
  M6; R8 axons project to medulla layer M3. Neither R7 nor R8 makes synapses in
  the lamina.

## Function

- Wavelength-specific (chromatic) photoreception.
- R7 and R8 of the same ommatidium act in a paired, opponent manner: R7
  activates R8 via histaminergic inhibitory synapses, and they form reciprocal
  inhibitory contacts.
- Input to colour-processing medulla circuits including Dm8, Tm5, and DRA
  pathways.

## Mapping notes

- The label aggregates R7 and R8 collectively; the cluster does not resolve the
  two inner photoreceptor subtypes.
- FBbt:00006008 "inner photoreceptor cell" is the canonical grouping term for
  R7 + R8, directly analogous to FBbt:00006007 "outer photoreceptor cell" for
  R1-R6.
- Individual R7 (FBbt:00004225) and R8 (FBbt:00004227) terms exist but are more
  specific than this cluster.
