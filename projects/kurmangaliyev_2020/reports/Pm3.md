# Pm3

*Cluster `Pm3` (type `Pm3`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Pm3 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

Pm3 is a proximal medullary amacrine interneuron of the adult optic lobe. It
branches at the proximal surface of the medulla, with both processes forming a
broad terminal arbor in medulla layer M9. The arbors of different cells
intermingle significantly and arbor density is heterogeneous. Pm3 occupies a
peripheral intracolumnar position. It is GABAergic. There are approximately
35-40 Pm3 neurons per hemisphere.

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with a GABAergic proximal medulla
  interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Pm3
  (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (proximal layer M9).
- **Cell class:** optic lobe amacrine interneuron, arborising at the proximal
  surface of the medulla; peripheral intracolumnar position.

## Function

- Proximal medulla interneuron; lateral integration within layer M9 at the
  proximal surface of the medulla. GABAergic neurotransmission. Arbors of
  neighbouring cells intermingle extensively, suggesting broad lateral
  inhibitory integration.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt has a specific term "proximal medullary amacrine neuron Pm3"
  (FBbt:00110066) whose definition matches this cluster precisely: same
  proximal-surface branching, same M9 layer target, same broad intermingling
  arbor morphology, same peripheral intracolumnar position, and same GABAergic
  identity (Davis et al., 2020). Cell count estimate (35-40 per hemisphere,
  Nern et al., 2025) is consistent with scRNAseq cluster size. No subtype
  ambiguity: Pm3 is not split into further subtypes in FBbt. This is an exact
  match.
