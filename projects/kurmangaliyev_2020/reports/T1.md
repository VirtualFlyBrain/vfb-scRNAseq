# T1

*Cell-type report for cluster `T1` (type T1, class N -- neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from established
optic-lobe anatomy (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989; Meinertzhagen and O'Neil,
1991; Rivera-Alba et al., 2011; Takemura et al., 2008; Takemura et al., 2013;
Nern et al., 2025).*

## Summary

T1 is an intrinsic columnar neuron of the *Drosophila* optic lobe whose soma
lies in the cortex of the medulla. The cell body fiber branches at the medulla
surface to form a T-shaped linking fiber: one arm is a bush-like arborization
in the distal part of a single medulla column (layers M1 and M2), and the other
is a bundle of climbing fibers that extends into the equivalent column of the
lamina. T1 is confined to the lamina-medulla axis and does not project to the
lobula or lobula plate. There is one T1 cell per optic column (unicolumnar).
In the lamina its connections are exclusively postsynaptic, principally from
lamina amacrine neurons; in the medulla it is strongly synapsed by centrifugal
neuron C3 in layer M2 and by lamina monopolar neuron L2.

## Markers

- Cluster label is the canonical anatomical name T1 (Fischbach and Dittrich,
  1989); no driver-based qualifier.
- Identity assigned in the atlas by correlation with the known T1 morphological
  cell type and cluster-specific marker genes identified in supplementary
  analysis (Kurmangaliyev et al., 2020).
- Unicolumnar; among the largest 40-50 transcriptional clusters in the dataset,
  consistent with one cell per column.

## Location

- **Soma:** medulla cortex.
- **Medulla arborisations:** bush-like, in distal medulla column layers M1 and M2.
- **Lamina:** climbing fibers in the equivalent lamina column; all connections
  postsynaptic.
- **Routing:** cell body fiber branches at the medulla surface in a T-shape
  linking the medulla and lamina columns.
- Does not extend to the second optic chiasm, lobula, or lobula plate.

## Function

- T1 is an intrinsic lamina-medulla relay neuron conveying input from lamina
  amacrine cells and monopolar neuron L2 to the medulla.
- Its exclusively postsynaptic lamina connections and strong medulla input from
  C3 position it as a local feedforward element within a single optic column.
- The function is distinct from the translobula T2/T3 neurons and the
  direction-selective T4/T5 neurons.
