# C3

*Cell-type report for cluster `C3` (type C3, class N -- neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from established
optic-lobe anatomy (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989; Morante and Desplan, 2008;
Rivera-Alba et al., 2011; Takemura et al., 2013; Fei et al., 2010;
Nern et al., 2025).*

## Summary

C3 is a centrifugal columnar neuron of the adult *Drosophila* optic lobe that
carries signals from the medulla back to the lamina (centrifugal direction).
It is GABAergic and unicolumnar, with one cell per optic column. It arborizes
in medulla layers M1-2, M5, and M9-10, and makes more restricted arborizations
in the lamina; it forms both presynapses and postsynapses in the lamina. In the
medulla, C3 is strongly presynaptic to lamina monopolar neuron L2 and columnar
neuron T1, and strongly postsynaptic to L1.

## Markers

- Cluster label is the canonical anatomical name C3 (Fischbach and Dittrich,
  1989); no driver-based qualifier. "C" denotes centrifugal direction, not
  the dataset class code.
- Identity assigned in the atlas by correlation with the known C3 morphological
  cell type and cluster-specific marker genes identified in supplementary
  analysis (Kurmangaliyev et al., 2020).
- Unicolumnar; cluster size consistent with one cell per optic column.

## Location

- **Arborisations:** medulla layers M1-2, M5, and M9-10 (wide-field within the
  medulla); more restricted arborisations in the lamina.
- **Synaptic polarity in the lamina:** both presynaptic and postsynaptic
  (Morante and Desplan, 2008; Rivera-Alba et al., 2011).
- **Key medulla connectivity:** strongly presynaptic to L2 and T1 in medulla
  layer M2; strongly postsynaptic to L1 (Takemura et al., 2013).
- Does not extend to the lobula or lobula plate.

## Function

- C3 is a GABAergic centrifugal feedback neuron conveying inhibitory signals
  from the medulla to the lamina, modulating L2 and T1 activity.
- Its wide-field medulla arborisations (M1-2, M5, M9-10) and lamina output
  position it as a centrifugal integrator spanning multiple medulla processing
  layers.
- Classified as an optic lobe centrifugal columnar neuron and optic lobe
  wide-field columnar neuron in FBbt, distinguishing it from narrow-field
  transmedullary and intrinsic columnar types.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt carries an exact term "centrifugal neuron C3" (FBbt:00003744) matching
  this cluster label and anatomy one-to-one.
- The FBbt definition cites Fischbach and Dittrich (1989) for medulla layer
  arborisations and Morante and Desplan (2008) and Rivera-Alba et al. (2011)
  for lamina synaptic polarity, Takemura et al. (2013) for medulla connectivity,
  and Fei et al. (2010) for GABAergic neurotransmitter identity; all features
  are consistent with this cluster.
- No FBbt subtypes of centrifugal neuron C3 exist; the term represents the
  single recognised C3 cell class.
