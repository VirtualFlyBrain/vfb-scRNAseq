# L5

*Cluster `L5` (subtype == type `L5`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the lamina monopolar neurons; anatomy established, dataset
context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

L5 is lamina monopolar neuron 5 — a columnar interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. It is unusual among the lamina monopolar neurons in having almost no
arborization in the lamina itself. Instead, it forms a branched arborization in
medulla layers M1 and M2 and a clump of terminals in M5. It is a cholinergic
neuron and occurs once per optic column.

## Markers

- Lamina monopolar (L-neuron) identity; profiled among the medulla-projecting
  lamina neurons in the atlas connectome figure (L1-L5, Figure S11).
- scRNAseq-based neurotransmitter evidence consistent with cholinergic
  identity (Davis et al. 2020; choline acetyltransferase / vesicular
  acetylcholine transporter expression).

## Location

- **Cell class:** lamina monopolar columnar neuron (class N).
- **Soma/dendrites:** minimal lamina arborization; **axon:** projects to medulla
  layers M1 and M2 (branched arborization) and a terminal clump in M5.

## Function

- Forms strong reciprocal synaptic connections with lamina monopolar neuron L1
  in the medulla (Takemura et al. 2013). Receives substantial input from L2 and
  C2 neurons. Has many presynapses onto medullary intrinsic neurons Mi1 and Mi4
  and transmedullary neuron Tm3a (Takemura et al. 2013). Cholinergic
  neurotransmitter. One cell per optic column.
