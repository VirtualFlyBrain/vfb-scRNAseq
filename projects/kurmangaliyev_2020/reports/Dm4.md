# Dm4

*Cluster `Dm4` (type `Dm4`, class N=neuron) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Dm4 is an established optic-lobe cell type defined by classical
morphology and confirmed by scRNAseq.*

## Summary

Dm4 is a distal medullary wide-field amacrine interneuron of the adult optic
lobe. Its soma is located in the anterior region of the cell body rind of the
medulla cortex. The main neurite extends into medulla layer M6, then bends back
to arborize at the boundary between M5 and M6, and also in M3B, with mixed
morphology arborizations. The arbor varies in shape between cells but covers
approximately 21 columns, with individual arbors tiling across the medulla.
Dm4 is positioned centrally within each column, surrounded by the terminals of
photoreceptors R7 and R8, and its cell boundaries follow column boundaries.
Approximately 40 Dm4 neurons are present per hemisphere. Dm4 is glutamatergic
(Davis et al., 2020). It is classified as a secondary Notch OFF hemilineage
neuron born from a medulla neuroblast.

## Markers

- Transcriptomic identity consistent with glutamatergic medulla interneuron.
- Cluster is named directly after the canonical morphological type Dm4
  (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989).

## Location

- **Neuropil:** medulla (layers M3B, M5/M6 boundary).
- **Cell body:** anterior cell body rind of the medulla cortex.
- **Cell class:** optic lobe wide-field amacrine interneuron, columnar-edge-case
  (arbor covers ~21 columns with tiling).

## Function

- Medulla interneuron; wide-field lateral integration across the distal medulla
  with columnar tiling. Glutamatergic neurotransmission.
