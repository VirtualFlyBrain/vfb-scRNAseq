# T2

*Cell-type report for cluster `T2` (type T2, class N — neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from established
optic-lobe anatomy (Fischbach and Dittrich, 1989; Shinomiya et al., 2019).*

## Summary

T2 is a translobula T neuron of the adult *Drosophila* optic lobe. Its soma
sits posteriorly adjacent to the gap between the medulla and the lobula plate.
The cell body fiber travels along the proximal surface of the medulla, then
bifurcates at the second optic chiasm: one branch arborises within a medulla
column (layers M9, M1, M2, M5) and the other forms a broad terminal arborisation
in lobula layer 3. The cell appears capable of both cholinergic and GABAergic
neurotransmission.

## Markers

- Cluster label is the canonical anatomical name T2; no driver-based qualifier.
- Assessed as cholinergic via Cha-GAL4.7.4 driver and anti-ChAT antibody
  co-labelling (Varija Raghu et al., 2011).
- GABAergic identity assessed by intersection of VGAT-GAL4 expression and
  anti-GABA antibody staining (Raghu et al., 2013).

## Location

- **Soma:** posteriorly adjacent to the gap between the medulla and lobula plate.
- **Medulla arborisations:** layers M9, M1, M2, and M5 within a single column;
  fine, bushy character in M9.
- **Lobula arborisations:** layer 3, terminal bleb-type branches spanning more
  than a single column width.
- **Routing:** cell body fiber projects along the proximal medulla surface before
  branching in the second optic chiasm.

## Function

- T2 is a translobula neuron conveying information between the medulla and lobula,
  consistent with its wide-field lobula layer 3 terminal field.
- Dual cholinergic/GABAergic competence is unusual and may reflect subtype
  heterogeneity or context-dependent transmitter release.
- Not direction-selective in the manner of T4/T5; likely involved in higher-order
  integration across medulla columns.
