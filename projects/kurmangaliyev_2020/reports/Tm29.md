# Tm29

*Cluster `Tm29` (subtype == type `Tm29`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). Anatomy based on established optic-lobe morphology; dataset context
from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

Tm29 is a transmedullary columnar neuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe.
Its primary input zone is around 5 columns of medulla layer M6, where it also
carries a small number of presynapses; it also has minor arborization in medulla
layers M3 through M8. It crosses the second optic chiasma and forms mixed
pre- and post-synaptic arbors in around 3 columns of lobula layer 5B. The neuron
is glutamatergic. There are approximately 170-270 of these cells per hemisphere.
Tm29 belongs to the adult transmedullary CB3851 group together with Tm39 and Tm40
(Nern et al., 2025).

## Markers

- Glutamatergic identity established by Davis et al. 2020.
- Cluster identity confirmed by correlation with reference transcriptome datasets
  and cell-type-specific marker gene expression (Kurmangaliyev et al., 2020,
  Figure S5C).
- Member of the Notch OFF hemilineage, born from a medulla neuroblast during
  the Erm/Ey temporal window (Konstantinides et al., 2022).

## Location

- **Cell class:** transmedullary (Tm) columnar neuron; class N (neuron) in the
  atlas.
- **Dendrites / primary input:** medulla layer M6 (principal zone, approximately
  5 columns); minor arborizations in M3-M8.
- **Axon terminal:** lobula layer 5B (mixed pre- and post-synaptic, approximately
  3 columns).
- **Organisation:** columnar; approximately 170-270 cells per hemisphere.

## Function

- Glutamatergic; transmits visual information from medulla M6 to lobula layer 5B
  across the second optic chiasma.
- Part of the CB3851 transmedullary neuron group defined by FlyWire connectome
  data; proposed subtypes Tm29, Tm39 and Tm40 identified at light level
  (Nern et al., 2025).
