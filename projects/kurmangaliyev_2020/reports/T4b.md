# T4b

*Cell-type report for cluster `T4b` (subtype; parent type `T4.T5`, class N -- neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Evidence from established
optic-lobe anatomy; the atlas paper local index was not available for this run.*

## Summary

T4b is one of the four subtypes (a-d) of T4 neurons, the ON-motion
direction-selective columnar neurons of the *Drosophila* optic lobe. T4 (ON) and
T5 (OFF) neurons are transcriptomically closely related -- in this atlas they form
a single combined `type` (`T4.T5`) that resolves into eight directional subtypes
(T4a-d, T5a-d). Each of the four T4 subtypes projects to one of the four layers of
the lobula plate and is tuned to one of the four cardinal directions of motion;
**T4b corresponds to lobula plate layer b (layer 2)** and encodes diagonal
back-to-front motion.

## Markers

- T4 and T5 share most transcriptomic markers; the atlas distinguishes the
  directional subtypes within the combined T4/T5 population.
- The paper validates regional T4/T5 subtypes with transgenic lines including
  `CG15537` (restricted to T4 neurons targeting the dorsal third of the medulla
  and lobula plate layers a/b) and the `23G12-LexA` (T4/T5) line.
- Directional subtype identity a-d is defined by lobula-plate layer targeting and
  direction tuning rather than a single unique transcriptomic marker.

## Location

- **Soma / cell class:** columnar neuron of the optic lobe (class N).
- **Dendrites:** medulla layer M10 (confirmed for all T4 subtypes).
- **Axon terminals:** lobula plate -- T4b terminates in **lobula plate layer b (2)**.

## Function

- T4 neurons are first-order **ON-pathway, direction-selective** motion-detecting
  neurons. The four subtypes encode the four cardinal directions of visual motion,
  one per lobula-plate layer; T4b encodes the diagonal back-to-front direction read
  out in layer 2.

## Mapping notes

- FBbt has an exact subtype term for T4b: `T4b neuron` (FBbt:00003733), defined as
  the T4 subtype with its lobula plate arbor in layer 2 responding to diagonal
  back-to-front motion. FBbt also contains finer sub-subtypes T4bi (FBbt:00052243)
  and T4bii (FBbt:00052244) distinguishing diagonal-down vs diagonal-up variants;
  the atlas cluster T4b does not resolve to that granularity.
