# Cluster Report: Dm9

## Atlas
Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020 (GSE156455) — adult Drosophila optic lobe scRNAseq atlas.

## Summary
Dm9 is a wide-field distal medullary amacrine interneuron of the adult optic lobe. It arborises in medulla layers M1 and M6A with columnar processes between these two layers; the processes in M2 and M5 are closely associated with R7 and R8 photoreceptor axons. Each Dm9 neuron covers approximately 7 columns laterally. The cluster corresponds to the well-characterised glutamatergic Dm9 cell type defined by its distinctive wide-field amacrine morphology and photoreceptor input association.

## Markers
Key markers consistent with Dm9 identity: expression of genes associated with glutamatergic transmission (vGluT); optic-lobe neuron markers (repo-negative, indicating neuronal rather than glial identity). The Dm prefix denotes distal medullary membership of this cell type family.

## Location
Adult medulla, distal layers M1 and M6A; optic lobe of the supraesophageal ganglion.

## Neurotransmitter
Glutamatergic (Davis et al., 2020).

## Morphology
Wide-field amacrine (no long axon); arbors span multiple columns (~7) in M1 and M6A; columnar processes in M2 and M5 closely apposed to R7 and R8 terminals. Approximately 110 Dm9 neurons per hemisphere.

## Cell count (cluster)
Neuron class (N); cluster label Dm9.
