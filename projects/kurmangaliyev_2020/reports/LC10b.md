# LC10b

*Cell-type report for cluster `LC10b` (subtype; parent type `LC10`, class N — neuron)
in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020, Drosophila visual system developmental atlas
(GSE156455, DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.10.006). Lobula anatomy from established
optic-lobe literature (Wu et al. 2016; Davis et al. 2020).*

## Summary

LC10b is one of several subtypes of the LC10 lobula columnar neuron class. LC10
neurons are visual projection neurons whose cell bodies lie in the dorsal region
of the lateral cell body rind; their axons run ipsilaterally along the anterior
optic tract and terminate in the anterior optic tubercle (AOTU). The LC10 class
is divided into two main groups (LC10A and LC10B) that differ in the sector of
the AOTU they innervate. LC10b is a subtype of the LC10A group; it has its main
dendritic arbors in lobula layers 4 to 6 (densest in layer 6), with many parallel
processes between those layers, and its presynaptic sites concentrated in layer 6
with some in layers 4 and 5A. It is a cholinergic neuron.

## Markers

- Cluster label "LC10b" is an anatomical identity assigned in the Kurmangaliyev
  2020 atlas; the underlying driver-line identity traces to the LC10 family.
- Neurotransmitter: cholinergic (VAChT-positive, confirmed by Davis et al. 2020).
- No single transcriptomic marker unique to LC10b is reported in the atlas paper;
  subtype resolution within LC10 relies on the combined transcriptome and
  comparison with morphologically defined types.

## Location

- **Class:** visual projection neuron; lobula columnar neuron (optic lobe).
- **Dendrites:** lobula layers 4, 5A, 5B, and 6; arbors densest in layer 6.
- **Presynaptic sites:** principally lobula layer 6, some in layers 4 and 5A.
- **Axon target:** anterior optic tubercle (AOTU), LC10A sector (medial zone
  running along most of the dorsal/ventral axis; see also LC10B sibling group
  which fans out near the AOTU).

## Function

LC10 neurons convey visual information from the lobula to the AOTU, a
pre-motor centre for flight and course control. Within the LC10A subgroup,
LC10b and LC10c neurons deliver topographically organised input to the AOTU
from different lobula layers, contributing to the relay of small-object and
wide-field motion signals important for visual flight control.
