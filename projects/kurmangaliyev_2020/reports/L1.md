# L1

*Cluster `L1` (subtype == type `L1`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the lamina monopolar neurons; anatomy established, dataset
context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

L1 is lamina monopolar neuron 1 — a columnar interneuron of the lamina and the
principal input to the ON motion-detection pathway. It is one of five lamina
monopolar cell types (L1-L5).

## Markers

- Lamina monopolar (L-neuron) identity; profiled among the medulla-projecting
  lamina neurons in the atlas connectome figure (L1-L5, Figure S11).

## Location

- **Cell class:** lamina monopolar columnar neuron (class N).
- **Soma/dendrites:** lamina; **axon:** projects to the medulla (synapsing onto
  Mi1 and other ON-pathway targets).

## Function

- Receives histaminergic input from outer photoreceptors R1-R6 in the lamina and
  relays the signal to the medulla; primary interneuron of the ON pathway.
