# Mi9

*Cluster `Mi9` (subtype == type `Mi9`, class N) in Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020
(GSE156455). One of the 13 medulla neurons profiled with connectome data
(Figure S11). Anatomy is established; dataset context from the atlas paper.*

## Summary

Mi9 is a medulla intrinsic neuron -- a narrow-field columnar cell intrinsic to
the medulla and a component of the OFF motion-detection pathway. It arborises in
both the outer (M2-M4) and inner (M9-M10) medulla layers and establishes both
pre- and post-synaptic connections in those layers. It is a major postsynaptic
target of lamina monopolar neuron L3.

## Markers

- Profiled in the atlas among 13 medulla neurons with mapped connectivity
  (Figure S11; connectome adapted from Takemura et al., 2013).
- Glutamatergic neurotransmitter identity; expressed markers consistent with
  glutamatergic medulla interneurons.

## Location

- **Cell class:** medulla intrinsic narrow-field columnar neuron (class N).
- **Arborisations:** outer medulla layers M2, M3, M4 and inner medulla layers
  M9-M10; bleb-type and fine terminal arborization morphology.

## Function

- Glutamatergic; receives major input from L3 (the OFF-pathway lamina monopolar
  neuron) and participates in the OFF motion-detection circuit as an interneuron
  within the medulla.
- Establishes pre-synaptic connections in both inner and outer medulla layers,
  consistent with a relay role in vertical signal propagation through the medulla
  column.
