# Lawf1

## Summary

Lawf1 (lamina wide-field 1) is a cholinergic interneuron of the adult Drosophila optic lobe. Its cell body resides in the medulla cortex. The main projection ascends via the first optic chiasm into the lamina, where it travels within a single column before making wide-field arborizations in the distal lamina. Additional arborizations occur on the distal surface of the medulla in a circular field spanning approximately 20 columns, with linking fibers descending into medulla layer M4 covering 30-40 columns. In the lamina it forms numerous presynaptic connections to lamina monopolar neuron L3 and to lamina intrinsic (amacrine) neurons. Lawf1 is one of two lamina wide-field neuron subtypes (Lawf1 and Lawf2) that together constitute the lamina wide-field neuron class.

## Markers

- Fer2 (transcription factor expressed by all lamina wide-field neurons)
- Cholinergic identity (Davis et al., 2020)

## Location

- Cell body: medulla cortex
- Projections: lamina (wide-field arborizations in distal lamina), medulla layer M1 (surface arborizations), medulla layer M4 (wide arborization field via linking fibers)
- Optic lobe; visual system

## Function

Columnar interneuron of the optic lobe visual processing circuit. Acts presynaptically onto L3 lamina monopolar neurons and lamina intrinsic neurons. Receives input from photoreceptors R1-R3 and R6, and from several lamina neuron types, as assessed by EM reconstruction (Rivera-Alba et al., 2011). Participates in wide-field integration across the lamina and medulla.
