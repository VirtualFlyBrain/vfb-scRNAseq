# Im10

*Type `Im10` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 249; P15 168 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15.*

## Summary
Immature neurons at P15 forming a 'tail' of differentiating cells attached to the cluster(s) for T4/T5: probably immature T4/T5 neurons.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given for this type. The paper reports that P15 'tails' of differentiating cells expressed genes known to be transiently expressed by newborn neurons, including Hey, nerfin-1 and zelda (vfl) (Extended Data Fig. 9c); this is stated for tails in general, not for this cluster specifically.

## Location
Optic lobe neurons born recently from GMCs; because neurons of one type are produced days apart, the optic lobe contains neurons at many stages of differentiation (paper). On UMAP most P15 neuronal clusters converge towards the GMC and NB clusters, so tails are read as intermediate states between progenitors and mature neurons.

## Function
No functional claim; the cluster represents a transient developmental state of neurons that will mature into the type(s) named above. In the paper, such tails are not present at later stages, and nerfin-1 marks the younger P15 cells in the Tm3 example.

## Label provenance
Label and comment come from the authors' Supplementary Table 1 (cluster 249, name `Im10`). The label is the authors' final identity; no disagreement between sources was found. Supplementary Table 1 comment: "P15 unsupervised cluster 100 Form a 'tail' attached to T4/T5 clusters on tSNE and UMAP vizualizations: probably immature T4/T5 neurons." The authors' confidence is expressed as 'probably'; the identity rests on UMAP/tSNE adjacency (and, for Im12, on cell composition), not on marker genes.

## Mapping notes
Authors' call: probably immature T4/T5 neurons. All Im clusters were deliberately separated from the mature neuron clusters and given an 'immature' designation because tails of very young neurons 'might often be mixtures of different cell types'. Granularity: immature state of a single named type, with the authors' 'probably' hedge. Developmental state: immature (newborn, differentiating) neuron at P15 only; the tails are absent at later stages.
