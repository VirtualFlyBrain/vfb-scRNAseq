# NB

*Type `NB` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 256; P15 386 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15.*

## Summary
Optic lobe neuroblasts (neural stem cells) at P15; the authors also note the cells are probably dying.

## Markers
Supplementary Table 1: expresses dpn, ase, mira --> neuroblasts (citing Wallace et al., 2000). Expresses grim --> the cells are probably dying (Extended Data Fig. 9, green circles).

## Location
Optic lobe progenitor pool. The paper describes the inner proliferation centre (IPC) and medial outer proliferation centre (OPC) as progressively converted into neuroblasts that divide asymmetrically, each division self-renewing and producing a ganglion mother cell (GMC) that divides once to give two neurons or glia (paper, citing ref. 31).

## Function
Neural stem cells that divide asymmetrically, self-renewing and producing GMCs (paper). grim expression suggests this late-pupal population is being eliminated.

## Label provenance
Label and comment come from the authors' Supplementary Table 1 (cluster 256, name `NB`). The label is the authors' final identity; no disagreement between sources was found. Supplementary Table 1 comment: "P15 unsupervised cluster 119. Expresses dpn, ase, mira --> neuroblasts (Wallace et al., 2000). Expresses grim --> the cells are probably dying. (Extended data Fig. 9, green circles)."

## Mapping notes
Single type call: neuroblast of the optic lobe (progenitor cell state, P15 pupa). Not subdivided by lineage (OPC versus IPC) by the authors.
