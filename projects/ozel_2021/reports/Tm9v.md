# Tm9v

*Cluster `Tm9v` (author class 227, one cluster per stage in the supervised annotation; cells per stage: P15 241, P30 275, P40 174, P50 226, P70 338, adult 987; stages P15, P30, P40, P50, P70, adult) in Ozel et al. 2021 (GEO GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe scRNAseq, pupal time given as % pupal development at 25 C.*

## Summary
Ventral subpopulation of Tm9 neurons, defined by Wnt4 expression.

## Markers
Wnt4+ (Tm9d is Wnt10+). Few genes separate them: Wnt4, Wnt10, mamo, frizzled and a few others. Several cell adhesion molecules differ at P50.

## Location
Optic lobe: medulla to lobula (transmedullary). Wnt4-Gal4 expression overlapped only ventral Tm9s (Fig. 5a). The authors saw no obvious morphological difference by sparse labelling.

## Function
Not established; the authors suggest differential Wnt signalling partitions the receptive visual field and downstream circuitry.

## Label provenance
The authors named the subtypes Tm9v and Tm9d. Resolved as separate clusters only at P50 and earlier; at P70 and adult the division was by back-projection (Methods), which is unreliable (out-of-bag error 22-23% for Tm9). Tm9 subgroups were not previously described. Source: Supplementary Table 1, row 227. Adult annotation comment: "Backprojection from P70 (Methods)." Pupal annotation comment: "class 121 cells from P50 unsupervised cluster 10 P70: Backprojection from P50 (Methods). P15: 19% of class 227 cells belonged to an unsupervised cluster to which we assigned the identity 'cluster 242'"

## Mapping notes
Ventral subpopulation of a single classic Tm9 type; no separate classic type exists. Adult and P70 assignments are classifier back-projections of lower reliability. P15, P30 and P40 cells are developing (immature) neurons, still wiring; P50 and P70 are pharate-adult stages (P70 = late pupa) and the adult sample is 1-3-day-old adult. The authors report that neuronal transcriptomes converge on those of older cells of the same type within ~15 h of birth, so type identity is already recognisable at P15.
