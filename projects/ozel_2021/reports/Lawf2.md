# Lawf2

*Cluster `Lawf2` (author class 8, one cluster per stage in the supervised annotation; cells per stage: P15 145, P30 156, P40 111, P50 92, P70 141, adult 435; stages P15, P30, P40, P50, P70, adult) in Ozel et al. 2021 (GEO GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe scRNAseq, pupal time given as % pupal development at 25 C.*

## Summary
Lamina wide-field neuron type 2 (Lawf2), identified from a clear transcriptome match. At P15 Lawf1 and Lawf2 were separated by a dedicated neural network.

## Markers
The paper's text does not list specific marker genes for this type; the authors identified it by correlation of its adult transcriptome with isolated-population transcriptomes (Methods, Extended Data Fig. 3), and a neural-network classifier propagated the identity across stages.

## Location
Optic lobe: medulla and lamina (lamina wide-field neurons, named after Fischbach & Dittrich 1989 type nomenclature).

## Function
Visual system interneuron; the paper does not discuss function.

## Label provenance
Label assigned by the authors by transcriptome match. Source: Supplementary Table 1, row 8. Adult annotation comment: "Clear match to Lawf2 transcriptome (Extended data Fig.3)." Pupal annotation comment: "P15: cells were predicted have the identity 'cluster 8' or 'cluster 113' by a dedicated NN (see Methods)."

## Mapping notes
Single, classic type (Lawf2), one author cluster; distinct from Lawf1. P15, P30 and P40 cells are developing (immature) neurons, still wiring; P50 and P70 are pharate-adult stages (P70 = late pupa) and the adult sample is 1-3-day-old adult. The authors report that neuronal transcriptomes converge on those of older cells of the same type within ~15 h of birth, so type identity is already recognisable at P15.
