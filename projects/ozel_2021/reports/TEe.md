# TEe

*Type `TEe` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 233; P15 89 cells; P30 137 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15 and P30.*

## Summary
TEe ('early') is the third transient extrinsic (TE) neuron cluster, alongside TEv and TEd. The authors regard it as younger, differentiating TE neurons.

## Markers
Paper: bsh+/hth-. TEe was connected to both TE clusters on UMAP (Fig. 2a). Bsh-Gal4 FLEXAMP lineage memory labelled all TE neurons, which the authors read as TEe containing younger bsh+ TE neurons that lose bsh as they mature. TEv expresses Wnt4 and TEd Wnt10; TE neurons are enriched for secretory markers (Fs, dimm) (paper; whether TEe shares these is not stated in the evidence available).

## Location
Bsh+/Hth- cells labelled by R10D10-Gal4 were seen at the tips of the OPC in L3 optic lobes, suggesting TE cells are produced there; the authors propose TE neurons are produced continuously from a small number of progenitors at the OPC tips. Bsh+ TE neurons were present in decreasing numbers until P50 but not at P70.

## Function
TE neurons are transient (they die in late pupa; sickle expressed at P70), superficially innervating, and secretory; the authors compare them to mammalian Cajal-Retzius cells. TEe itself is the immature stage of this population.

## Label provenance
Supplementary Table 1, cluster 233, name `TEe`, comment (P30 unsupervised cluster 2): "Could be differentiating TEs, as it is attached to TEv/TEd clusters on tSNE and UMAP vizualizations." The main text names it TEe (early) and is more definite that it holds younger bsh+ TE neurons.

## Mapping notes
Single authors' type, a developmental stage (early/immature) of TE neurons rather than a separate adult neuron type; the Supplementary Table 1 wording is hedged ('could be differentiating TEs'). Stages P15 and P30 only; the TE population is pupal-specific and absent in adults.
