# Im06

*Type `Im06` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 245; P15 95 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15.*

## Summary
Immature neurons at P15 forming a 'tail' of differentiating cells attached to the cluster(s) for Tm29/TmY5a: probably immature Tm29/TmY5a neurons. Defined as the cells of P15 unsupervised cluster 88 that the neural-network classifier did not identify as Tm29.

## Markers
No cluster-specific marker genes are given for this type. The paper reports that P15 'tails' of differentiating cells expressed genes known to be transiently expressed by newborn neurons, including Hey, nerfin-1 and zelda (vfl) (Extended Data Fig. 9c); this is stated for tails in general, not for this cluster specifically.

## Location
Optic lobe neurons born recently from GMCs; because neurons of one type are produced days apart, the optic lobe contains neurons at many stages of differentiation (paper). On UMAP most P15 neuronal clusters converge towards the GMC and NB clusters, so tails are read as intermediate states between progenitors and mature neurons.

## Function
No functional claim; the cluster represents a transient developmental state of neurons that will mature into the type(s) named above. In the paper, such tails are not present at later stages, and nerfin-1 marks the younger P15 cells in the Tm3 example.

## Label provenance
Label and comment come from the authors' Supplementary Table 1 (cluster 245, name `Im06`). The label is the authors' final identity; no disagreement between sources was found. Supplementary Table 1 comment: "cells from P15 unsupervised cluster 88 that were not identified as Tm29 by the neural network classifier. Form a 'tail' attached to Tm29/TmY5a clusters on tSNE and partly on UMAP vizualizations: probably immature Tm29/TmY5a neurons." The authors' confidence is expressed as 'probably'; the identity rests on UMAP/tSNE adjacency (and, for Im12, on cell composition), not on marker genes.

## Mapping notes
Authors' call: probably immature Tm29/TmY5a neurons. Defined as the cells of P15 unsupervised cluster 88 that the neural-network classifier did not identify as Tm29. All Im clusters were deliberately separated from the mature neuron clusters and given an 'immature' designation because tails of very young neurons 'might often be mixtures of different cell types'. Granularity: remainder-type grouped call of immature neurons spanning several named types, not a single type. Developmental state: immature (newborn, differentiating) neuron at P15 only; the tails are absent at later stages.
