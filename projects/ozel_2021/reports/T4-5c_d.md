# T4-5c/d

*Cluster `T4-5c/d` (author class 235, one cluster per stage in the supervised annotation; cells per stage: P15 980, P30 1567, P40 1039, P50 1281, P70 1794, adult 5358; stages P15, P30, P40, P50, P70, adult) in Ozel et al. 2021 (GEO GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe scRNAseq, pupal time given as % pupal development at 25 C.*

## Summary
Combined T4/T5 subtypes c and d (T4c, T4d, T5c, T5d), directionally selective motion-detecting neurons.

## Markers
Three axes of T4/T5 division: T4 vs T5 (TfAP-2), a/b vs c/d (bi), and b/c vs a/d (grn). The paper notes bi specifies the c/d identity (ref 43). Subtype markers are not retained in adult.

## Location
T4 dendrites in the medulla and T5 in the lobula, axons to the lobula plate. The authors state the polarised dendritic arbors mirror direction selectivity (their refs 6, 42).

## Function
Each of four subtypes (a/b/c/d) processes motion in one of four cardinal directions (paper, ref 41).

## Label provenance
The eight subtypes were resolved only at P50 (a separate P50 clustering splits this class into T4c, T4d, T5c, T5d); the a/b vs c/d split was projected to other stages by subclustering. The table: "Subdivision projected from P50 to all stages." Source: Supplementary Table 1, row 235. Adult annotation comment: "Subdivision projected from P50 to all stages (main text, Methods). The OOBE between class 234 and 235 is 7% in adults." Pupal annotation comment: "Subdivision projected from P50 to all stages (main text, Methods)."

## Mapping notes
Grouped call: c/d group over four types (T4c, T4d, T5c, T5d) with common parent (T4/T5 neurons). At P50 only, the finer clusters exist in a separate clustering. P15, P30 and P40 cells are developing (immature) neurons, still wiring; P50 and P70 are pharate-adult stages (P70 = late pupa) and the adult sample is 1-3-day-old adult. The authors report that neuronal transcriptomes converge on those of older cells of the same type within ~15 h of birth, so type identity is already recognisable at P15.
