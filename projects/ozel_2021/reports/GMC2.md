# GMC2

*Type `GMC2` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 254; P15 372 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15.*

## Summary
One of three P15 clusters that the authors identify as ganglion mother cells (GMCs) of the optic lobe, the transient intermediate between a neuroblast and the two daughter neurons or glia.

## Markers
Supplementary Table 1: dpn-, ase+ --> ganglion mother cells (citing Weng et al., 2013); circled in Extended Data Fig. 9. No further marker genes are listed in the evidence available for this type.

## Location
Optic lobe progenitor pool. The paper describes the inner proliferation centre (IPC) and medial outer proliferation centre (OPC) as progressively converted into neuroblasts that divide asymmetrically, each division self-renewing and producing a ganglion mother cell (GMC) that divides once to give two neurons or glia (paper, citing ref. 31).

## Function
Not a differentiated cell type: a short-lived progenitor state. The paper notes that most P15 neuronal clusters converge on the GMC and NB clusters on UMAP, consistent with an ordered path from progenitor to neuron.

## Label provenance
Label and comment come from the authors' Supplementary Table 1 (cluster 254, name `GMC2`). The label is the authors' final identity; no disagreement between sources was found. Supplementary Table 1 comment: "P15 unsupervised cluster 117. dpn-, ase+ --> ganglion mother cells (Weng et al., 2013) (Extended data Fig. 9, cyan circles)"

## Mapping notes
Grouped authors' call shared with the other two GMC clusters (GMC1, GMC2, GMC3 are separate author clusters with the same ganglion mother cell identity; the paper says 'GMCs (3 clusters)'). Developmental stage: P15 pupa, a transient progenitor cell state. Granularity implied: ganglion mother cell of the optic lobe (not neuron-type specific).
