# GMC3

*Type `GMC3` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 255; P15 364 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15.*

## Summary
One of three P15 clusters that the authors identify as ganglion mother cells (GMCs) of the optic lobe, the transient intermediate between a neuroblast and the two daughter neurons or glia. The authors add that this cluster likely contains some immature neurons: only some of its cells expressed ase and it sat between the GMCs and immature neurons on the UMAP (Supplementary Table 1; Extended Data Figs 9 and 3a).

## Markers
Supplementary Table 1: dpn-, ase+ --> ganglion mother cells (citing Weng et al., 2013); circled in Extended Data Fig. 9. No further marker genes are listed in the evidence available for this type.

## Location
Optic lobe progenitor pool. The paper describes the inner proliferation centre (IPC) and medial outer proliferation centre (OPC) as progressively converted into neuroblasts that divide asymmetrically, each division self-renewing and producing a ganglion mother cell (GMC) that divides once to give two neurons or glia (paper, citing ref. 31).

## Function
Not a differentiated cell type: a short-lived progenitor state. The paper notes that most P15 neuronal clusters converge on the GMC and NB clusters on UMAP, consistent with an ordered path from progenitor to neuron.

## Label provenance
Label and comment come from the authors' Supplementary Table 1 (cluster 255, name `GMC3*`). The label is the authors' final identity; no disagreement between sources was found. Supplementary Table 1 comment: "P15 unsupervised cluster 118. dpn-, ase+ --> ganglion mother cells (Weng et al., 2013) Likely contains some immature neurons as only some of its cells expressed ase (Extended data Fig. 9) and the cluster was situated between GMCs and immature neurons on UMAP visualization (Extended data Fig. 3a)."

## Mapping notes
Grouped authors' call shared with the other two GMC clusters (GMC1, GMC2, GMC3 are separate author clusters with the same ganglion mother cell identity; the paper says 'GMCs (3 clusters)'). Developmental stage: P15 pupa, a transient progenitor cell state. Granularity implied: ganglion mother cell of the optic lobe (not neuron-type specific). GMC3 is flagged by the authors as possibly mixed with immature neurons.
