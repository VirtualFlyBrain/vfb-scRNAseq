# Apop

*Type `Apop` (Supplementary Table 1 cluster 252; P15 256 cells) in Özel et al. 2021 (Nature; GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe, pupal stage only; the type is found only at P15.*

## Summary
Cells with no clear identity expressing high levels of apoptotic markers; an authors' state call rather than a cell type. They likely represent neurons that die shortly after birth.

## Markers
Supplementary Table 1: high levels of apoptotic markers (grim, rpr, skl) (Extended Data Fig. 9, black circle).

## Location
No specific location is given. The authors interpret the cells as products of the asymmetric division of a GMC (citing Bertet et al., 2014; Erclik et al., 2017).

## Function
Neurons predicted to die soon after birth (authors' interpretation). The paper describes this cluster as 'undifferentiated apoptotic neurons' among the six low-NN-confidence P15 clusters.

## Label provenance
Label and comment come from the authors' Supplementary Table 1 (cluster 252, name `Apop`). The label is the authors' final identity; no disagreement between sources was found. Supplementary Table 1 comment: "P15 unsupervised cluster 115. Neurons with no clear identity expressing high levels of apoptotic markers (grim, rpr, skl) (Extended data Fig. 9, black circle). These likely represent neurons that die shortly after their birth from the asymmetric division of a GMC (Bertet et al., 2014; Erclik et al., 2017)"

## Mapping notes
Not a neuron type: an authors' state call (undifferentiated, apoptotic neurons without identifiable type), P15 pupa. It is a remainder-like group with no type-level identity, so it does not specify any particular neuron class.
