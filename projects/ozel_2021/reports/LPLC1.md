# LPLC1

*Cluster `LPLC1` (author class 148, one cluster per stage in the supervised annotation; cells per stage: P15 21, P30 38, P40 28, P50 36, P70 57, adult 116; stages P15, P30, P40, P50, P70, adult) in Ozel et al. 2021 (GEO GSE142787). Female Canton-S optic lobe scRNAseq, pupal time given as % pupal development at 25 C.*

## Summary
Lobula plate/lobula columnar neuron type LPLC1, identified by the authors from a clear transcriptome match.

## Markers
The paper's text does not list specific marker genes for this type; the authors identified it by correlation of its adult transcriptome with isolated-population transcriptomes (Methods, Extended Data Fig. 3), and a neural-network classifier propagated the identity across stages.

## Location
Optic lobe: lobula plate and lobula (LPLC = lobula plate-lobula columnar neurons).

## Function
Visual feature-detecting columnar output neuron of the optic lobe; the paper does not discuss function.

## Label provenance
Label assigned by the authors by transcriptome match. Source: Supplementary Table 1, row 148. Adult annotation comment: "Clear match to LPLC1 transcriptome (Extended data Fig.3)."

## Mapping notes
Single, classic type (LPLC1), one author cluster. P15, P30 and P40 cells are developing (immature) neurons, still wiring; P50 and P70 are pharate-adult stages (P70 = late pupa) and the adult sample is 1-3-day-old adult. The authors report that neuronal transcriptomes converge on those of older cells of the same type within ~15 h of birth, so type identity is already recognisable at P15.
